End-State Breath

Picture the end state as a breath you can finally take.

Not perfection. Not a utopia. Just a country where the rules are legible, the money trails are visible, and the outcomes match the promises more often than they don’t. A place where power can’t hide behind confusion.

In that end state, democracy isn’t a performance—it’s maintenance. People don’t have to be heroes just to be heard. Ordinary life has room to be ordinary again.

Hold that feeling for a moment. Then we go back into the house and start making it real.
