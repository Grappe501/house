# Epilogue Micro-Beats — *The House Is Ours* (v11)
**Date:** 2026-01-31

> The epilogue closes the emotional circle.
> It mirrors the Prologue without repeating it.
> The reader leaves grounded, calm, and capable.

---

## EPILOGUE — *The House Is Ours*

### Intent
- **Emotional:** Quiet strength → belonging
- **Cognitive:** Shared ownership replaces waiting
- **Function:** Final payoff of all core metaphors

### Micro-Beats
1. **Return to the house**
   - Mirror the Prologue’s opening image
   - No description overload; keep it intimate

2. **Evidence of repair**
   - Subtle cues: steadier light, quiet hum, warmth
   - No claim of perfection

3. **Ownership shift**
   - Language moves from “the system” to “our house”
   - Collective responsibility without obligation

4. **Time horizon**
   - Suggest continuity across generations
   - No urgency; patience is power

5. **Closing line**
   - Calm, invitational, open-ended
   - Leaves space for reader action

### Do-Not-Do
- No victory language
- No enemies
- No calls to donate or join

---

## Symmetry Check
- Prologue: weight unnamed → Epilogue: weight shared
- Prologue: inherited house → Epilogue: chosen ownership
- Prologue: isolation → Epilogue: belonging

---

## Final Note
The book does not end with a command.
It ends with a door left open.
