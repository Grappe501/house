# Book Physics & Geometry — Core Specification (v1)
Date: 2026-01-31

This document defines the non-negotiable physical, temporal, and mathematical constraints
of *The House We Live In*. These constraints give the book meaning before prose exists.

## Primary Constraints
- Total Pages: 100 (fixed)
- Total Words: 22,000 (fixed)
- Average Words per Page: 220
- Total Audio Runtime: 4 hours (±1 minute)
- Effective Narration Speed: 92 WPM

## Pages as Meaning
Pages are not containers for text.
Pages ARE the structure.

Each page represents one *Room* in the House.

## Page Archetypes
- Compression Pages: 90–140 words (stress, tension, disruption)
- Flow Pages: 200–260 words (narrative grounding)
- Expansion Pages: 320–380 words (growth, revelation, load-bearing beams)

## Audio Design
Silence is intentional.
Pacing is controlled by page archetype.
Narration speed varies, but the average is fixed.

## Sacred Geometry
- 100 Pages = 100 Rooms
- 4 Acts × 25 Pages
- Repeating 5-page breathing cycles within each Act
