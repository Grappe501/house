# Room-Based Narrative Architecture (v1)

The book is not chapter-driven.
It is room-driven.

## Definition of a Room
A Room is:
- One page
- One emotional container
- One psychological state

Rooms may contain:
- One paragraph
- Dialogue only
- Fragmented prose
- Dense narrative

## Movement
Turning a page = entering a new Room.
Rooms are not scrolled; they are experienced.

## Rule of Intentional Breaks
Rules exist to be broken, but only with intent.
Breaking pattern signals meaning.

## Rooms vs Chapters
Chapters are navigational.
Rooms are experiential.

Readers remember Rooms.
