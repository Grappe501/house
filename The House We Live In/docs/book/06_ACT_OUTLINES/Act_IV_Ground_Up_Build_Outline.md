# Act IV — Maintenance
_Last updated: 2026-02-01_

**Act Title:** Maintenance  
**Rooms:** 076–100  
**Act Promise:** The system survives success. The house stays standing because maintenance becomes culture.

Act IV is where the work stops being a heroic build and becomes a **stable practice**:
- routine measurement
- routine correction
- distributed stewardship
- defense against capture and drift
- generational handoff

---

## Act IV non-negotiables
1. **Maintenance is power:** the ability to keep things working is governance.
2. **No “final victory” tone:** stability is a practice, not a climax.
3. **Drift is normal:** the system must expect drift and correct it.
4. **Repair is cheap early:** show why small fixes prevent collapse.
5. **People can run it:** legibility + local stewardship are the point.
