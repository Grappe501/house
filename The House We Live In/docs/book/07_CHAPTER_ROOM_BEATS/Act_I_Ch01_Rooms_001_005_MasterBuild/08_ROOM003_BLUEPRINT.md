# Room 003 Blueprint — The Shape You’re In (Somatic Containment)

## The job (locked)
Introduce **structure as felt containment** without naming it.
(Reference: `docs/rooms/ROOM_003_EXPLANATION.md`)

## Core emotional state
**Curious containment.** Not trapped — held.

## Micro-beats
1) Invite the reader to notice edges (without naming walls)
2) Offer “shape” as a neutral concept
3) Normalize being “inside” something as ordinary
4) Let silence confirm the feeling

## Symbol use
- Margins subtly widen (felt space)
- Language: “held,” “shape,” “here”

## Word-choice discipline
- Use body-friendly words (breath, shoulders, weight) sparingly.
- Avoid clinical language (nervous system, regulation).
- Avoid political/system words.

## Rhythm recipe
- Slightly more spacious paragraphs.
- One sentence that functions like a handrail (simple and true).

## Meaning seeds (later use)
This room is the first seed for later “systems are containers” teaching.
But we do not teach it yet — we only create the sensation.

## Handoff to Room 004
Room 004 names the container as “house.”
Room 003 must make that naming feel like relief, not a twist.
