# Room 005 — MasterBuild Blueprint (10×)
**Chapter 01 / Rooms 001–005**  
**Room Function:** RESIDUE / EXIT (Carry Forward)  
**Act:** I (Discomfort → Trace)

This file defines the complete structural, symbolic, emotional, and linguistic blueprint for Room 005.
No prose is written until this blueprint is locked.

---

## A. Prime Directive

Room 005 ends the first five-room circuit by leaving the reader with **residue**, not resolution.

Target internal reader response:
> “I’m carrying that with me.”

Not:
- “I understand the house.”
- “I know what’s happening.”
- “I’m in danger.”

Room 005 closes the circuit with a **single clean image** or **single quiet question**.

---

## B. Position in the 5-Room Circuit

**Incoming state (Room 4):**
- Internal pressure
- Self-awareness without accusation
- Mild discomfort

**Outgoing state (next chapter Room 1):**
- A trace carried forward
- Continued willingness to proceed
- A subtle shift in how the reader interprets “ordinary”

Room 005 should feel like leaving a room and realizing you didn’t leave empty-handed.

---

## C. Emotional & Nervous System Profile

- Emotion: quiet aftertaste, soft unresolvedness
- Nervous system: release *without relief*
- Success signal: reader pauses after the final line

This is a “soft landing” that still changes the reader’s posture.

---

## D. The Residue Object (Choose One)

Room 005 must anchor residue in **one** of these forms:

1) **A single image** (object / light / shadow / wear pattern)
2) **A single gesture** (hand hovering, step back, breath held then released)
3) **A single sentence-question** (not a hook; a quiet uncertainty)
4) **A single ordinary detail re-seen** (same thing, different feeling)

Do not combine. One anchor only.

---

## E. Symbol Discipline

Room 005 is allowed to reuse earlier symbols (door, hallway, light, wear) but must obey:

- **No escalation:** the symbol gains weight, not threat
- **No explanation:** the reader feels meaning, but is not told meaning
- **No new mythology:** nothing “revealed”

Disallowed:
- new rules
- new entities
- new mechanics
- explicit interpretation

---

## F. Language & Diction Rules

Allowed:
- clean, plain words
- soft certainty (“it stays with you”)
- subtle sensory closure (temperature, light, sound fading)

Required technique:
- **Compression** (fewer adjectives, fewer clauses)

Banned:
- conclusion language (“therefore,” “so,” “this means”)
- cliffhanger language (“until you notice…” “and then…”)
- commands (“remember,” “don’t forget”)

The last line must not feel like a hook.
It must feel like a quiet closing of the hand.

---

## G. Rhythm & Pacing Design

- The room should **shorten** as it ends
- The final 3–5 lines should be lean and simple
- Use whitespace as silence

### Final line rule
The last line must:
- stand alone
- be emotionally neutral in diction
- carry emotional weight through placement

---

## H. Micro-Beat Spine

1) Transition toward exit (movement without urgency)
2) The house returns to “normal,” but the reader is changed
3) The residue anchor appears (image/gesture/question)
4) A quiet seal (not comfort, not dread)
5) Exit with trace carried forward

---

## I. What This Room Must NOT Do

Room 005 must NOT:
- resolve the distortion
- relieve the internal pressure fully
- introduce new mystery as a teaser
- shift tone darker than Act I ceiling
- set stakes

This is residue, not plot.

---

## J. Future Payoff Awareness

Room 005 should plant:
- the sense that ordinary spaces train us
- a subtle “I adapt without noticing” implication
- a portable image that later chapters can echo

Later Acts may reinterpret the residue as:
- normalized self-regulation in unjust systems
- a learned posture of compliance
- a seed of reclaimable agency

Do not name any of that here.

---

## K. Acceptance Gates

A Room 005 prose draft is approved only if:

1) Exactly one residue anchor exists
2) No resolution or explanation is offered
3) The ending is not a hook
4) The reader still feels safe enough to continue
5) The chapter feels complete as a circuit

---

## Editorial Warning

Room 005 commonly fails in two ways:
1) It becomes a teaser (wrong genre)
2) It becomes a conclusion (too soon)

Correct tone:
> “Nothing happened. And yet something did.”
