# Room 005 Blueprint — The First Step (Motion without Demand)

## The job (locked)
Introduce movement without requiring action.
Change occurs through **time**, not effort.
(Reference: `docs/rooms/ROOM_005_EXPLANATION.md`)

## Core emotional state
**Gentle shift.** The reader senses motion as natural.

## Micro-beats
1) Confirm stillness is allowed (no urgency)
2) Introduce the idea that things change while we stand here
3) Let the room “move” slightly (visual drift)
4) End with residue (quiet curiosity)

## Language constraints
- No literal walking directives (“go,” “follow,” “deeper”)
- No destination language
- No “you must choose” framing

Preferred verbs: “drift,” “turn,” “soften,” “notice.”

## Visual/audio
- First permissible background drift (extremely slow)
- Warmth nudges up
- Optional: subtle air-shift ambience (not melodic)

## Residue image options (choose one later)
- a barely shifting shadow at the edge of perception
- the sensation of weight changing in your hands
- a quiet line that reframes time as the moving element

## Handoff to Room 006 (next chapter begins)
Room 006 may begin to introduce the first “ordinary room” content.
Room 005 must end without raising threat, only curiosity.
