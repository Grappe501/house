# Room 004 Blueprint — The House (Naming the Metaphor)

## The job (locked)
Name the structure once, plainly, offering—not imposing—the metaphor.
(Reference: `docs/rooms/ROOM_004_EXPLANATION.md`)

## Core emotional state
**Shared reference.** The reader feels: “Okay. We can call it that.”

## The naming moment (script constraint)
The word “house” appears once (or at most twice if one is in the title UI only).
Preferred line:
“You can think of it as a house.”

## Why this wording
- “You can” preserves agency
- “think of it” frames metaphor as tool, not doctrine
- “a house” keeps it ordinary and shared

## Micro-beats
1) Echo the felt containment (without repeating “shape” too much)
2) Offer the metaphor
3) Step back (do not elaborate)
4) Let the reader sit with it in silence

## Symbol + sensory
- Warmth increases slightly
- Margins slightly more open
- No motion
This is the first “beam” moment: a stable reference that later chapters can build on.

## What we must NOT do here
- No explanation of what each room represents
- No list of systems (education, health, money) yet
- No hint of villainy

## Handoff to Room 005
Room 005 introduces motion (“change happens”) without demand.
Room 004’s job is to make the container usable before movement begins.
