# Room 003 — MasterBuild Blueprint (10×)
**Chapter 01 / Rooms 001–005**  
**Room Function:** DISTORTION (First Fracture)  
**Act:** I (Permission → Unease)

This file defines the complete structural, symbolic, emotional, and linguistic blueprint for Room 003.
No prose is written until this blueprint is locked.

---

## A. Prime Directive

Room 003 introduces the **first fracture** in the house’s normalcy without naming it, explaining it, or escalating it.

Target internal reader response:
> “That felt slightly wrong.”

Not:
- “What does this mean?”
- “Is this dangerous?”
- “Who did this?”

---

## B. Position in the 5-Room Circuit

**Incoming state (Room 2):**
- Safety
- Familiarity
- Trust in normal rules

**Outgoing state (Room 4):**
- Mild unease
- Subtle self-monitoring
- Unnamed tension

The house remains neutral. Reality becomes slightly unreliable.

---

## C. Emotional & Nervous System Profile

- Emotion: mild unease, cognitive dissonance
- Nervous system: brief sympathetic flicker, no sustained vigilance
- Success signal: reader instinctively rereads one sentence

---

## D. The Fracture Rule

Room 003 may introduce **exactly one** distortion.

Allowed categories (choose one only):
- Spatial
- Temporal
- Causal
- Procedural

**Recommended for Act I:** Procedural distortion  
Something functions, but the reason it functions feels off.

No stacking. No escalation.

---

## E. Symbol Set (Limited)

One previously introduced ordinary object may carry the distortion.

Examples (do not commit yet):
- Light switch works, but not when expected
- Sound occurs after its cause
- Door closes fully but without resistance
- Hallway feels longer returning than entering

Disallowed:
- Locks
- Surveillance
- Written rules
- Machinery
- Anything implying design or intention

---

## F. Language & Diction Rules

Allowed:
- Neutral observation
- Qualifiers (“almost,” “not quite,” “just”)
- Plain verbs

Required technique:
- Hedging without explanation

Banned:
- wrong
- broken
- trap
- system
- control
- watched
- designed

---

## G. Rhythm & Pacing

- Slightly more varied than Room 2
- One sentence should subtly interrupt rhythm
- No staccato clusters

Paragraph rule:
- One paragraph should end early, creating a pause

---

## H. Micro-Beat Spine

1. Normal continuation
2. Encounter with the fracture
3. Reality check (nothing is “wrong”)
4. Internal flicker (self-orientation)
5. Forward motion without resolution

---

## I. Prohibitions

Room 003 must NOT:
- Explain the distortion
- Introduce stakes
- Suggest danger
- Invite suspicion of the house

---

## J. Future Payoff Awareness

The distortion must later be interpretable as:
- normalized control (Act II)
- something that “has always been this way”
- something reclaimable in Act IV

Do not write this yet.

---

## K. Acceptance Gates

A Room 003 prose draft is approved only if:

1. Exactly one distortion exists
2. Unease, not fear
3. No explanation
4. Clean handoff to Room 4
5. No Act II leakage

---

## Editorial Warning

If it feels clever, it’s wrong.
If it feels eerie, it’s too much.
If it feels explainable, it’s premature.

Correct tone:
> “That was strange. Anyway…”
