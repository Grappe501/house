# Acceptance Gates — Chapter 01 (Rooms 001–005)

A room draft is approved only if it passes:

## Gate A — Act I Safety
- Reader feels safe to stay.
- No menace cues.
- No “you can’t leave” framing.

## Gate B — Single Job
Per Room Theory: one emotional state per room.
If the room argues, explains, or multitasks, revise.

## Gate C — Metaphor Discipline
- Renovation language only (no destruction fantasies).
- House is neutral.
- Metaphor is felt, not explained.

## Gate D — Pacing Integrity
- The room can be read slowly without impatience.
- White space is used intentionally.
- Audio pauses feel supportive, not theatrical.

## Gate E — Foreshadowing Legality
- Only motifs, no thesis.
- No explicit “system/machine” talk before Act II.

## Gate F — Handoff Readiness (later mechanics)
- The room plants stable nouns we can later drill down on.
- It does NOT preview solutions.
