# Room 001 — Audit Pass (Canonical Reference)

This audit evaluates **Room 001 — prose pass v1** against the project’s locked standards:
- **5-Room Chapter Arc (Room 1 = Entry / Consent)**
- **Act I Tone Contract (warm, human, observational)**
- **Act containment** (no Act II leakage)
- **Future viability** (space for escalation into Acts II–IV)

This is not a beauty critique. It is a **fitness and control audit**.

---

## 1) Macro Function Check

### Room Function: ENTRY / CONSENT  
**Verdict:** ✅ PASS

The room performs one primary job: it **invites the reader to arrive** without pressure, threat, or agenda.

It avoids the classic early-failure modes:
- implying a trap too soon
- signaling destiny
- recruiting the reader into a thesis
- making the reader feel watched or tested

---

## 2) Psychological Contract Audit

### Core question:
**Does the reader feel they chose to be here — and can still leave?**

**Strong consent anchors:**
- “You can stop here.”
- “Nothing happens if you do.”
- “There’s no consequence for turning away.”

These lines actively reduce threat and establish reader agency early, which is required for Act I.

**Verdict:** ✅ Contract honored

---

## 3) Rhythm & Nervous System Audit

**Sentence-length profile:**
- short declaratives for grounding (“The door is unlocked.”)
- gentle expansions for softness
- generous whitespace for breath

This produces low-vigilance pacing (parasympathetic), appropriate for Act I entry.

**Verdict:** ✅ Rhythm correct

---

## 4) Symbol Introduction Audit

**Symbols introduced and their Act I safety status:**

| Symbol | Status | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| House | ✅ Allowed | neutral container, non-ideological |
| Door | ✅ Allowed | entry without test |
| Unlocked | ⚠️ Watch | powerful; must remain non-agentive |
| Familiarity | ✅ Allowed | pre-memory, non-destiny |

### The “Unlocked” line (highest risk)
“The door is unlocked.” is the most potent line in the room.

It is handled correctly because it removes intention:
- “Not because someone is waiting.”
- “Not because you were expected.”
- “because no one felt the need…”

This prevents premature trap framing and preserves future escalation space.

**Verdict:** ✅ Approved

---

## 5) Word Choice Discipline

**Strong control choices:**
- “announce” removes spectacle
- “ordinary” de-exceptionalizes without flattening
- “keeps to itself” keeps agency low
- “not recognition exactly, not memory” blocks premature interpretation

This is essential for Act I: the reader learns how to feel without being told what it means.

**Verdict:** ✅ Lexical discipline strong
