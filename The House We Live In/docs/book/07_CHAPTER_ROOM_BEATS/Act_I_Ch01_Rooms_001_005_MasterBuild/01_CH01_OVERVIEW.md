# Chapter 01 Overview — Rooms 001–005

## Chapter purpose
This first 5-room cycle builds the **psychological contract** of the entire book:
- the reader is safe here
- the pace is intentional
- meaning is felt before it is named
- the “House” is a neutral structure (built, not evil)

This chapter is the *front porch* of the whole experience.

## 5-page breathing cycle mapping (Act I pattern)
Per `docs/book/02_EMOTIONAL_SCORE.md`, the cycle repeats:
1) Compression  
2) Flow  
3) Flow  
4) Expansion (Beam)  
5) Compression or Silence

**Chapter 01 mapping (recommended):**
- **Room 001 (Compression):** consent + tempo (tight, minimal claims)
- **Room 002 (Flow):** presence + permission (steady forward movement)
- **Room 003 (Flow):** containment felt somatically (movement without stakes)
- **Room 004 (Expansion/Beam):** name “house” once (shared reference frame)
- **Room 005 (Silence/Compression):** first motion without demand (integration through quiet drift)

## Emotional curve (micro)
**Start:** guarded attention → **Middle:** settled attention → **End:** gentle curiosity

No dread. No menace. No “trap” language.  
Unease is permitted only as *small incongruities*, not threats.

## Narrative voice
Act I voice is:
- warm, human, non-condescending
- precise without being clever
- humble in claims (“you can think of it as…”)

Avoid:
- theatrical eeriness
- authority postures (“listen,” “you must”)
- any promise of a coming reveal

## What we introduce (and what we do NOT)
### Introduced in Chapter 01
- **Tempo as ethics:** slowness as consent
- **Attention as participation:** nothing to “win”
- **House as shared container:** a usable metaphor with no villain

### Not introduced yet
- politics, parties, enemies
- system critique
- the later “working man’s democracy” mechanics
- “how it works” explanations

We only lay **the felt conditions** that make later mechanics readable.

## Foreshadowing rules (allowed only as texture)
- early foreshadowing must read like ordinary observation
- no explicit “system” language
- no direct mention of future reforms

Foreshadowing is done via recurring motifs (see Symbol Map).
