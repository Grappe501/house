# Guidance for Filling Act I Room Functions

Rules:
- Every Room has ONE primary job.
- Silence Rooms may have < 100 words.
- No solutions, no blame, no policy.
- Each Room must progress recognition slightly.

Room cadence:
- Rooms 1–5: Entry and trust
- Rooms 6–15: Weight and shared experience
- Rooms 16–20: Subtle structural awareness
- Rooms 21–25: Breath, safety, curiosity

Reader must feel calmer at the end of Room 25 than at Room 1.
