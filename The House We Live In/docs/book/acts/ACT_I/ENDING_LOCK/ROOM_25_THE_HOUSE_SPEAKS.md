# Room 25 — The House Speaks (Act I terminus — immutable)

## Function (non-negotiable)
Reframe the house as a system that requires the reader.

## Emotional design
- Entry: alert
- Exit: clear, implicated  
No fear. No anger. Only clarity.

## Symbol set
- Shift language from space → function
- House speaks neutrally
- No apology, no menace

**Meaning:** the system does not hate you; it needs you

## Language constraints
**Allowed:** the house works; it always has; you can stay; most people do  
**Forbidden:** if you don’t; or else; any threat framing

## Audio / silence
Silence preferred. If audio exists, it ends abruptly.

## Door logic
No clear exit presented. No next step explained.

## Final line (locked)
> “You can stay.  
> Most people do.  
> The house needs you.”

## Exit condition
Reader knows:
1) this is a system
2) they participate in it
3) participation has been framed as freedom

## Failure mode (critical)
Do not escalate. Do not explain. Do not soften.
