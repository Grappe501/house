# Psychological Resonance Model (v1)

This book encodes psychological states rather than teaching typologies.

## Stress vs Growth Encoding
Stress states are encoded through:
- Short sentences
- Fragmentation
- Absolutes
- Constrained word counts

Growth states are encoded through:
- Longer sentences
- Imagery
- Choice language
- Expanded word counts

## Enneagram as Hidden Engine
- Readers are never told about Enneagram
- All humans cycle through stress/growth
- Each Act includes multiple psychological lenses

## Goal
Readers recognize themselves without being labeled.
Identity emerges organically.
