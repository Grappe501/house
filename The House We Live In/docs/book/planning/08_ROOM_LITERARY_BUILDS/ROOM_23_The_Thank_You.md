# Room 23 — The Thank You

## Function (non-negotiable)
Reveal how compliance is emotionally rewarded.

## Emotional design
- Entry: neutral
- Exit: validated, faintly hollow  
This is the most dangerous room emotionally (because it feels good).

## Symbol set
- Praise without specifics
- Gratitude without outcome
- Appreciation detached from power

**Meaning:** belonging as currency

## Language constraints
**Allowed:** people like you; it matters; you’re needed; it wouldn’t work without you  
**Forbidden:** explaining what is being supported; any measurable result

## Audio / silence
Warm tone if audio exists. No climax. No resolution.

## Door logic
Door appears as a courtesy. Opens gently.

## Exit condition
Reader feels: “I matter here.” and faintly: “But to what?”

## Failure mode
Do not overplay warmth. Do not tip into sentimentality. Do not expose the mechanism.
