# LEVEL 3 — ACT III MECHANICS REGISTRY

This registry defines the mechanisms introduced or matured in Act III.
These mechanics operationalize reconstruction and transition.

---

## R1 — Structural Locking
**Source Rooms:** 054, 064  
**Description:** Rules and institutions designed to resist quiet reset.  
**Enforcement:** Local authority, transparency, auto-review triggers.  
**Measurement:** Policy persistence index.

---

## R2 — Local Feedback Governance
**Source Rooms:** 056, 057, 058, 059  
**Description:** Decisions corrected quickly through proximity and feedback loops.  
**Enforcement:** Local metrics dashboards, response deadlines.  
**Measurement:** Decision latency reduction.

---

## R3 — Public Option Baselines
**Source Rooms:** 062, 063  
**Description:** Non-extractive baseline services disciplining markets.  
**Enforcement:** Access guarantees, pricing floors.  
**Measurement:** Market price compression above baseline.

---

## R4 — Anti-Predation Market Rules
**Source Rooms:** 061, 063  
**Description:** Competition without urgency monetization or hidden fees.  
**Enforcement:** Functional fee definitions, audits.  
**Measurement:** Predation index.

---

## R5 — Transition Sequencing
**Source Rooms:** 066–070  
**Description:** Phased change to avoid shocks and collapse.  
**Enforcement:** Timelines, dependency mapping.  
**Measurement:** Transition stability score.

---

## R6 — Quality of Life Index
**Source Rooms:** 071–075  
**Description:** Composite measure of time, cost, stability, and fear reduction.  
**Enforcement:** Public reporting and benchmarking.  
**Measurement:** QOL score trend.
