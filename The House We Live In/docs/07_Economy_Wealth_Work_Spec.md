# Economy, Wealth, and Work (Capitalism for the 90%)

## Anchor promise
Improve quality of life for:
- individuals ≤ $250k/year
- businesses ≤ $20M/year revenue

## Economic thesis
Markets can remain — but wealth must stay tied to:
- work
- value creation
- community prosperity

The system fails when wealth becomes:
- political leverage
- monopoly control
- debt leverage over labor
- abstraction divorced from contribution

## Key mechanisms (high level)
- Anti-monopoly enforcement designed for modern corporate structures
- Bust concentration without collapsing markets
- “Finance class transition”: make it **better** for investors long-term by stabilizing demand and reducing systemic risk
- Worker prosperity fund/dividend concepts (structured to avoid “socialism” framing)
- Strong social network: free education, free healthcare, universal broadband

## Debt transitions (placeholders to expand)
- Student debt retirement tied to public benefit
- Medical debt retirement as part of universal care shift
- Mortgage transition strategies to expand ownership without collapse

## Incentives for service
- defense/service incentives tied to skills, education, housing access, and community building

## GDP and shared prosperity
- design for exponential productivity gains while circulating wealth locally
- local reinvestment incentives; community equity; co-ownership models

## Placeholders
- Tax structure options (including IRS simplification alternatives)
- Revenue sources for the People’s system
- National debt paydown strategy and time horizons
