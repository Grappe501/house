# Monetization & Integrity (Funding Without Capture)

## Objective
Generate enough revenue for the author to:
- reduce outside work
- build full-time
- fund infrastructure and organizers

While preserving:
- trust
- mission
- independence

## Product ladder (freemium)
### Free
- the core philosophy
- the “Start Here” WHY
- model explanations at a high level
- limited scenario tooling

### Paid (subscription)
- deep dives
- advanced models and sliders
- “how to organize” training paths
- audio library for every page
- moderated community

### Premium
- facilitator toolkits
- local cohort guides
- training-of-trainers
- org memberships (higher tier)

## Pricing options (placeholders)
- $5–$15/month individual
- $99/year
- sliding scale scholarships
- organizational tier (custom)

## Integrity constraints
- no ads
- no donor direction
- no selling user data
- privacy-first and user-owned data

## Marketing principle
The book spreads the idea.  
The website sells the **experience**: depth, tools, training, and community.
