# Roadmap Phases (Book-First to Movement)

## Phase 0 — Foundation (done)
- Website scaffold
- Scenario engine + QoL timeline
- Pillar engine + scorecards
- Snapshot exports

## Phase 1 — Narrative lock
- Vision & guardrails finalized
- Book outline + chapters
- Draft opening + action ending
- Voice calibration

## Phase 2 — Book drafting
- write full manuscript
- internal review for neutrality and clarity
- conversion to print + ebook + audio

## Phase 3 — Site weaving
- link every book concept to site pages
- hover definitions and deep links
- chapter landing pages
- build the “Library” (ideologies) templates

## Phase 4 — Monetization + community
- subscription tiers
- training paths
- moderated comments/community spaces
- organizer cohorts

## Phase 5 — Data and credibility
- wire Census/BLS/BEA data where safe
- expand QoL/pillar models
- publish snapshots and public methodology pages

## Phase 6 — Movement tooling
- local group toolkits
- facilitation scripts
- accountability dashboards
- scaling infrastructure

## Rule
If the book isn’t compelling, the site won’t matter.
