# Content Style Guide (Neutral, High-Trust Voice)

## Tone
- calm, firm, human
- plain language
- respectful of different backgrounds
- avoids shame and tribe language

## Forbidden patterns (book)
- “left vs right”
- demonization (evil, enemies, traitors)
- conspiracy framing
- culture-war bait

## Preferred framing
- systems and incentives
- outcomes over intentions
- stability and dignity
- opportunity and fairness
- local control + national competence

## Reader posture
Assume the reader is:
- busy
- smart
- tired
- skeptical
- open if respected

## Definition rule
Define every concept the first time it appears:
- short definition in text (book)
- hover definition on website
- deep dive link optional

## Persuasion rule
Lead with:
1) “Here’s what you’re experiencing”
2) “Here’s why systems create it”
3) “Here’s how we could build differently”

## Writing templates (website ideologies)
Each ideology/system page uses:
- What problem it tried to solve
- What incentives it created
- How it worked when it worked
- How power concentrated / failed
- Lessons relevant to WMR

## Faith & civil rights
- Respect belief and community
- Enforce separation through institutional boundaries
- Frame civil rights as freedom from coercion, not culture conflict
