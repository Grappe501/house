# Governance System Spec (Decentralized, Worker-First Democracy)

## Goal
Decentralize domestic power, strengthen democracy, reduce money capture, and make government accountable and legible.

## Core design
- **Local-first** domestic authority (by geography)
- National government: world-stage representation + national standards only where truly “for all”
- Systems over personalities; term limits; transparency by default
- Reduced bureaucracy; sunset rules for agencies/programs
- Escalating authority: neighborhood → city/county → state → national

## Representative democracy fixes (high level)
- End electoral college effects via practical reforms (path depends on legal strategy)
- Replace winner-take-all incentives with more representative systems (e.g., ranked methods)
- De-emphasize parties via open access, ballot design, and funding constraints
- Strong anti-corruption and anti-lobby capture rules

## Justice design
- No private prisons
- Crime reduction > incarceration
- Community-centered policing (local governance)
- Federal force rules: no domestic occupation; local leaders retain control; federal role is support/accountability

## Civic rights pillars
- Data ownership and privacy as a central civil right
- Anti-discrimination protections for withholding personal data
- Radical transparency in government: budgets, contracts, audits, outcomes

## Transition constraint
Pragmatic shift without constitutional convention.
Paths include:
- federal statutes
- state compacts and laws
- constitutional amendments via normal process
- agency restructuring via legislation

## Placeholders (to be expanded)
- Election mechanics and party-neutral ballot access
- Local governance “constable-style” or community accountability models
- Federal vs state program ownership and standards
