# Organizing Action Playbook (End-of-Book → Real Life)

## Goal
The reader finishes and immediately knows:
- what to do next
- how to do it
- how to invite others without conflict

## Principles
- relational organizing (trust networks)
- listen first; speak second
- low barrier to entry
- consistent, repeatable micro-actions

## End-of-book actions (draft set)
1) Share one idea with one person (not a debate)
2) Host a small conversation (2–6 people)
3) Learn one civic tool (local meeting, ballot process, public records)
4) Share the link and invite exploration
5) Join an organizer training path (paid tier optional)

## Training ladder (website)
- Level 1: Conversation skills + listening loops
- Level 2: Local civic process literacy
- Level 3: Team formation and role structure
- Level 4: Campaign and initiative mechanics
- Level 5: Train-the-trainer

## Community accountability
- no hate
- no conspiracies
- truth + respect
- action beats outrage
