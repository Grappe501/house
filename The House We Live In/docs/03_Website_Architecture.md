# Website Architecture (IA + Book Mapping Rules)

## What the website is
A **living appendix + lab** that can expand into thousands of hours of learning:
- deep dives (education library)
- interactive models (scenario/pillar/QoL)
- training systems (organizing)
- eventually: recorded audio for every page in the author’s voice

## IA principles
- Keep the top level minimal (non-overwhelming)
- Push complexity downward into drilldowns
- “No cross-navigation for now” is OK; later we weave the network

## Required top-level zones
1) **Start Here** (the WHY)
2) **The Model** (HOW it works)
3) **The Future** (WHAT it looks like by decade)
4) **Impact Lab** (sliders, scenarios, QoL)
5) **Organize** (action + tools)
6) **Library** (systems + ideologies; optional advanced sections)

## The “education, not identity” library
- political philosophies and historical systems
- structured templates (see `05_Content_Style_Guide.md`)
- tie every ideology to: goals, incentives, failures, capture mechanisms

## The “How the world actually works” section (advanced)
- not in main nav
- opt-in depth
- evidence-first; avoid conspiratorial tone
- explains capital flows, debt, reserve currency dynamics, corporate leverage

## Mapping rules: book ↔ website
- Every book chapter has a canonical landing page
- Every concept has:
  - definition (hover)
  - short explainer
  - deep dive page
  - model tie-in (if applicable)
- Every website deep dive references:
  - the relevant chapter(s)
  - the modeling assumptions

## Content lifecycle tags
Use tags in page headers or frontmatter:
- `core` (free)
- `deep` (free or paid)
- `tool` (paid)
- `training` (paid)
- `advanced_global` (opt-in)
- `sensitive` (careful framing)
