# Room 001 — The Door (Prose v1)

The house doesn’t announce itself.

It doesn’t sit at the end of anything important. There’s no sign, no marker, no sense that you were meant to arrive here. If you weren’t already nearby, you would never think to look for it.

From the outside, it looks ordinary in the quiet way ordinary things often do. It keeps to itself. It doesn’t ask questions. It doesn’t try to be understood.

The door is unlocked.

Not because someone is waiting. Not because you were expected. It’s unlocked the way many things are—because no one felt the need to secure it.

You can stop here.

Nothing happens if you do. The house won’t follow you. There’s no consequence for turning away. Most people would.

But standing this close, there’s a small, almost unnoticeable feeling that settles in—not recognition exactly, not memory, just the sense that this place isn’t entirely unfamiliar.

Like a thought you’ve brushed against before.

You don’t have to decide anything yet.

You’re only at the door.
