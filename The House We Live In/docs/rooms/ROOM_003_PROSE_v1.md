# Room 003 — The Hall

The hallway continues on from the entryway without much change.

The walls are plain. The floor stays level. Your steps sound the way you expect them to. There’s nothing about the space that suggests it should feel different from the one behind you.

Partway down, you reach for the light switch.

It turns on.

Not immediately, but close enough that you almost don’t notice the pause between the movement of your hand and the change in the room.

The light settles into place. Everything looks as it should.

You stand there for a moment, long enough to be sure nothing is wrong.

Nothing is.

Still, you find yourself waiting a second longer than necessary before moving on.
