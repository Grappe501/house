# Character Bible (Archetypes, Micro‑characters, Voice Rules)

## Core ensemble (recurring)
These carry structure without preaching.

### The Builder
- **Role:** sees load paths; speaks plain; understands “why it holds”
- **Function:** translates system mechanics into physical truths (beams, joints, stress)
- **Avoid:** technocratic tone; keep human and tactile

### The Neighbor
- **Role:** moral center; does not argue; shows up
- **Function:** belonging; the porch; the proof that “people still do this”
- **Avoid:** saintliness; make them busy, tired, real

### The Clerk
- **Role:** gatekeeper by design, not by intent
- **Function:** shows how systems turn humans into gates
- **Avoid:** villain framing; show their constraints

### The Broker
- **Role:** smooth transactional “helper”
- **Function:** embodies monetized complexity (fees, fine print, extraction)
- **Avoid:** mustache-twirling; make them charming

### The Organizer
- **Role:** weaver of people; builds repeatable method
- **Function:** bridges book → Level 2 transition plan → Level 3 tools
- **Avoid:** savior narrative; they are a facilitator, not a hero

## Rotating “working-life mirrors” (micro-characters)
Use as brief scene-lamps; do not overbuild.
- Nurse / EMT — burnout, time theft, moral injury
- Teacher — paper maze, blame shifting
- Small business owner — fees, capture, distortion
- Gig worker — instability, administrative friction
- Caretaker — invisible labor and the weight

## The Shadow presence (force, not person)
### The Hand on the Thermostat
- **Meaning:** capture as a systemic force
- **Rule:** show effects before explaining

### The Reset
- **Meaning:** progress undone quietly
- **Rule:** never melodrama; make it clinical and inevitable until Act III

## Voice rules (consistency)
- Primary voice: intimate, clear, cinematic detail
- Structural voice (“Beam mode”): minimal, clean, memorable lines
- Humor: dry, brief, human (never snark)
- Anger: allowed, but transmuted into clarity and invitation

## Character continuity ledger
For recurring characters, track:
- What they believe now
- What they fear
- How they changed since last appearance
- What symbol they carry (ladder, clipboard, keys, tape measure, porch light)
