# Vision and Contract (Non‑Negotiables)

## What the book *is*
A literary, room-driven work that functions as:
- a **psychological rescue**
- a **systems awakening**
- a **pragmatic invitation** into the New Future Government layer
- a **transparent proof layer** that earns trust instead of demanding it

It is not a policy whitepaper with metaphors. It is a story that *happens to be structurally true*.

## The reader promise
The reader will leave with:
1) **Relief** (“I’m not crazy.”)  
2) **Clarity** (“This has a shape.”)  
3) **Agency** (“We can rebuild it.”)  
4) **A next door to walk through** (Level 2 model + Level 3 proof)

## Aesthetic rules (genius-level discipline)
### 1) Show, don’t announce
- Never preach the thesis.
- Put the thesis inside objects: receipts, waiting rooms, a porch light, a cracked beam.

### 2) A room is an emotional container
Each room page must have:
- a dominant **emotion**
- a dominant **image**
- a dominant **motion** (what changes by the end)

### 3) Modes (rotate for pacing)
- **Compression:** breathless, fragmented, close to the body  
- **Flow:** lyrical narrative, scene-based  
- **Beam:** clean structural clarity, “load-bearing insight”  
- **Silence:** short page that allows meaning to land

Rule: **Every Beam must be earned** by lived detail (Compression/Flow) before it.

### 4) No cartoon villains
The antagonist is structure: incentives, capture, friction, narrative camouflage.

### 5) The book is a staircase
- Act I lowers shame.
- Act II builds a map.
- Act III offers a buildable future.
- Act IV returns belonging and agency.

## The 3-deep system promise (site-wide)
- Level A: **What it is** (rooms + explainers)
- Level B: **Impact** (trajectory shifts + metrics)
- Level C: **Mechanics/Proof** (assumptions + models + sliders)

The book must make the reader *want* to go deeper, not feel forced.

## Craft targets
- Emotional coherence > cleverness
- Symbols evolve; they do not repeat
- Every act ends with a door, not a conclusion
