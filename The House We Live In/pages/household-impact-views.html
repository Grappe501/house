What the numbers mean

  • Estimated household value is a proxy for increased stability: lower net outflows, fewer crises, and higher purchasing power.
  • Hours gained is a proxy for reduced churn and better time security as AI productivity is shared.
  • Stress reduction is a proxy for health and mental wellbeing improvements under stability-focused policy.
