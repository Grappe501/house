THE PSYCHOLOGICAL CONTRACT

If you proceed…

The House will be polite. It will offer clarity. It will reward cooperation. And it will never admit what it takes.

This book does not ask you to be heroic. It asks you to be honest—about what systems do to people, and what people do to survive them.

  • Move at your own pace.
  • Notice what feels “reasonable.”
  • Track what gets smaller inside you.

HOW TO READ
