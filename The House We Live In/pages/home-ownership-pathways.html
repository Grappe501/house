WHY

Ownership builds local wealth

Home ownership is one of the few wealth-building paths available to working families. We expand it while preventing a new bubble.

HOW

Ownership pathways (menu)

  • Down payment support tied to residency and income thresholds.
  • Shared equity models that lower monthly costs while preserving long-run ownership.
  • Community land trusts to keep land affordable and prevent speculation.
  • First-home priority in local markets before institutional buyers.
WHAT

What it changes

  • More families able to build equity
  • More stability for kids and schools
  • More local entrepreneurship (home equity enables business risk)
