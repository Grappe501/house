Impact Lab

Overview

Universal utilities and modern emergency services reduce household costs, increase opportunity, and prevent disasters from turning into long-term poverty. Broadband access especially accelerates rural revival and national productivity.

Key outcome: fewer catastrophic failures + higher regional mobility + stronger local economies.
Metrics & projections

What we measure

  • Broadband coverage, price, and speed by region; adoption rates and equity gaps.
  • Water quality compliance and remediation timelines; shutoff rates and affordability burden.
  • Fire/EMS response times and coverage; volunteer department stability indicators.
  • Disaster losses and recovery times; resilience hub utilization metrics.
  • Local economic indicators tied to connectivity: remote work, small business formation, telehealth access.
