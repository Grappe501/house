Why universal rules matter

A floor of dignity

Tenant protection should not depend on zip code. A universal baseline prevents the race-to-the-bottom where communities compete by weakening protections.

Baseline rights

What every lease must include

  • Plain-language terms and standardized disclosures.
  • Habitability and repair timelines with penalties for neglect.
  • Fee transparency and limits on junk fees.
  • Mediation and due process before eviction.
Enforcement

How it becomes real

  • Local enforcement offices with published response timelines.
  • Public dashboards for complaints and resolutions.
  • Anti-retaliation enforcement with serious penalties.
