Impact Lab

Overview

The system limits extreme wealth capture and breaks monopoly choke points while preserving entrepreneurship. Growth increases by unlocking productivity (education/healthcare), competition, and local recirculation of wealth.

Template: current system → new system change → mechanism → risks/safeguards → metrics → projections (10/25 years).
Core frame

What changes for the billionaire class

  • They keep the ability to build and invest.
  • They lose the ability to convert wealth into political dominance and permanent market control.
  • Extreme accumulation is limited through anti-capture taxation and competition enforcement.
Mechanisms

How we break monopoly power without crashing the economy

  • Orderly breakups with continuity planning (no chaos).
  • Decentralize capital access: small business credit backstops and regional finance.
  • Proxy governance transparency and accountability (future build).
  • Public dividend layer via People’s Wealth Fund to share upside without nationalization.
