What this is

True Costs Ledger (auditable + horizon-aware)

This ledger is designed to stay symmetrical and auditable: same categories, same horizons, both scenarios. v0.2 adds horizon-aware paths and cumulative totals with an explicit break-even year calculation.

Rules: (1) publish assumptions, (2) publish confidence bands, (3) allow criticism in the open, (4) update transparently.

How this stays unbiased

Guardrails built into the model

  • Same categories in both scenarios (no moving the goalposts).
  • Growth rates and ramp costs are explicit and editable.
  • Low/base/high bands show uncertainty.
  • Break-even is calculated mechanically from the same ledger.
