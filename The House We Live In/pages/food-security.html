Food as infrastructure

Universal food baseline + availability standards

  • Dignified food baseline for households, with gradual tapering.
  • National food availability standard: no community left as a food desert by default.
  • Universal free school meals and strengthened community food infrastructure (hubs, cold storage, distribution).
  • Anti-gouging enforcement during disruptions and support for local producers.
