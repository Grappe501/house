What is a food hub?

The missing middle of the food economy

A food hub is local infrastructure that makes local and regional food viable at scale: aggregation, cold storage, basic processing, packaging, and distribution.

  • Farmers get a stable route-to-market.
  • Communities get reliable supply and lower price volatility.
  • Schools and hospitals can source locally without chaos.
How it works

A practical operating model

  • Ownership: public, cooperative, or hybrid—must be locally governed with transparency.
  • Contracts: stable purchase agreements with local producers.
  • Distribution: scheduled deliveries to retailers, schools, clinics, resilience hubs.
  • Workforce: good local jobs in logistics and processing.
Anti-extraction rules

Keep it local

  • Caps on absentee profit extraction where public money is involved.
  • Open-book procurement and pricing for public contracts.
  • Preference for small and midsize producers.
