Impact Lab

Overview

The transition to free education and stable homeownership requires eliminating stranded student debt and converting mortgages away from extractive finance—without triggering market collapse.

Template: current system → new system change → mechanism → risks/safeguards → metrics → projections (10/25 years).
Student debt

Abolish legacy student debt

  • Cancel federal student loans as a transition justice measure once education is free going forward.
  • Provide a conversion pathway for private student loans (buyout at fair value or standardized income-based conversion).
Mortgages

Mortgage conversion (swap, don’t smash)

  • Create a public refinance option (Homeownership Trust) with low fixed rates and standardized protections.
  • Swap private mortgage assets into stable instruments to avoid bank runs and panic.
  • Targeted principal reduction for underwater/predatory cases, phased by region and risk.
