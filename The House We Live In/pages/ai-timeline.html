WHY

We need a timeline to stay sane

People argue about AI as if it’s a single event. It’s a curve. The curve matters.

We use 1/5/10/20/50-year horizons to forecast pressure points and policy needs.
Scenario model

Capability trajectory (non-technical)

  • 1 year: widespread copilots, automation in office workflows, creative tools, early robotics pilots.
  • 5 years: heavy automation in customer service, logistics planning, coding support; robotics begins scaling.
  • 10 years: major displacement in routine knowledge work; autonomous systems common in industry.
  • 20 years: near-total automation of many task categories; “human-required” domains shrink or transform.
  • 50 years: potential “super-AI” regimes; governance determines whether humans remain sovereign.

This is a scenario scaffold. We will refine with external references and uncertainty bands.

Pressure points

What breaks first in the status quo

  • Wage collapse in automatable sectors
  • Wealth concentration from ownership of compute/data
  • Market capture (few firms set prices)
  • Surveillance and manipulation at scale
WMR response

What stabilizes society

  • Productivity dividend into People’s Wealth Fund
  • Free education + paid transitions
  • Anti-monopoly enforcement for compute/data and platform markets
  • Strong privacy and data ownership rights
