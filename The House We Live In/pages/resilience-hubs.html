Resilience hubs

A practical community safety net

Resilience hubs are places people already trust—schools, libraries, community centers—equipped to provide support during disasters: cooling/warming, charging, communication, supplies, and basic care coordination.

What they include

Minimum hub capabilities

  • Backup power and communications (including satellite or radio where needed).
  • Water and basic supplies storage.
  • Community volunteer coordination and information systems.
  • Partnership with local fire/EMS and health services.
