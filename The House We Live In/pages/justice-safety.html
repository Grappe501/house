Golden Circle

WHY: safety is stability

Most “crime policy” treats harm after it happens. This system treats safety as a product of stability: housing, healthcare, education, fair wages, and community trust.

Core goal: decrease crime and decrease imprisonment — permanently.
Golden Circle

HOW: local control + transparent systems

  • Local-first public safety: communities define priorities and oversight.
  • No private prisons. No perverse profit incentives in punishment.
  • Prevention pipelines: mental health, addiction treatment, youth programs, job pathways.
  • Restorative options: when appropriate, repair harm instead of multiplying it.
  • Visibility: publish use-of-force, complaints, discipline outcomes, and spending.
