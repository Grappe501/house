Why this matters

Urban success requires moving people, not just cars

Congestion is not solved by adding lanes forever. Efficient cities move people using high-frequency networks, reliable corridors, and safe active transportation—while still allowing vehicles for those who need them.

The urban/suburban package

What metros build

  • Frequent routes: a core grid with 10–15 minute service.
  • BRT corridors: dedicated lanes, signal priority, off-board payment where applicable.
  • Regional rail: all-day service that functions like transit.
  • First/last mile: microtransit zones and safe bike/walk networks feeding trunk lines.
Energy and efficiency

Trade-offs explained

  • High-capacity modes (rail/BRT) are most energy-efficient at high occupancy.
  • Microtransit is valuable as a feeder and in low-density zones; it becomes costly if it replaces high-capacity routes.
  • Electrification reduces local pollution and can reduce total emissions as the grid cleans up.
