Local Food Systems & Resilience

Home / The Model / Food & Agriculture / Local Systems
WHY

Resilience means you can feed yourself

Global supply chains are efficient — until they aren’t. Regional resilience prevents price shocks and shortages.

HOW

Local production and processing capacity

  • Regional production targets based on population and nutrition needs.
  • Invest in small/mid-scale processing (meat, dairy, grains, produce).
  • Cold storage and logistics hubs accessible to small producers.
  • Public procurement that prioritizes local supply for schools and institutions.
WHAT

Rural-to-urban value capture

  • More rural jobs in processing and logistics
  • Shorter supply chains and more price stability
  • Higher quality and traceability
