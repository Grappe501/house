Dignity + mobility

End poverty as a permanent caste

  • Stability Floor: healthcare, education, housing stability, food baseline, and shock-cash support.
  • Auto-enrollment and gradual tapers (no benefit cliffs).
  • Mobility ladder: paid training, apprenticeships, childcare, transportation support.
  • Living wage enforcement with underpayment penalties funding the Workers’ Fund.
