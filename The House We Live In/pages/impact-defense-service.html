Impact Lab

Overview

This defense model maintains deterrence while shifting spending toward readiness, resilience, and auditability. Service incentives broaden opportunity and strengthen domestic capacity through a Civil Service Corps.

Key outcome: stronger security with less waste and fewer open-ended wars.
Metrics & projections

What we measure

  • Readiness: training hours, maintenance backlog, logistics performance.
  • Procurement: cost overruns, schedule delays, vendor concentration.
  • Resilience: infrastructure hardening progress; disaster recovery time; cyber incident outcomes.
  • Service incentives: recruitment/retention, veteran outcomes (housing, education, health).
  • Global posture: conflict duration, alliance cooperation indices, diplomatic stability.
