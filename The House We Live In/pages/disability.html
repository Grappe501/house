Philosophy

Dignity is a design requirement

Disability policy often fails by treating people as suspects rather than citizens. This model replaces humiliation with support, and replaces “prove you’re suffering” with transparent eligibility and services.

The goal is not to force work. The goal is to maximize agency: the ability to live safely, participate, and choose work when possible.

Rights floor

What is guaranteed

  • Healthcare, mobility supports, and assistive technology as rights.
  • Stable income floor without punitive “cliffs.”
  • Accessible housing and transportation standards.
  • Caregiving support and legal protections against exploitation.
Work by choice

Participation without coercion

  • Work is optional where health requires; supported where possible.
  • Partial participation pathways with gradual benefit tapering (no cliff punishments).
  • Employer standards for accommodations and non-discrimination enforcement.
Principle: society benefits when people can contribute without being punished for needing support.
Administration

How we stop the bureaucracy from becoming cruelty

  • Plain-language eligibility and stable review windows.
  • Independent appeals, rapid timelines, and automatic continuation during review.
  • Fraud control via targeted audits—not universal suspicion.
Integration

How this connects to retirement and the poverty floor

  • Caregiving and disability time counts toward retirement credits.
  • Housing and food stability systems reduce secondary harms.
  • Public banking and fair credit reduce exploitation vulnerability.
