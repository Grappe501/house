Mental Health Infrastructure

Home / The Model / Health & Wellbeing / Mental Health
WHY

Mental health is public safety and economic stability

When mental health is untreated, costs reappear as:

  • ER overuse
  • family crisis
  • addiction and overdose
  • workforce instability
  • incarceration
Rule: crisis response is healthcare, not jail.
HOW

The mental health infrastructure stack

  • Integrated care: mental health services embedded in primary care.
  • Crisis response teams: non-carceral mobile teams with escalation protocols.
  • Addiction treatment: evidence-based treatment, not punishment-first.
  • Workplace mental health: predictable schedules, paid leave, and burnout prevention.
WHAT

Universal access and workforce buildout

  • Grow the provider workforce with subsidized education and service incentives.
  • Telehealth parity + rural hubs + mobile units.
  • Community-based peer support networks.
QoLI linkage

Metrics we will wire

  • Overdose/ER overuse proxies
  • Crisis call outcomes (treatment vs arrest)
  • Work loss days and stress burden proxies
