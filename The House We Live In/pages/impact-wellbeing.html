Impact Lab

Overview

Reducing chronic stress improves health, learning, and economic output. The effect is compounding: stable housing + fair finance + accessible care + time stability reduces emergency utilization and increases productivity.

Key outcome: less crisis, more capacity.
Current system

What we see today

  • High burnout, anxiety, addiction, and untreated conditions.
  • Cost barriers and provider shortages create long wait times.
  • Emergency care substitutes for preventive care.
  • Workplace unpredictability and debt traps amplify stress.
New system

What changes

  • Universal healthcare + mental health access; crisis response separated from criminalization.
  • Predictable work/time rules; transportation efficiency reduces commute stress.
  • Stability policies reduce financial panic and housing churn.
  • Community wellbeing hubs increase access and belonging.
Metrics & projections

What we measure

  • Mental health access and wait times; crisis calls diverted to care.
  • Overdose rates, relapse rates, and treatment continuity.
  • Preventive care utilization; chronic disease markers over time.
  • Workplace injury and fatigue indicators; absenteeism and productivity signals.
  • Emergency room use for preventable conditions.
