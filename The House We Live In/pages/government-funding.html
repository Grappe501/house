Golden Circle

WHY: government funding must be simple, fair, and non-punitive

Working families experience government as a maze. Under WMR, funding is designed to be transparent, predictable, and built around the principle: tax extraction more than work.

Goal: fund education, healthcare, infrastructure, and justice without allowing wealth to buy power.

HOW

Where money comes from (and why this is still capitalism)

  • Money is generated by private production, services, entrepreneurship, and innovation.
  • The public role is to set rules so the gains are not captured by monopolies.
  • Capitalism remains: businesses compete and profit — but cannot extract unlimited rent from essentials.
  • Public infrastructure lowers costs for everyone, including business, raising GDP productivity.

Pragmatism

How this phases in without breaking the system

  • Phase 1: simplify collection, close obvious loopholes, publish transparent dashboards.
  • Phase 2: shift burden from wages to extraction (monopoly rents, speculation, vacancy, externalities).
  • Phase 3: expand People’s Wealth Fund mechanisms as automation rises.
  • Rule: every new revenue mechanism must include a household impact test and published assumptions.
