Audit Log & Patch Notes (v1)

Institutional memory, made legible: what changed, why, and what we learned.

Minimum log fields

  • date, owner, scope
  • what_changed (diff summary)
  • why (metric or incident)
  • expected_effect
  • verification_plan

Patch note format (human)

vX.Y — We changed ___ because ___. We expect ___. We will verify by ___.
