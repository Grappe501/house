Free Education (Pre-K to Career)

Home / The Model / Education & Skills / Free Education
WHY

Free education pays for itself

Education increases productivity, reduces crime drivers, improves health outcomes, and grows GDP. When it’s funded publicly, the return is captured by society instead of being taxed as debt.

HOW

Universal public pathway (excellent by design)

  • Early childhood: Pre-K access for every family.
  • K–12: strong baseline standards + local enrichment + teacher respect.
  • Community college and trade: free credentialing aligned to regional needs.
  • University: free public option for qualified students; service incentives for high-need fields.
  • Adult transitions: rapid retraining modules with childcare and transport support.
WHAT

Delivery model (decentralized, accountable)

  • Local school governance remains local, but funding equalizes opportunity.
  • National government sets rights and transparency standards, not ideology.
  • Open curriculum resources and audit-ready spending visibility.
