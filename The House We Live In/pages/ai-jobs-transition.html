WHY

Automation without a dividend is displacement

If AI increases output but families lose income, the system becomes unstable. WMR treats automation as a public dividend event.

HOW

Transition infrastructure

  • Paid retraining: income replacement while training.
  • Apprenticeship expansion: earn-while-you-learn pathways.
  • Reduced hours: as productivity rises, the standard workweek shrinks over decades.
  • Local job pipelines: tie training to regional development (energy, housing, care, infrastructure).
WHAT

What happens to job loss

  • People are not “thrown away” — they shift lanes with support.
  • Job loss becomes a managed transition, not a personal catastrophe.
  • AI-driven output raises the dividend, which stabilizes demand and small business revenue.
