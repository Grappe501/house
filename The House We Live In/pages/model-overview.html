System design

The model in one view

  • Democracy design: popular vote presidency; House elected with multi-member districts + ranked-choice voting; voting rights baseline; anti-gerrymander architecture; party de-emphasis incentives.
  • Anti-capture rules: public elections, transparency, dark-money restrictions, revolving door limits, procurement accountability.
  • Economic citizenship: free education (pre-K through career), healthcare free at point of service, right to organize and bargain, portable benefits, stability floor.
  • Capitalism with guardrails: bust monopolies, limit extreme wealth capture, expand worker ownership pathways, People’s Wealth Fund dividend for citizens.
  • Justice & safety: community safety governance, reduce poverty penalties, rehabilitation and reentry guarantees, focus on violent crime clearance and prevention.
  • Digital civil rights: personal data ownership, freedom from data coercion, collective negotiation of data terms, surveillance limits, algorithmic due process.
