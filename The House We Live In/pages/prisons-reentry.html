Prisons, Sentencing & Reentry

Home / The Model / Justice & Safety / Prisons & Reentry
WHY

Cages are the most expensive failure

Prisons are a last resort — not a business model. The aim is fewer crimes, fewer cages, and safer communities.

Non-negotiable: no private prisons.
HOW

Sentencing and incarceration redesign

  • Front-end prevention reduces first-time offending.
  • Earned release tied to education, treatment, and accountability milestones.
  • Proportional sentencing with periodic review.
  • Reentry first: housing, job placement, and health continuity begin inside.
WHAT

Reentry infrastructure

  • Guaranteed ID, job pipeline, and transitional housing support.
  • Sealed records for eligible offenses after stability milestones.
  • Family reunification support and trauma-informed services.
  • Community accountability plans with measurable goals.
Cost logic

Hidden costs become visible

Status quo spends through chaos: emergency care, child instability, unemployment, repeat incarceration.

WMR spends on stability: treatment, education, housing, work — lowering total system cost.
