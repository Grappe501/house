Price Transparency & Supply Chain Accountability

Home / The Model / Food & Agriculture / Price Transparency
WHY

Price manipulation is hidden taxation

When pricing is opaque, families pay more and producers earn less. Transparency restores real market signals.

HOW

Supply chain accountability (scaffold)

  • Required disclosure of price spreads (farm gate → processor → distributor → retail).
  • Audit authority for suspicious pricing spikes.
  • Anti-gouging triggers during emergencies.
  • Open ownership visibility (who owns what parts of the chain).
WHAT

Tools for citizens and businesses

  • Public dashboards showing regional food prices
  • Procurement tools to prioritize local supply
  • Consumer transparency labels for origin and processing
