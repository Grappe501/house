Utilities: Fire & Water Reliability

Home / The Model / Utilities
Principle

Fire and water are civilization infrastructure

Fire protection and clean water are not optional services. When these systems fail, working people pay first—in illness, insurance costs, property loss, and community collapse.

Goal: universal reliability, transparent governance, and affordability.
Water utilities

Clean water, full accountability

  • Universal water safety standards with public reporting.
  • Infrastructure renewal: replace aging lines and treatment systems with priority on high-risk areas.
  • Polluter accountability: strict penalties and cleanup responsibility.
  • Affordability floor: water burden caps and assistance where needed.
Fire protection

Regional strength, local control

  • Universal coverage standard: response time and coverage benchmarks.
  • Regional compacts: rural departments supported through shared equipment, training, and mutual aid.
  • Wildfire and disaster readiness: linked to Environment & Resilience pillar.
  • Insurance alignment: public investment lowers risk and insurance costs over time.
Incentivize public systems

Make the public option the default

  • Reliability dashboards: publish outages, boil notices, response times, and maintenance backlogs.
  • Affordability protections: prevent price shocks and predatory fee structures.
  • Local governance: elected/representative boards with anti-corruption rules.
  • Performance-based funding: federal support tied to outcomes, not politics.

People use public systems when they are reliable, transparent, and fairly priced.
