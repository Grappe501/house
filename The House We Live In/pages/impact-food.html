Overview

Food injustice is solved by guaranteeing both purchasing power and availability: universal baseline benefits, availability standards, school meals, and local distribution infrastructure that markets can’t opt out of.

Template: current system → new system change → mechanism → risks/safeguards → metrics → projections (10/25 years).
Guarantee

Food baseline + availability standard

  • Dignified household food baseline with gradual tapering.
  • National availability standard to eliminate food deserts by default.
  • Universal free school meals and community food hubs (storage, distribution).
