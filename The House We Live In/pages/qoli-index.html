Quality of Life Index (QoLI)

This is the capstone measurement that ties every pillar together into one transparent, auditable picture: what life feels like for a working family over time.

Always shown side-by-side: Status Quo Trajectory vs Working Majority Republic.

Golden Circle

WHY • HOW • WHAT

  • WHY: Make prosperity measurable for the 90%, not the 10%.
  • HOW: Composite index built from domain scores (health, housing, affordability, time, safety, agency, etc.).
  • WHAT: A public scoreboard at 1/5/10/20/50 years, with assumptions, ranges, and costs.

Methodology, weights, and assumptions are public and adjustable. No black box.
