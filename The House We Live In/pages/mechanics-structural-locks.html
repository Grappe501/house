Level 3 — Structural Lock Mechanisms

Act III Beam Room 054. Mechanism-first durability. (v1 scaffold)

What a “structural lock” is

A structural lock is a design choice that makes quiet reset difficult: it forces drift to become visible, expensive, or reversible by the public—not only by insiders.

Lock types (v1)

  • Visibility locks: public dashboards, mandatory disclosures, audit trails
  • Process locks: supermajority requirements to reverse, multi-step reversals with delays
  • Local locks: affected communities get correction authority
  • Trigger locks: automatic review when indicators worsen (QOL components)

Measurements

  • Policy persistence index: time-to-reversal, erosion rate
  • Drift visibility score: how quickly the public can detect change
