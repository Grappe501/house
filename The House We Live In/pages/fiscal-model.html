WHY: fiscal stability protects the working majority

Debt and deficits are not just accounting — they determine whether families face inflation, austerity, or instability. WMR treats fiscal policy as a public safety system designed to protect households while funding universal floors.

Golden Circle

HOW: the strategy (unbiased)

  • Reduce recurring burden (bend essential cost curves).
  • Improve revenue quality (shift from wages to extraction and speculation).
  • Grow the real economy (infrastructure + competition + shared AI productivity).
  • Pay down debt with explicit rules and transparent reporting.

How we keep this honest

  • We publish assumptions and allow public critique.
  • We keep categories symmetric across scenarios.
  • We show low/base/high bands and update when new evidence arrives.
  • We separate mechanisms (what changes) from values (what we want).
