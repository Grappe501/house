WHY

Private education exists — but can’t sabotage the public option

Families will make choices. The goal is not to ban private education, but to ensure it cannot drain the public system or become an escape hatch for elites while everyone else is left behind.

HOW

Rules that incentivize public buy-in

  • Public schools are funded to be excellent everywhere — making private unnecessary for most.
  • Transparency requirements for any institution receiving public funds.
  • Anti-discrimination enforcement and accessibility standards.
  • Limit political indoctrination where public funding is involved (focus on civic competence).
WHAT

What we incentivize

  • Families choosing public systems because quality is high.
  • Private options that innovate without extracting public capacity.
  • Shared civic curriculum baseline for democratic coherence.
