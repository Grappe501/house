Methods

How the fiscal and ledger math works (v0.2)

  • True Costs Ledger: start from baseline annual costs by category, apply scenario growth rates, add explicit WMR investment ramps.
  • Cumulative totals: sum annual totals over the chosen horizon.
  • Break-even year: first year where cumulative WMR ≤ cumulative Status Quo.
  • Debt dynamics scaffold: debt evolves via primary balance + interest; GDP grows via real+inflation; WMR assumes modest growth uplift from infrastructure.

v0.3 will replace category baselines and growth rates with sourced data series and publish sensitivity analysis.

What we still need to make it real

Data ingestion checklist

  • Authoritative baseline: revenues/outlays breakdown (CBO / Treasury)
  • Debt held by public vs intragovernmental (Treasury)
  • Category baselines: NHEA for healthcare, NCES for education, EIA for energy, DOT for transport, DOJ/BJS for justice
  • Evidence-based growth-rate bounds per category
  • Implementation costs for WMR pillars (range-based)
  • Publish a “Model Change Log” page and version every update
