Community Energy & Local Ownership

Home / The Model / Energy / Community Energy
Principle

Local ownership prevents extraction

When energy projects are owned locally (or revenue-shared locally), communities benefit and opposition decreases. This is how we build projects without sacrificing local dignity.

Models

Ways to do it

  • Community energy cooperatives.
  • Municipal utilities and public power expansions.
  • Revenue-sharing agreements with strict transparency.
  • Tribal and rural community ownership tools.
Fairness rules

No land exploitation

  • Clear contracts, no hidden easements.
  • Fair compensation for land and community impacts.
  • Local hiring and training requirements.
