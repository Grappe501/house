WHY: surpluses must be allocated by rule, not politics

When government runs a surplus, the real fight begins: who gets it? WMR prevents personality-driven spending by using transparent, published allocation rules. This makes debt paydown credible while still investing in the working majority.

Principle: stability first, then debt paydown, then build lasting prosperity.

WHAT

What each bucket means

  • Debt paydown: reduces interest burden and future vulnerability.
  • People’s Wealth Fund: shares gains broadly and stabilizes demand as AI automation rises.
  • Reinvestment: productivity infrastructure (transport/utilities), resilience, and capacity.

These are scaffolds; shares can be adjusted, but only through published democratic rule changes.

Guardrails

When we slow paydown on purpose

  • If unemployment rises sharply or household costs spike, allocation shifts away from paydown.
  • If essential systems are underbuilt (utilities/transport), reinvestment share rises.
  • If debt/GDP meets target, People’s Wealth Fund share increases.
