What this is

AI multiplier in the QoLI model

AI is treated as a cross-cutting multiplier: it can raise quality of life fast if gains are shared, or collapse stability if job loss and capture dominate.

  • Status quo: automation pressure reduces household stability; benefits concentrate.
  • WMR: productivity dividend + retraining + anti-monopoly rules convert AI into shared prosperity.

How it works

Policy → protection → shared gains

  • Jobs transition: paid retraining + apprenticeships + shorter workweek over time
  • Dividend: productivity gains flow to the People’s Wealth Fund
  • Anti-monopoly: break compute/data/platform capture
  • Data rights: people own personal information; no discrimination for withholding
