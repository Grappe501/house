WHY

Prevention creates exponential returns

Prevention is the only healthcare policy that compounds. It reduces suffering and lowers long-run cost.

Key idea: invest upstream so downstream spending shrinks.
HOW

Prevention stack

  • Food and nutrition access
  • Clean air and water enforcement
  • Vaccination and screening
  • Maternal health support
  • Physical activity infrastructure (parks, safe streets, transport)
WHAT

Long-run outcomes

  • Lower chronic disease load
  • Higher productivity and learning outcomes
  • Lower disability and family crisis burden
