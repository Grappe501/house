How AI changes the whole system

AI changes everything — but the direction depends on governance.

  • Status quo: job loss + capture → lower QoLI for most, higher super-wealth for few.
  • WMR: shared productivity + transitions → higher QoLI and more local prosperity.
QoLI linkage

Where AI maps into metrics

  • Income & Work: dividend offsets displacement; shorter workweek increases wellbeing.
  • Affordability: cost compression must be passed through (anti-monopoly).
  • Agency: transparency and audit requirements prevent manipulation.
  • Time & Stress: stability infrastructure reduces crisis churn during transition.
