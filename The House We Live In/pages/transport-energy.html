The reality

Transportation is an energy system

Every mobility choice has an energy cost. The future system works by:

  • moving more people per vehicle where density supports it
  • electrifying the fleets that run the most miles
  • using demand-responsive service only where it outperforms fixed routes
A practical hierarchy

Efficiency-first routing

  1. Walking/Biking for short trips where safe infrastructure exists.
  2. Frequent bus/BRT for corridors with steady demand.
  3. Rail for the highest-demand spines and intercity corridors.
  4. Microtransit to feed hubs and serve low-density areas.
  5. Cars remain essential in some regions, but the system reduces forced dependence.
Why electrification matters

Cost stability and resilience

  • Electric fleets can lower operating cost volatility.
  • Charging at hubs integrates with resilience planning (backup power).
  • Electrification works best when paired with reliable high-occupancy routes.
