Water as critical infrastructure

You can’t “market solve” contamination

Water systems are natural monopolies. They must be run with accountability, transparency, and long-term maintenance discipline.

What changes

The new rules

  • Public dashboards: testing results, violations, and remediation timelines.
  • Lead line replacement and aging pipe renewal prioritized.
  • Shutoff protections for hardship; humane payment plans.
  • Polluter-pays enforcement where upstream contamination occurs.
