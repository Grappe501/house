Golden Circle

Why → How → What

WHY: working families should have time, stability, and dignity—not a permanent fight for survival.

HOW: shift incentives toward broad prosperity using enforceable rules: public goods (education/health), anti-monopoly, worker dividends, transparency, and local power.

WHAT: a set of phased policies that re-balance the economy, strengthen democracy, and reduce coercive stress.

V55 focus: timeline views (1 / 5 / 10 / 20 / 50 years) and “assumptions cards” on every projection.

Method

Assumptions & transparency

Every projection has three layers:

  1. Observed baselines (ACS/BLS) when available
  2. Transform rules (explicit mapping from data → proxy)
  3. Scenario rules (how WMR reforms change trajectories)

Nothing is hidden. If we guess, we label it. If we don’t know yet, we leave it as a placeholder and track it.
