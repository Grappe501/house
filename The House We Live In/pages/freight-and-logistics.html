WHY

Freight efficiency is hidden prosperity

Every household pays for freight inefficiency through higher prices. Modern logistics reduce inflation pressure and improve GDP productivity.

HOW

Freight modernization (scaffold)

  • Rail corridors for freight where it beats trucks on cost/energy
  • Port modernization and routing efficiency
  • Last-mile upgrades that reduce congestion and emissions
  • Anti-monopoly enforcement against logistics capture
WHAT

Outcomes (scaffold)

  • Lower consumer prices (affordability domain)
  • Higher business productivity
  • Reduced fuel dependency volatility
