What CLTs solve

Affordability without endless subsidies

Community Land Trusts keep homes affordable permanently by preventing speculative land inflation. They are a decentralization tool: local communities keep land rooted.

How CLTs work

Split land from structure

  • Trust owns land; resident owns the home.
  • Resale formula allows equity gains but keeps price affordable for the next family.
  • Local governance ensures community rules and transparency.
Scaling CLTs

From pilot to statewide system

  • Public banking credit to acquire land strategically.
  • Right-of-first-refusal on bulk sales to prevent investor sweeps.
  • Integrate with food hubs and mobility hubs for holistic community revitalization.
