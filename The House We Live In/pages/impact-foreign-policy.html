Impact Lab

Overview

A working-majority foreign policy increases domestic prosperity by aligning trade and security with public interests. Internationally, it improves credibility by reducing profit-driven conflicts and increasing predictability.

Key outcome: stronger alliances + stronger domestic base + fewer costly wars.
Metrics & projections

What we measure

  • Trade balance composition: productive exports vs financial extraction.
  • Domestic capacity: strategic supply chain resilience indices.
  • Alliance credibility indicators: treaty compliance, shared exercises, coordination speed.
  • Conflict metrics: duration, cost, and veteran impact over time.
  • Corruption metrics: money laundering enforcement and foreign influence transparency actions.
  • Global standing: trust and partnership indicators, refugee crisis prevention outcomes.
