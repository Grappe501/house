Philosophy

Energy is a public necessity: reliability, affordability, and transition over time

Energy policy must prioritize reliability and price stability for households and industry. We keep private innovation, but treat the grid and core access as a regulated utility backbone.

Model

Utility backbone + competitive generation

  • Universal service energy guarantee: no household loses basic power/heat due to temporary hardship.
  • Grid modernization: transmission buildout, resilience hardening, cybersecurity, and disaster recovery.
  • Price stability rules: transparent rates, limits on predatory spikes, reinvestment mandates.
  • Transition over time: reliability-first decarbonization with workforce protections.
Implementation

How we move the system without shocks

  • Phase schedules tied to readiness metrics (grid capacity, storage, reliability).
  • Domestic manufacturing strategy for grid and generation components.
  • Regional flexibility: different mixes can meet national outcomes if reliability and affordability targets are met.
