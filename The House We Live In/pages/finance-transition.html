Philosophy

Transform finance by making stability more profitable than extraction

This system does not try to “destroy Wall Street.” It collapses the political power of the finance class by removing the incentives that make extraction profitable and by offering a better, safer long-horizon investment environment.

  • Finance becomes boring again: fewer crashes, fewer bailouts, fewer hidden risks.
  • Investors still win: they earn steady returns through stability assets and productive investment.
  • Workers win: household balance sheets improve; local economies grow; wages rise with productivity.
Mechanics

The Stability Asset and the Swap Rule

Major transitions follow one rule: swap, don’t smash. When we remove extraction (mortgages, predatory fees, speculative leverage), we convert legacy cashflows into predictable instruments.

  • National Prosperity Bond: a safe, long-duration asset backed by reliable revenue streams and productivity growth.
  • Mortgage conversion expansion: legacy high-interest mortgages are refinanced into stable low-cost instruments; holders receive stable substitutes.
  • Capital standards: leverage and systemic risk rules make speculative instability less profitable.
Generational leveling: over time, returns shift away from rent-seeking and toward productive investment. The economy becomes less fragile and more investable.
Outcome

What changes over 10–25 years

  • Lower systemic crash frequency and severity.
  • Higher productive investment share (infrastructure, housing supply, manufacturing, energy, broadband).
  • Stronger household balance sheets and higher local spending velocity.
  • Reduced ability for concentrated capital to purchase political outcomes.
