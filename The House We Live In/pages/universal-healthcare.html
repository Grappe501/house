WHY

Medical debt is a tax on being human

Medical debt doesn’t just harm individuals — it destabilizes families, employment, and local economies. If healthcare is guaranteed, we must retire legacy medical debt as a system correction.

Non-negotiable: healthcare is always free at point of use.
HOW

Funding and incentives (not socialism — shared infrastructure)

  • Replace premiums and deductible chaos with a transparent contribution structure.
  • Separate funding from employment to improve labor mobility and bargaining power.
  • Payment reforms: reward outcomes and prevention, not volume.
  • Fraud controls: public audit trails + strict penalties for billing abuse.
WHAT

Retiring medical debt (pragmatic transition)

  • Create a Medical Debt Retirement Trust that purchases debt at market-discount and extinguishes it.
  • Fund via a small slice of system savings + contributions + enforcement against predatory billing.
  • Ban new medical debt accumulation for covered care.

This is a scaffold. We will add detailed transition math and safeguards.
