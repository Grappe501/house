How health reforms cascade

Health reform changes:

  • Household costs (premiums, deductibles, medical debt)
  • System costs (public/private shifts)
  • Hidden costs (bankruptcy, ER overuse, incarceration spillover)
  • Time/stress and productivity
QoLI linkage

Policy → metric → QoLI

  • Universal healthcare reduces household healthcare burden → higher Affordability and Health.
  • Mental health crisis response reduces arrests → higher Safety & Justice.
  • Stress reduction improves health outcomes → higher Time & Stress and Health.
