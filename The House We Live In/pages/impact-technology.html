Impact Lab

Overview

Data rights and AI governance reduce exploitation and increase trust. Automation dividend design turns productivity gains into shared prosperity rather than displacement. This pillar protects civil rights while keeping national competitiveness strong.

Key outcome: less surveillance, more bargaining power, safer AI, shared productivity.
Metrics & projections

What we measure

  • Data access/portability compliance; number of coercive-data enforcement actions.
  • Privacy harm indicators: breaches, covert tracking incidents, biometric misuse.
  • AI audit outcomes: bias, security vulnerabilities, and harm mitigation rates.
  • Labor impacts: displacement rates, reemployment timelines, wage insurance utilization.
  • Automation dividend growth and distribution via the People’s Wealth Fund.
  • Competitiveness: R&D intensity, startup formation, export competitiveness in trustworthy AI.
