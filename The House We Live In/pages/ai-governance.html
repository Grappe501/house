Philosophy

Regulate AI like aviation, medicine, and finance: risk tiers + testing + liability

We don’t regulate AI by guessing every future application. We regulate it by risk. Low-risk uses face light-touch rules; high-risk uses face strict safety, transparency, and due-process requirements.

The goal is twofold: protect people and win globally by leading in trustworthy AI standards.

Rules

The Working‑Majority AI Framework

  • Prohibited: certain coercive uses that destroy rights (e.g., unbounded mass biometric surveillance in public spaces).
  • High-risk: employment, housing, credit, insurance, education, sentencing, critical infrastructure.
  • General purpose: transparency + provenance + safety documentation.
  • Low-risk: disclosure-only or minimal requirements.
Safety

Frontier model testing and accountability

  • Pre-deployment red team testing and independent safety evaluation for frontier models.
  • Cybersecurity and model theft resistance requirements.
  • Clear liability rules for negligent deployment in high-risk domains.
  • Algorithmic due process: explanation, appeal, and human review where AI affects life chances.
Key safeguard: if you can’t explain and audit the decision in a high-risk domain, you can’t deploy it.
Competitiveness

How we don’t fall behind

  • Massive talent pipeline: free education + paid training + immigration pathways for high-skill roles.
  • Compute and chip capacity strategy; secure domestic supply chains.
  • Fast diffusion into small business and manufacturing—AI as a productivity tool for the 90%.
  • Standards leadership: export trustworthy AI standards through alliances and trade.
