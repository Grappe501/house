Principle

Wealth cannot become government

When wealth concentrates, it becomes political power and overwrites democracy. Wealth limits exist to prevent oligarchy, not to punish success.

Pragmatic design

Phase-in, diversify, normalize

  • Phased caps: gradual thresholds to avoid shock.
  • Broad ownership: convert concentrated holdings into public-benefit trusts and citizen ownership rails.
  • Exit barriers: anti-haven and exit taxes prevent flight games.
  • Innovation reward: real entrepreneurship is protected; monopoly rent is targeted.
What billionaires do next

How society changes

  • Capital shifts from hoarding to productive investment.
  • Philanthropy becomes supplemental, not a shadow government.
  • Wage growth and local enterprise become the new prestige and stability engine.
