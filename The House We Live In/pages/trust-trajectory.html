Trust Trajectory

Act IV Beam anchor: Room 094 (Trust Dividend). This is a Level 2 narrative+metrics bridge.

What this claims (carefully)

Trust is not a request. It’s an output: it accumulates when the system behaves predictably, corrects itself, and remains legible.

We treat this as a trajectory, not a slogan.

Signals (v1)

  • Volatility reduction: fewer emergencies, fewer last-minute reversals.
  • Participation elasticity: people show up when it feels worth it.
  • Compliance costs: fewer enforcement actions needed over time.

Mechanisms that generate trust

  • Drift alarms + timely correction
  • Readable audit trails and patch notes
  • Role rotation and visible stewardship
  • Training pipelines that prevent “expert monopolies”

How this ties to QOL

Trust affects the fear component in the QOL Index: predictable systems lower uncertainty. It also reduces time-costs: fewer loops, fewer crises, fewer emergency rules.
