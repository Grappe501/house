Community Policing & Local Control

Home / The Model / Justice & Safety / Community Policing
WHY

Safety requires legitimacy

When communities do not trust policing, they stop cooperating — and safety gets worse.

Rule: power escalates locally. Public safety is local by default.
HOW

Constable-style structure (modernized)

  • Community Safety Councils set priorities and evaluate outcomes.
  • Local oversight boards with subpoena power for misconduct investigations.
  • Transparent discipline (with privacy-protecting redaction where required).
  • Separation of roles: mental health response is not armed response.
  • Training standards set nationally, implemented locally.
WHAT

Federal role (limited and supportive)

  • Provide funding, training frameworks, and data systems.
  • Enforce baseline civil rights protections.
  • No federal forces on the ground controlling communities.
  • In emergencies, federal assets operate under local leadership control.
Metrics

What we track publicly

  • Use-of-force incidents (rate + context)
  • Complaint outcomes and discipline timelines
  • Clearance rates for serious crimes
  • Community trust surveys
  • Response outcomes for mental health calls

These metrics feed the QoLI Safety & Justice domain over time.
