Level 2 — Reform Durability Model

Act III Beam Room 054 reference page. (v1 skeleton)

Core claim

Reforms don’t stick because the system has reset paths. Durability happens when reset paths are blocked by structure, not by willpower.

Durability checklist (v1)

  • Authority: who can reverse it, and how easily?
  • Transparency: can the public see drift early?
  • Triggers: does the structure auto-review when metrics worsen?
  • Local feedback: do affected communities have correction power?
