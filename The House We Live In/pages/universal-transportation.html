WHY

Universal transportation is an equalizer

Universal transportation reduces isolation, expands job access, lowers household costs, and increases national productivity. It also enables a healthier distribution of population by making rural and small-town living realistic.

HOW

Service guarantees (scaffold)

  • Minimum service: every community has a reachable mobility hub or on-demand system.
  • Interoperability: one payment system, one scheduling interface, public service standards.
  • Pricing: affordability protections; no punitive fees; transparent subsidies where justified.
  • Safety: design for safety; reduce traffic deaths and injuries.
WHAT

System components (scaffold)

  • Rural hub-and-spoke mobility (microtransit + fixed routes)
  • Regional express corridors (bus first; rail where it wins)
  • Urban transit upgrades (frequency, reliability, safety)
  • Freight modernization (rail + ports + routing)
