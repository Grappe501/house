WHY

Energy is the backbone of the economy

Energy policy must prioritize reliability and affordability. Transition is staged to avoid system shocks. The key question is: who benefits from modernization — families or monopoly owners?

HOW

Staged transition (scaffold)

  • Grid resilience first: harden infrastructure, reduce outage risk
  • Price transparency: fuel mix, capital costs, profits, and rate impacts
  • Local reinvestment: projects hire locally and return value locally
  • Distributed energy where it reduces costs (microgrids, storage, efficiency)
  • Long-run decarbonization without sacrificing reliability
WHAT

Household and national outcomes (scaffold)

  • Lower volatility in utility bills
  • Reduced outage costs (food spoilage, missed work, health risk)
  • More local energy jobs and supply chains
  • Higher national productivity with stable inputs
