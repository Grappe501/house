Impact Lab

Overview

Disability support increases social stability and reduces long-run costs by preventing homelessness, untreated illness, and exploitation. A humane system also reduces administrative waste and adversarial churn.

Current system

What we see today

  • Humiliating proof rituals; slow determinations; frequent benefit cliffs.
  • Higher homelessness and crisis utilization when supports fail.
  • Work disincentives created by punitive eligibility rules.
New system

What changes

  • Dignity-first supports with stable eligibility and clear appeals.
  • No-cliff tapering; partial participation encouraged where possible.
  • Universal accessibility standards reduce exclusion.
Metrics & projections

What we measure

  • Housing stability, health outcomes, and crisis-service utilization.
  • Time-to-decision and appeal resolution performance.
  • Employment participation by choice (where applicable) and income stability.
  • Administrative cost per beneficiary and error rates.
