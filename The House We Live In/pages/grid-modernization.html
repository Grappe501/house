Why the grid matters

Everything depends on it

Transportation electrification, universal broadband, hospitals, water systems, and manufacturing all require a reliable grid. Grid modernization is national competitiveness infrastructure.

Microgrids and resilience

Local power in emergencies

  • Microgrids for hospitals, resilience hubs, and key community centers.
  • Islandable systems for disaster events.
  • Community storage to reduce peak stress and lower costs.
Cybersecurity

Treat it like defense

  • Mandatory security standards and incident reporting.
  • Red-team testing and supply chain security requirements.
  • Rapid response partnerships with the Cyber Corps.
