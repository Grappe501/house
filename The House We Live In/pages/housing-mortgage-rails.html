Problem

Mortgage debt is a stability risk

Homeownership becomes fragile when mortgages are opaque, fees are predatory, and refinancing is controlled by banks with incentive to extract.

Public banking mortgage rails

A clean, transparent mortgage system

  • Plain-language standardized mortgages with minimal fees.
  • Automatic refinancing when rates drop (shared benefit).
  • Foreclosure prevention with mediation and restructuring options.
  • Local credit allocation prioritizes owner-occupants.
Future debt reset compatibility

Preparing for the mortgage transition

Later builds will define how mortgage debt can be reduced without collapsing the system—through refinancing, public buy-downs, and phased transitions. This page is the scaffold for that future policy.
