Drift Review Cadence

Last updated: 2026-02-01. Act IV Beam anchor: Room 079 (The Drift Test).

What “drift” means here

Drift is the slow lean: policies and practices move away from their intended outcomes without a headline. Drift is normal. Ignoring drift is how systems decay and how capture returns quietly.

Cadence (v1)

  • Weekly: drift scan (check a short metrics list) + publish a note (“green / yellow / red”).
  • Monthly: review top 3 drift signals + choose corrections + publish patch notes.
  • Quarterly: audit walkthrough (public, legible) + test for loopholes (Room 087).
  • Annual: recalibrate thresholds + refresh the shared manual (Room 098).

Threshold philosophy

  • Green: normal variation; monitor.
  • Yellow: likely drift; schedule a correction proposal.
  • Red: threshold breach; trigger playbook immediately.
