Rural reality

Distance is the hidden tax

Rural households often face long travel times to full-service grocery stores. That increases fuel costs, reduces diet quality, and makes seniors and low-income families vulnerable.

Solutions

Access without forcing urbanization

  • Mobile markets on predictable routes.
  • Hub-and-spoke distribution: food hubs → mobility hubs → local pickup points.
  • Community-owned stores where the market fails.
  • Delivery support for seniors and disabled residents.

Universal transportation makes these solutions cheaper and more reliable.

Metrics

How we prove it worked

  • Travel time to staples and produce.
  • Price index vs regional wages.
  • Fresh food availability and stock stability.
