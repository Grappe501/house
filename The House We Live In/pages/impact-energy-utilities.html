Impact Lab

Utilities impact everything

  • GDP productivity: stable, universal infrastructure increases output and reduces friction.
  • Rural revitalization: broadband + reliable utilities make rural living viable and desirable.
  • Cost of living: utilities are fixed costs; reducing extraction raises quality of life instantly.
  • AI & competitiveness: compute and digital economy require stable power and universal access.
Metrics (scaffold)

What we will measure

  • Household utility burden (% of income)
  • Outage frequency/duration; water safety incidents
  • Broadband coverage and performance parity
  • Local reinvestment rates and job creation
  • QoLI affordability/time-stress improvements
