# Glossary → Dive Map

## Purpose
Definitions in bubbles are “Level A.” Dives are “Level C-ish”: deeper context, mechanics references, and cross-links.

## Glossary term format (in room HTML)
- `data-def="short definition"`
- optional: `data-dive="/pages/book/dives/act-i/dive-threshold.html"`

## Dive page expectations
A dive page should include:
- a short definition (repeat)
- why it matters in the house metaphor
- where it appears in the book (room references)
- links to:
  - Level B impact pages (if relevant)
  - Level C mechanics pages (if relevant)
- a “return to room” link

## Priority initial dive terms
Start with terms already appearing in Act I:
- threshold
- load
- friction
- reset
- paper maze
- trap door
- porch

## Cross-link rule
Every dive must link to at least one:
- Impact page or
- Mechanics page
Otherwise it stays Level A only and isn’t worth being a “dive” yet.
