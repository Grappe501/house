# Settings and Atmosphere (Grounding the Metaphor)

The house is the stage, but reality must echo through recurring real-world settings.

## Primary settings (repeat across Acts)
### Waiting room (healthcare)
- sensory: fluorescent light, forms, silence, clock
- function: time theft + vulnerability + the paper maze

### Break room (work)
- sensory: microwaves, schedules, “policy” posters
- function: compression, exhaustion, quiet dignity

### Kitchen table (budget)
- sensory: receipts, phones, a notebook, late-night quiet
- function: the weight + the trap door

### Car at a red light (commute)
- sensory: radio news, dashboard light, tired eyes
- function: time theft + the myth layer (attic narratives)

### School office (paper maze)
- sensory: laminated signs, the wrong line, the wrong day
- function: complexity as control

### City hall hallway (local governance)
- sensory: carpet, closed doors, “meeting moved”
- function: legitimacy and distance

### Bank lobby / loan office (basement mechanics)
- sensory: polite smiles, rates, fine print
- function: friction as revenue

## Atmospheric palette (by Act)
- Act I: dim, close, creaking, breath
- Act II: lamps turn on, structure revealed
- Act III: daylight, tools, measurable steps
- Act IV: porch lights, shared work, calm

## Setting checklist for rooms
- What sensory detail anchors the scene?
- What system pressure is present (time, money, complexity, capture)?
- What symbol appears (door, beam, thermostat, porch)?
- What changes by the end of the room?
