# Level 2 & Level 3 Handoffs (How the Book Pulls Readers Deeper)

## The “Door Line” system
A **Door Line** is the final line of a room that:
- lands the emotion
- implies the next step
- becomes a clickable link in the website reader

### Door Line types
1) **Trajectory Door** (Level 2 visual)
   - “See the decade-by-decade map.”

2) **Impact Door** (Level B)
   - “Here’s how this changes outcomes.”

3) **Mechanics Door** (Level C)
   - “Here’s what enforces it.”

4) **Proof Door** (Level C tools)
   - “Move the slider; watch the indicator respond.”

## Act-level handoffs (requirements)
- End Act I: soft trajectory invitation (reader is still tender)
- End Act II: explicit map invitation (reader is curious)
- End Act III: strong side-by-side invitation (reader is ready to judge)
- End Act IV: proof layer invitation (reader wants receipts)

## How Level 2 should feel (promise)
**The New Future Government** is presented as:
- pragmatic
- legible
- local-first
- anti-capture by design
- market-preserving
- transitionable without fantasy

Deliverables in Level 2:
- side-by-side trajectories
- clear sequencing (“what changes first”)
- a “why it works” explanation without ideology

## How Level 3 should feel (promise)
**Proof without arrogance.**
- assumptions are explicit
- uncertainty is acknowledged
- sources are linkable
- interactive tools are honest (no fake precision)

Deliverables in Level 3:
- assumption registry
- source registry + audit trail
- sliders / scenarios
- indicator panels (Quality of Life, stability, cost burden, etc.)
