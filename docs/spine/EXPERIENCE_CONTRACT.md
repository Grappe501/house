# EXPERIENCE CONTRACT

This work is a **guided psychological and emotional experience**.

It is intentionally paced.
It is intentionally structured.
It is intentionally quiet.

## Consent
By beginning Story Mode, you consent to proceed at the pace of the experience.
You may pause or exit at any time.
You are never required to continue.

This experience:
- Controls time, pacing, and silence
- Uses audio, light, and motion intentionally
- Is designed to regulate attention and emotion

It does **not**:
- Diagnose
- Manipulate beliefs
- Demand action
- Remove your agency

## Modes
- **Story Mode (Default):** guided, audio-led, paced
- **Watch Mode (Unlocked):** perceptual, symbolic, attentive
- **Discover Mode (Unlocked):** exploratory, contextual
- **Read Mode:** static text only

Depth must be earned through presence.
You may stop at any time.

## Safety
If this experience becomes distressing, stop.
Return later, or not at all.

The House will wait.
