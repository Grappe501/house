# ACT I EXPERIENCE SCORE

Act I is a one-hour guided experience.
Total runtime: **3600 seconds**.

The act is divided into five movements.
Each room is scored for emotional function, pacing, silence, and discovery.

---

## Movement 1 — Arrival & Regulation (Rooms 001–005)
Goal: safety, permission, nervous system settling.

## Movement 2 — Naming the Pressure (Rooms 006–010)
Goal: relief, validation, stress without blame.

## Movement 3 — Structural Seeing (Rooms 011–016)
Goal: legibility, understanding, curiosity.

## Movement 4 — Widening the We (Rooms 017–021)
Goal: belonging, trust, grounded anger.

## Movement 5 — Threshold & Agency (Rooms 022–025)
Goal: ownership, readiness, calm continuation.

---

## Room Scoring Template

Each room specifies:
- Emotional target
- Audio pace profile
- Silence weight
- Visual posture
- Discovery eligibility

---

## Key Rules
- Audio ON by default
- No reading ahead in Story Mode
- Text visible only while spoken
- Pausing audio hides text
- Watch/Discover unlock only after full Act completion
- One hidden discovery max per room
- Silence counts as time

Act I is frozen.
No changes permitted without full-book integration pass.
