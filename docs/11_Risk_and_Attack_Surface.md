# Risk & Attack Surface (How this gets misread)

## Likely attacks
- “It’s socialism” (even if it isn’t)
- “It’s authoritarian” (because it changes structures)
- “It’s conspiracy” (if global section is framed poorly)
- “It’s anti-business” (unless thresholds are front-and-center)
- “It’s anti-faith” (unless separation is framed respectfully)

## Preemptive mitigation
- emphasize thresholds: $250k / $20M
- emphasize markets remain; monopolies don’t
- emphasize local power; national limits
- keep global section opt-in, evidence-first
- define terms and avoid loaded labels

## Tone discipline
Never sound like:
- revenge
- purge
- takeover
Always sound like:
- stability
- dignity
- fairness
- competence
- accountability

## Operational safety
- moderation policies for community
- no harassment or targeting
- no data exploitation
