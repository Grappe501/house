# Room 006 — Edges

## Purpose
Room 006 introduces boundaries as informational rather than restrictive.

Edges orient the reader without confining them.

---

## Psychological Objectives
- Reduce fear of structure
- Normalize boundaries
- Establish spatial awareness

---

## Core Design Principles
- No walls or borders
- No responsibility language
- Edges are sensed, not shown

---

## Visual Behavior
- Margins slightly narrower
- Stable light level
- Continued subtle drift

---

## Spoken Experience
Boundaries are framed as information:
“They tell you where you are.”

---

## Outcome
By the end of Room 006, the reader experiences boundaries as grounding rather than limiting.
