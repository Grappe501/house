# Room 002 — Presence

## Purpose
Room 002 is the first content-bearing room, but its content is *orientation*, not information.

Its role is to move the reader from consent into **quiet attention**.

---

## Psychological Objectives
- Anchor attention in the present moment
- Reduce performance anxiety
- Normalize stillness
- Establish that attention alone is sufficient

---

## Core Design Principles
- Continued audio-led pacing
- No analysis or metaphor
- No discovery or signals
- Gentle increase in light

---

## Spoken Script Overview
The language:
- Separates the reader from past and future
- Removes expectation of evaluation
- Frames the experience as a place to stand

The key line:
> “This is not a test.”

This directly counters internalized judgment.

---

## Visual Behavior
- Background slightly brighter than Room 001
- No motion
- No geometry shifts
- Text appears only while spoken
- Screen empties during pauses

This reinforces that silence is safe.

---

## Sound Design
- No music
- No environmental sound
- Voice and pause do the work

---

## Outcome
By the end of Room 002, the reader has:
- Settled into attention
- Released performance pressure
- Accepted slowness as intentional
- Prepared psychologically for meaning

Room 002 completes the transition from arrival to readiness.
