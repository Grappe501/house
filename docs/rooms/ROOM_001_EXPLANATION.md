# Room 001 — Arrival

## Purpose
Room 001 is the threshold into the House. Its sole function is to establish **consent, safety, and tempo**.  
It does not deliver content. It delivers *conditions*.

This room teaches the reader how this experience works without explaining rules explicitly.

---

## Psychological Objectives
- Reduce vigilance and urgency
- Establish voluntary surrender to pace
- Signal authority without dominance
- Create nervous-system safety
- Transition the reader from control to presence

---

## Core Design Principles
- Audio-led, default ON
- Silence is intentional and counted
- No interaction beyond consent
- No metaphor, no analysis, no discovery

---

## Spoken Script Overview
The script uses:
- Short declarative sentences
- Long pauses (often longer than comfort)
- Flat, grounded delivery
- Explicit exit permission

Nothing is hidden. Nothing is demanded.

---

## Visual Behavior
- Background: dim, warm neutral
- No motion
- No geometry changes
- Text appears only while spoken
- Text disappears during pauses

Silence results in a blank screen.

---

## Ethical Framing
Room 001 explicitly discloses:
- This is a guided psychological experience
- The reader may leave at any time
- No beliefs or actions are required

This establishes trust and informed consent.

---

## Outcome
By the end of Room 001, the reader has:
- Slowed their breathing
- Accepted the pace
- Understood that time—not scrolling—controls the experience
- Entered the House willingly

Room 001 is complete only when nothing feels rushed or clever.
