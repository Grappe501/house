# Room 005 — The First Step

## Purpose
Room 005 introduces movement without requiring action.

Change occurs through time, not effort.

---

## Psychological Objectives
- Normalize motion as organic
- Remove pressure to decide or perform
- Allow participation without agency demand

---

## Core Design Principles
- No literal walking language
- No direction or destination
- First permissible visual drift

---

## Visual Behavior
- Extremely slow background drift
- Light slightly warmer
- No visible motion cues

Motion is felt, not observed.

---

## Spoken Experience
Movement is framed as something that happens naturally when stillness changes.

---

## Outcome
By the end of Room 005, the reader understands that change is already occurring.
