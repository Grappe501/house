# Room 004 — The Pause

You continue down the hall, but your pace shifts without your meaning to change it.

There’s no reason to slow down. Nothing ahead suggests it. And yet, your steps fall a little more carefully now, as though you’ve decided they should.

You become aware of where you’re standing. How close you are to the wall. How the floor feels under your feet.

You stop.

Not because something blocks the way. Not because you’re unsure where to go. You simply pause, standing there long enough for the moment to register.

The house doesn’t respond. It doesn’t seem to notice.

Still, the pause feels deliberate, even if you didn’t choose it.

After a second, you move again.
