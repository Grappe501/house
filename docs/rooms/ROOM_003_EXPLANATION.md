# Room 003 — The Shape You’re In

## Purpose
Room 003 introduces structure without naming it. The reader begins to sense containment as a bodily experience rather than an idea.

This room establishes the *felt presence* of structure before language is applied.

---

## Psychological Objectives
- Create curiosity without threat
- Establish containment as neutral
- Encourage somatic recognition
- Prepare the reader for metaphor without explanation

---

## Core Design Principles
- No naming of the structure
- No visual symbols
- No motion beyond inherited stillness
- Silence as confirmation

---

## Spoken Experience
Language is suggestive, not declarative.  
Phrases like “a shape” and “held” allow the body to decide meaning.

---

## Visual Behavior
- Slightly wider margins
- No borders or lines
- Light increment barely perceptible

Containment is felt, not shown.

---

## Outcome
By the end of Room 003, the reader recognizes that they are *already inside something*, without being told what it is.
