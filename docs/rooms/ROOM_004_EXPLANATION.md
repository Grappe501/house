# Room 004 — The House

## Purpose
Room 004 names the structure for the first time using ordinary language.

The goal is shared reference, not symbolic weight.

---

## Psychological Objectives
- Translate sensation into language
- Preserve agency by offering, not imposing, metaphor
- Establish the House as neutral structure

---

## Core Design Principles
- The word “house” appears once
- No visual emphasis
- No elevation of metaphor

---

## Spoken Experience
The naming moment is delivered plainly:
“You can think of it as a house.”

This phrasing preserves choice.

---

## Visual Behavior
- Slight warmth increase
- No motion
- Margins subtly more open

---

## Outcome
By the end of Room 004, the reader accepts the House as a usable reference frame.
