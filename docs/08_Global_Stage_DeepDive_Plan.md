# Global Stage Deep Dive (Opt‑In, Evidence‑First)

## Purpose
Create an advanced library explaining:
- how nation-state relations function under capital constraints
- how debt, trade, and currency create leverage
- how corporate power often supersedes national sovereignty
- how worker-first systems can scale globally by example

## The book rule
The book provides only:
- a short, calm “glimpse” that money and leverage shape nations
- a bridge line pointing to deeper learning online

## Website rule
This section is:
- opt-in
- not in primary navigation
- evidence-first with citations and model transparency
- framed as education, not rage

## Long arc framing
- last ~500 years of extraction and concentration patterns
- how industrialization and financialization shifted power
- why workers globally experience similar pressures
- how a stable, prosperous U.S. working majority model could become the world’s rival philosophy

## Topics (to be built later)
- reserve currency dynamics
- sanctions as financial warfare
- “aid” and dependency loops
- capital flight and tax havens
- supply chains and labor arbitrage
- international institutions and bargaining power

## Outputs
- explainer pages
- diagrams
- scenario models (optional)
- reading lists (balanced sources)

## Risk controls
- avoid conspiratorial framing
- avoid ethnic/religious scapegoating
- keep to systems, incentives, documented history
