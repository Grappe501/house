# Book Blueprint (Primary Artifact — Next Phase)

## Working constraints
- ~100 pages paper format
- 3–4 hours read
- clear, calm, non-ideological tone
- reader assumes they know nothing about systems

## Purpose
1) Hook the reader on **WHY** (personal impact)
2) Reframe the reader on **HOW** (systems/incentives)
3) Invite the reader to **WHAT** (a pragmatic future path)
4) End with **ACTION** (relational organizing + civic tools)

## Structure (Golden Circle)
### Part I — WHY
- lived reality of work, time, stress, debt, instability
- “you’re not crazy” validation without blame
- show how the current trajectory plays out by decade (high level)

### Part II — HOW
- systems > personalities
- incentives > intentions
- money and complexity distort democracy
- glimpse: all nations are money-constrained and power-seeking (one short section)
- set the need for a new operating system

### Part III — WHAT
- present the new model at high level (not dense)
- pragmatic transition plan (no convention)
- show the future: stability, opportunity, decentralization, integrity
- clarify who benefits (≤ $250k individuals, ≤ $20M businesses)

### Ending — ACTION
- teach relational organizing without jargon
- give low-barrier actions (talk, share, convene, learn civic tools)
- invite them to the website for depth + tools + training

- Every concept word in the book maps to a website page
- Hover reveals short definitions
- Click opens drill-down

- Book can be free or low-cost
- Website depth + tools are paid (freemium)
- Training + organizer toolkits can be premium
