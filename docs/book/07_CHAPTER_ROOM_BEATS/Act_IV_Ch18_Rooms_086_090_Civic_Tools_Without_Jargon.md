# Act IV — Chapter 18 (Rooms 086–090)
## Civic Tools Without Jargon
_Last updated: 2026-02-03_

### Chapter promise (standalone)
Participation becomes self-defense when tools are legible and inspection is routine.

### Emotional arc (low-heat)
clear-eyed → cool scrutiny (beam) → measured legibility → composed rhythm → settled storage

### Symbols in play
plain tools • flashlight under floorboards • ledger • calendar • toolbox

### Non-drift rules
- No jargon without translation.
- No paranoia tone.
- Inspection is routine hygiene, not suspicion.
- End with silence.

## Five-room micro-structure
### Room 086 — Plain Tools
**Job:** Translate civic mechanics into household language.
**Key move:** jargon is distance; plain speech is presence.

### Room 087 — The Loophole Hunt (BEAM, locked)
**Job:** Establish continuous anti-capture review.
**Beam line (locked):** “Every rule creates a shadow. We check the shadows.”

### Room 088 — Open Ledger
**Job:** Make decisions legible: who decided, why, and what it cost.
**Key move:** legibility reduces rumor pressure.

### Room 089 — The Cadence
**Job:** Turn urgency into rhythm: scheduled review beats emergency response.
**Key move:** cadence preserves health and durability.

### Room 090 — Toolbox (silence)
**Job:** Store tools without fetishizing them.
**Key move:** tools rest; capacity remains.

## Word choices (why these nouns/verbs)
- Use **household physics**: load, joint, ledger, cadence, drift, repair.
- Prefer verbs that imply practice, not crusade: notice, record, schedule, test, restore, hand off.
- Avoid verbs that imply war or domination: fight, defeat, crush, take back.

## Required landing
The last room in this chapter must **lower heat** (silence) so the next chapter begins from steadiness.
