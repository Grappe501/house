# Color, Light, Audio, UI — Rooms 001–005

This chapter is sensory design first, meaning second.

## Palette (Act I warm neutral)
- Base: dim warm neutral (brown/umber/linen family; no pure black)
- Accent: soft brass / honey (sparingly, only in UI affordances)
- Text: off-white, not stark white (reduce cognitive “edge”)

## Light progression
- Room 001: dim, stable, no motion
- Room 002: +5–8% brightness (barely perceptible)
- Room 003: margins widen slightly (felt spaciousness)
- Room 004: warmth increase + tiny openness (naming moment is supported)
- Room 005: first permissible background drift (movement felt, not seen)

## Sound design
- Room 001: optional low hum or room tone (already present in assets)
- Rooms 002–004: no music; voice + pause does the work
- Room 005: consider introducing a barely-audible “air shift” (not a melody)

Rule: sound must never “tell the reader what to feel.” It only supports physiology.

## UI behavior
- Text appears only during narration
- Screen empties during pauses
- No interactive decisions yet beyond “audio on/off”
- No door-lines or mechanics links in Chapter 01

## Ethical guardrails (non-negotiable)
- No coercive framing
- Explicit exit permission in Room 001
- No “gotcha” reversals
- No fear-based hooks
