# Chapter 01 Symbol Map — Rooms 001–005

> Canon rule: The metaphor is never explained; it is **felt**.

This chapter seeds the symbol system at its gentlest setting.

## Primary symbols (with allowable intensity)
### 1) The Door (threshold / consent)
- **Meaning:** entry is voluntary; you control pace
- **Allowed cues:** unlocked, ordinary, human-scale
- **Not allowed:** trapped, sealed, “no way out” imagery (too early; violates safety)

### 2) Silence (space / nervous-system reset)
- **Meaning:** absence is not danger; it is room to breathe
- **Allowed cues:** pauses, blank screen, quiet warmth
- **Not allowed:** ominous quiet, predatory quiet, “the house holds its breath” (too threatening)

### 3) Light (permission / orientation)
- **Meaning:** increasing clarity without shock
- **Act I gradient:** dim warm neutral → slightly brighter → slightly wider space
- **Not allowed:** harsh white interrogation light, flicker horror cues

### 4) Containment / Shape (being inside systems)
- **Meaning:** we are always in a structure; that’s normal
- **Allowed cues:** “held,” “shape,” “rooms,” “walls” as neutral
- **Not allowed:** cage, prison, labyrinth (later Acts)

### 5) The Word “House” (shared reference)
- **Meaning:** ordinary language for complex structure
- **Rule:** appears once in Room 004, delivered plainly with optional framing (“you can think of it as…”)

## Secondary motifs (seed only)
These appear as background texture across the book; in Chapter 01 they should be *barely present*.

### “Weight”
- seed as fatigue / heaviness of modern life
- no blame, no cause yet

### “Care”
- care that feels human vs care that feels procedural
- in Chapter 01: keep it human (tea towel, soft light, ordinary wear)
- avoid “inspection” / “certificate” / “policy memo” vibes (too sharp)

### “Rules dressed as comfort”
- in Chapter 01, only a hint: the reader senses *expectation* without being told

## Symbol handoffs to later Acts (do not activate yet)
- Door → later becomes “doors” to mechanics pages (Level 2/3)
- House → later becomes the map of governance/economy/health/education rooms
- Light → later becomes clarity as legibility (systems can be understood)
