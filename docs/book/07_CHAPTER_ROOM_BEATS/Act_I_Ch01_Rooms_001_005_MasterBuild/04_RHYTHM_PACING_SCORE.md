# Rhythm & Pacing Score — Rooms 001–005

## Goal
Establish the book’s “musical score” without announcing it.

## Sentence-length profile (recommended)
- **Room 001:** 5–12 words average; clean declaratives; long pauses in audio
- **Room 002:** 8–16 words; steady flow; fewer fragments
- **Room 003:** 10–18 words; slightly more spacious; sensory language
- **Room 004:** 8–14 words; one “naming” sentence; then soften
- **Room 005:** 6–12 words; more white space; one slow turn

## White space and “breath”
- Include intentional paragraph breaks that *feel like exhale*.
- Silence is not filler; it is the container.

## Repetition (allowed)
Use controlled repetition to calm the reader:
- “You don’t have to…”
- “You can…”
- “Nothing…”

Do NOT repeat for menace (“again,” “still,” “can’t escape”) in this chapter.

## Camera distance (narrative lens)
- Rooms 001–002: close, gentle, first-person or second-person permissive
- Room 003: body-sensation lens (somatic)
- Room 004: name the metaphor, then widen slightly
- Room 005: micro-movement; hold the reader’s hand lightly

## Audio pacing
Room 001 sets the universal rule:
- text appears with speech
- silence empties screen
- narration pace ~ slow conversation, not performance

Chapter 01 is complete when the reader’s breathing slows by itself.
