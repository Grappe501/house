# Act III — Chapter 15 (Rooms 071–075)
## The New Trajectory — Locked 5-Room Arc

**Act Frame:** Reconstruction (REBUILD) — cognitive, not political  
**Chapter function:** Show the *felt* difference of a new trajectory in human terms, then exit Act III without ordering the reader to act.

---

## Chapter promise (standalone)
A new trajectory is not a miracle. It is **stress reduction** that compounds into quality of life. The proof is in the daily load, not the rhetoric.

### Emotional arc (low-heat)
Quiet lift → practical measurement → stable normal → comparative clarity → calm exit

### Symbol spine
**Slope/trajectory line** • **QOL ledger** • **thermostat** • **side-by-side panels** • **threshold**

### Non-drift rules
- No triumph. No victory. No “we did it.”
- No recruitment / marching orders.
- No moral superiority (“the awake,” “the good”).
- Invitation is allowed only as **orientation to tools** (Level 2), not as obligation.

---

## Five-room micro-structure
### Room 071 — New Trajectory
**Job:** Define the trajectory shift as reduced stress + increased slack.  
**Key move:** life improves because load drops first.  
**Reader gain:** can recognize “better” by felt strain, not promises.

### Room 072 — Quality of Life (BEAM)
**Job:** Establish measurement: what it costs to live (time, money, attention).  
**Key move:** QOL is an accounting frame, not a moral claim.  
**Reader gain:** a practical yardstick to judge any proposal.

### Room 073 — The Thermostat
**Job:** Show stability as a setting: feedback loops that keep temperature steady.  
**Key move:** stability is design, not speeches.  
**Reader gain:** understands why durable reforms feel boring.

### Room 074 — Side‑by‑Sides
**Job:** Present comparison as method: evaluate futures without persuasion.  
**Key move:** side-by-side panels as clarity tools.  
**Reader gain:** can compare trajectories without tribal heat.

### Room 075 — Threshold (Silence)
**Job:** Exit Act III: a map in hand, no order given.  
**Key move:** readiness without command.  
**Reader gain:** calm capacity to enter Act IV “do-phase” without panic.

---

## Hooks seeded → payoffs later
- **QOL ledger** becomes the non-ideological test in Act IV.
- **Thermostat** supports “get shit done” without overheating the reader.
- **Side‑by‑sides** become the bridge into Level 2 tools and the Act IV agenda.

---

## Language guardrails
Prefer: *trajectory, slope, load, slack, cost to live, thermostat, feedback, compare*  
Avoid: *win, defeat, revolution, awaken, righteous, duty, must*
