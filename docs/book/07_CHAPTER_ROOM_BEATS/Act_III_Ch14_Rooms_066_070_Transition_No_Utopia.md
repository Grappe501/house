# Act III — Chapter 14 (Rooms 066–070)
## The Transition (No Utopia) — Locked 5-Room Arc

**Act Frame:** Reconstruction (REBUILD) — cognitive, not political  
**Chapter function:** Turn “a better future” into a **buildable transition** without fantasy, without coercion.  
**Reader state entering (after Room 065):** steady, discerning, less reactive, more cost-aware.

---

## Chapter promise (standalone)
You can move from *current conditions* to a different trajectory **without a clean break**. Transition is carpentry: sequencing, constraints, interfaces, cure time.

### Emotional arc (low-heat)
Measured focus → sober realism → quiet confidence → decompression (silence)

### Symbol spine
**Schedule / order of operations** • **load-bearing constraints** • **bridge materials** • **interfaces/handoffs** • **curing joint**

### Non-drift rules
- No utopia language (no “finally,” no “end of,” no “once and for all”).
- No recruitment (“we must,” “join,” “take back”).
- No villains. No blame. No targets.
- Teach **how to judge sequencing**, not what to push.

---

## Five-room micro-structure
### Room 066 — Sequencing
**Job:** Establish that transition is an order-of-operations problem.  
**Key move:** “First move reduces pressure,” not “first move wins.”  
**Reader gain:** ability to ask “what unlocks the next step?”

### Room 067 — Non‑Negotiables
**Job:** Identify structural constraints that cannot be negotiated away.  
**Key move:** constraints as *physics*, not morality.  
**Reader gain:** clarity on what must be designed around, not argued away.

### Room 068 — Bridge Materials
**Job:** Show transition uses what exists: tools, habits, institutions, relationships.  
**Key move:** repurpose before replace.  
**Reader gain:** realism without resignation.

### Room 069 — Interfaces
**Job:** Make handoffs visible: where change gets lost or translated.  
**Key move:** interfaces are the real “failure points.”  
**Reader gain:** focus on joints, not slogans.

### Room 070 — Cure Time (Silence)
**Job:** Lower temperature; integrate.  
**Key move:** curing is part of the build; loading early is how things fail.  
**Reader gain:** patience without passivity.

---

## Hooks seeded → payoffs later
- **Interfaces** will echo in Act IV as “where effort converts vs evaporates.”
- **Cure time** will protect Act IV from urgency spirals.
- **Non‑negotiables** sets the “no fantasy” tone for the call-to-action phase.

---

## Language guardrails
Prefer: *sequence, load, joint, interface, cure, constraint, tradeoff, handoff*  
Avoid: *fight, demand, expose, overthrow, save, wake up, finally, destiny*
