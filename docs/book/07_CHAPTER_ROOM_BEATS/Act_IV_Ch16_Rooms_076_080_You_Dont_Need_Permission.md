# Act IV — Chapter 16 (Rooms 076–080)
## You Don’t Need Permission
_Last updated: 2026-02-03_

### Chapter promise (standalone)
Belonging becomes agency through presence and consent—no aggression required.

### Emotional arc (low-heat)
steady warmth → clear contact → pragmatic willingness → cool clarity (beam) → settled availability

### Symbols in play
threshold • eye level • small ask • level on a shelf • porch light

### Non-drift rules
- No recruitment language.
- No enemy framing.
- Agency is quiet, consent-based.
- End with silence, not adrenaline.

## Five-room micro-structure
### Room 076 — Knock
**Job:** Establish consent and belonging as the ethical start.
**Key move:** agency is allowed to begin quietly.

### Room 077 — Eye Level
**Job:** Define legitimacy through presence at human scale.
**Key move:** distance reduces feedback; nearness restores it.

### Room 078 — One Small Ask
**Job:** Make action feel normal: one step, not allegiance.
**Key move:** capacity respected is capacity sustained.

### Room 079 — The Drift Test (BEAM, locked)
**Job:** Teach drift detection as governance.
**Beam line (locked):** “Drift doesn’t announce itself. It leans.”

### Room 080 — Porch Light (silence)
**Job:** Close with quiet availability; no push.
**Key move:** the door remains visible; pressure stays low.

## Word choices (why these nouns/verbs)
- Use **household physics**: load, joint, ledger, cadence, drift, repair.
- Prefer verbs that imply practice, not crusade: notice, record, schedule, test, restore, hand off.
- Avoid verbs that imply war or domination: fight, defeat, crush, take back.

## Required landing
The last room in this chapter must **lower heat** (silence) so the next chapter begins from steadiness.
