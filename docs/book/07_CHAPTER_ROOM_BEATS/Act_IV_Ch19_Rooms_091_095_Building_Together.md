**Key move:** accountability without spectacle.

## Word choices (why these nouns/verbs)
- Use **household physics**: load, joint, ledger, cadence, drift, repair.
- Prefer verbs that imply practice, not crusade: notice, record, schedule, test, restore, hand off.
- Avoid verbs that imply war or domination: fight, defeat, crush, take back.
