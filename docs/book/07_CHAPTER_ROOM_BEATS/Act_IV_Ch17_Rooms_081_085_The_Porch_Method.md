# Act IV — Chapter 17 (Rooms 081–085)
## The Porch Method
_Last updated: 2026-02-03_

### Chapter promise (standalone)
Trust loops at human scale turn isolated actions into a stable system.

### Emotional arc (low-heat)
grounded → measured → quiet lift → practical lock-in (beam) → level rest

### Symbols in play
porch • invitation • repetition • routine • aftercare

### Non-drift rules
- Avoid ‘movement’ hype.
- Refusal is allowed.
- Repetition is framed as safety, not indoctrination.
- End with silence.

---

## Five-room micro-structure
### Room 081 — The Porch
**Job:** Introduce the porch as method: presence, repetition, safety.
**Key move:** legitimacy grows where people can return.

### Room 082 — The Offer
**Job:** Show “asks” as dignified choices, not tests of loyalty.
**Key move:** refusal is allowed; trust still holds.

### Room 083 — Trust Loops
**Job:** Teach repetition as the mechanism of safety.
**Key move:** loops are boring by design; that’s why they last.

### Room 084 — The New Default (BEAM, locked)
**Job:** Mark cultural embedding.
**Beam line (locked):** “What used to be extraordinary becomes normal.”

### Room 085 — Aftercare (silence)
**Job:** Reduce heat; protect capacity; prevent churn.
**Key move:** leave people steadier than you found them.


---

## Word choices (why these nouns/verbs)
- Use **household physics**: load, joint, ledger, cadence, drift, repair.
- Prefer verbs that imply practice, not crusade: notice, record, schedule, test, restore, hand off.
- Avoid verbs that imply war or domination: fight, defeat, crush, take back.

---

## Required landing
The last room in this chapter must **lower heat** (silence) so the next chapter begins from steadiness.
