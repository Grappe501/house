# ACT_I_ENDING_LOCK_v1

**Ending Architecture for Act I (Rooms 21–25)**  
_Status: Structural Lock_

## Global constraints
- No villains, threats, calls to action, or overt political language.
- Calm language; neutral tone; familiar metaphors.
- Discomfort arrives **after** reading (delayed recognition).

## Cross-room reader arc
| Room | Entry state | Exit state |
|---|---|---|
| 21 | Curious, alert | Relaxed, trusting |
| 22 | Comfortable | Slightly dulled |
| 23 | Neutral | Validated, faintly hollow |
| 24 | Receptive | Uneasy, guided |
| 25 | Open | Clear, implicated |

## Room 21 — The Well-Kept Place
**Function:** Normalize the system as competent and benign.  
**Symbols:** Clean lines, even light, absence of clutter.  
**Audio:** low hum or silence.  
**Door:** visible immediately, opens without effort.  
**Exit belief:** *“This place knows what it’s doing.”*

## Room 22 — The Routine
**Function:** Repetition as comfort, not constraint.  
**Symbols:** cyclical descriptions, reused phrasing.  
**Audio:** repetition or silence.  
**Door:** appears after routine completes.  
**Exit belief:** *“I’ve done this before.”*

## Room 23 — The Thank You
**Function:** Emotional reward for compliance.  
**Symbols:** praise without specifics; gratitude detached from outcomes.  
**Audio:** warm tone, no climax.  
**Door:** offered as courtesy.  
**Exit feeling:** *“I matter here… but to what?”*

## Room 24 — The Easy Door
**Function:** Conditional freedom (guidance without force).  
**Symbols:** one clear path; other paths vague.  
**Audio:** slight narrowing if used.  
**Door:** obvious door; others conceptual only.  
**Exit realization:** *“I wasn’t forced. I was guided.”*

## Room 25 — The House Speaks (Act I terminus)
**Function:** Reframe the house as a system that requires the reader.  
**Symbols:** space → function; neutral voice; no menace.  
**Audio:** silence preferred; if audio exists, end abruptly.  
**Door:** no clear exit; no next step explained.

**Final locked line:**
> You can stay.  
> Most people do.  
> The house needs you.

## Handoff note (Room 26)
Room 26 must assume: reader is awake but not activated; trust is cracked, not broken; curiosity is now personal.
