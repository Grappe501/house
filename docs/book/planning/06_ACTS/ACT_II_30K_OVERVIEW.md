# Act II — Rooms 26–50 (30,000‑Foot Overview)

## Act II Title: Compliance

### Core Act Promise
**You can leave the house—but not the system.**

Act II tests whether recognition is enough once consequences replace confusion.

---

## Structural Geometry

Act II applies **cyclical pressure with diminishing recovery**.

Relief → Cost → Adjustment → Rationalization  
(repeated, quieter, lonelier each time)

By Room 50, the reader no longer asks *“Is this happening?”*  
They ask *“What am I willing to live with?”*

---

## Five‑Module Architecture (Rooms 26–50)

### Module 1 — Normalization (Rooms 26–30)
**Pressure type:** Boredom, procedure, inevitability  
**House move:** “This is just how things are done.”

Reader learns to pre‑adjust behavior.  
First loss occurs quietly.

---

### Module 2 — Incentivized Compliance (Rooms 31–35)
**Pressure type:** Rewards, access, status  
**House move:** “We reward constructive participation.”

Nothing is taken.  
Things simply stop being offered.

---

### Module 3 — Isolation & Sorting (Rooms 36–40)
**Pressure type:** Loneliness, thinning allies  
**House move:** “People self‑select.”

Resistance becomes socially risky.

---

### Module 4 — Internalization (Rooms 41–45)
**Pressure type:** Self‑monitoring, guilt  
**House move:** “No one is making you do this.”

The reader briefly becomes what the system needs.

---

### Module 5 — Fracture Point (Rooms 46–50)
**Pressure type:** Forced cost, moral reckoning  
**House move:** “We need to know where you stand.”

Participation is revealed as the price of peace.

---

## Emotional Trajectory

Confidence → Weariness  
Agency → Cost‑awareness  
Solidarity → Isolation

Act II ends in **truth under pressure**, not rebellion.

---

## Act II → Act III Handoff

By Room 50, the reader understands:

> *The system does not need belief.  
> It only needs participation.*

Act III begins when compliance fractures.
