# Act I — Rooms 21–25 Plan

Rooms 21–25 — The Test → The Choice → The Naming → The Threshold → The Door

This final Act I module converts recognition into agency. The House pressures the narrator to revert—to minimize, to comply, to accept comfort without change. Each room asks for a different form of cooperation. The narrator refuses without dramatics, names the pattern aloud, and reaches a threshold. Room 25 is the Act Door: the moment the reader understands they are no longer just noticing the system—they are choosing how to meet it.
