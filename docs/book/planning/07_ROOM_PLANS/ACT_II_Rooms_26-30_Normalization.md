# Rooms 26–30 — Normalization (15,000‑Foot Module Outline)

## Module Thesis
**The most effective systems do not feel oppressive. They feel ordinary.**

Normalization is how compliance becomes invisible.

---

## Module Purpose
Rooms 26–30 transition the reader from *recognition* into *routine*.  
Pressure is reduced, clarity is offered, and the system becomes legible.

The danger here is not fear.
The danger is **settling**.

---

## Psychological Target
**Cognitive accommodation**

The reader begins adjusting behavior without being asked:
- “I’ll just do it this way.”
- “That’s how they want it.”
- “It’s not worth pushing on this.”

No shame. No coercion. Just adaptation.

---

| Room | Emotional State | Shift |

| 27 | Mild boredom | Attention dulls |

| 29 | Pre‑adjustment | Self‑editing |

---

## Symbolic Logic
Symbols are intentionally unremarkable:
- Procedures
- Consistency
- Polite signage
- Time blocks
- Neutral language

The House presents order as care.

---

## House Voice
- Calm
- Administrative
- Patient
- Mildly congratulatory

Common phrases:
- “For your convenience”
- “Standard process”
- “To keep things running smoothly”
- “Most people find…”

---

## Room Functions

### Room 26 — Orientation
**Purpose:** Reset expectations  
Rules are explained cleanly. Relief arrives.

Hidden move: The reader stops scanning for danger.

---

### Room 27 — Procedure
**Purpose:** Replace agency with process  
Tasks become steps. Repetition begins.

Hidden move: Thought becomes mechanical.

---

### Room 28 — Rhythm
**Purpose:** Install habit  
The system becomes ambient.

Hidden move: Resistance feels disruptive.

---

### Room 29 — Pre‑Adjustment
**Purpose:** Elicit self‑correction  
The reader edits themselves first.

Hidden move: Enforcement is internalized.

---

### Room 30 — The Ordinary Cost
**Purpose:** Reveal first loss  
Something small disappears.

Hidden move: Loss without drama trains tolerance.

---

## What Must Not Happen
- No villain
- No threat
- No punishment
- No confrontation

If the reader feels afraid, the module has failed.

---

## Handoff to Rooms 31–35
The closing thought should resemble:

*“If this is how it works, I might as well do it well.”*

That thought opens the door to **Incentivized Compliance**.

---

## One‑Sentence Module Thesis
**Rooms 26–30 show how systems gain power by making participation feel routine, reasonable, and unremarkable.**
