# Act I — Rooms 11–15 Plan

**Module:** Reframe → Scale Shift → Validation → Non‑Blame → Breath

## Module intent
This module is the first real *repair arc* in Act I. After the system is revealed (06–10), the narrator is given language that reduces shame. Stress is reframed as signal, then shown as structural; anger is validated without creating villains; blame is withheld; the reader is given breath and perspective before the next compression cycle.

## Reader movement
- Stress is named as signal (not verdict).
- Stress is shown as structural (designed pressure).
- Anger is validated as integrity (boundary alarm).
- Villains are refused (patterns over scapegoats).
- Breath/perspective installs hope as stability (not denial).

## Rooms 11–15 breakdown
### Room 11: Stress as Signal
- **Subtitle:** The body is not betraying you.
- **Psychology target:** Reframe stress from personal failure to intelligible signal. Reduce self-blame; increase curiosity.
- **Emotional move:** Tension → naming → insight → relief.
- **Symbols:**
  - **Foundation vibration (felt, not heard)** — stress as information moving through structure
  - **Hairline tremor line** — the smallest evidence is still evidence
  - **A simple gauge / indicator** — signal that can be read, not judged
  - **A note that says 'Not a verdict'** — permission to interpret instead of confess
  - **The hum softened into pulse** — the system has a rhythm; you can notice it
- **Prose beats:**
  - Narrator notices physical stress responses without moralizing them.
  - An object (gauge/line) frames stress as measurement, not guilt.
  - The House offers language that sounds helpful—but carries quiet control undertones.
  - Narrator accepts the naming while keeping their agency: 'signal, not verdict.'
  - Exit line points to the next realization: the signal is coming from the structure itself.
- **Handoff:** The narrator follows the signal outward—toward the load-bearing places where pressure is designed.

### Room 12: When Stress Becomes Structural
- **Subtitle:** Pressure is engineered.
- **Psychology target:** Shift from individual coping to systemic diagnosis: the design produces stress reliably.
- **Emotional move:** Recognition → sobriety → clarity.
- **Symbols:**
  - **Load-bearing wall** — invisible duties made permanent
  - **Crack that widens under 'normal use'** — harm isn’t exceptional; it’s expected
  - **Blueprint fragment** — the pressure was planned
  - **Support beam labeled 'personal responsibility'** — virtue language masking load transfer
  - **Door that sticks only for some** — unequal friction as design
- **Prose beats:**
  - Narrator maps where tension rises in the House—certain corners, certain doors.
  - A wall/beam takes more than it should; the metaphor turns structural.
  - Blueprint language appears: planned stress, not accidental stress.
  - The narrator sees how 'responsibility' is used to justify overload.
  - Exit with a sobering sentence: the structure can’t be solved by attitude alone.
- **Handoff:** The recognition unlocks anger—not explosive, but accurate. The next room permits it.

### Room 13: Quiet Anger Validated
- **Subtitle:** Anger as integrity.
- **Psychology target:** Normalize anger as a sign of violated boundaries, not a character flaw. Permit moral energy.
- **Emotional move:** Validation → release → steadiness.
- **Symbols:**
  - **Crack as honesty** — damage that tells the truth
  - **A small hammer left untouched** — violence is not required for truth
  - **A label: 'This is not you'** — separating identity from condition
  - **Red thread visible for first time** — the through-line is real
  - **A sealed jar of 'good manners'** — politeness as containment
- **Prose beats:**
  - Narrator admits anger in plain language—no apology.
  - The House tries to rename it as 'unreasonable'; narrator refuses the label.
  - Anger is framed as integrity: a boundary alarm.
  - A small, clean release—breath, unclenched jaw, steadier stance.
  - Exit into a more open space where blame is withheld to keep clarity.
- **Handoff:** With anger permitted, the narrator can stop hunting villains and start reading patterns.

### Room 14: No Villains
- **Subtitle:** Patterns, not monsters.
- **Psychology target:** Prevent scapegoating. Keep the reader from turning clarity into hatred; preserve safety and complexity.
- **Emotional move:** Safety → trust → grounded focus.
- **Symbols:**
  - **Wide room / open geometry** — space to think without panic
  - **Two chairs facing same direction** — alignment without coercion
  - **A blank plaque** — refusal to assign a single name
  - **A ledger with many hands** — systems are collective, not singular
  - **A window with no bars** — freedom begins as perception
- **Prose beats:**
  - Narrator is tempted to pick a villain; the room refuses easy targets.
  - Multiple causes appear: habits, incentives, fear, tradition, convenience.
  - The House’s politeness is shown as a social technology, not a person.
  - Narrator feels safer: complexity reduces paranoia.
  - Exit line introduces the need for breath: clarity requires oxygen.
- **Handoff:** The next room is a breathing space—an earned pause before the next squeeze.

### Room 15: Breathing Space
- **Subtitle:** Perspective without escape.
- **Psychology target:** Downshift the nervous system. Install hope as stability, not fantasy. Prepare the reader for deeper work.
- **Emotional move:** Stability → hope → readiness.
- **Symbols:**
  - **Window** — perspective—seeing beyond the immediate room
  - **Warm light** — earned safety
  - **A simple horizon line** — future exists
  - **Curtain that moves slightly** — air returns; the house can breathe
  - **A small note: 'Rebuild is possible'** — Act I promise fulfilled locally
- **Prose beats:**
  - Narrator reaches a window: light changes, geometry opens.
  - The hum is present but quieter; the narrator is less captured by it.
  - A simple image of outside—not necessarily an exit, but a horizon.
  - Hope is described as stability: breath, stance, patience.
  - Exit line: the narrator turns back—not to submit, but to continue with agency.
- **Handoff:** Act I’s first repair completes. The next module can tighten again without breaking the reader.
