# LEVEL 3 — ACT II ASSUMPTIONS REGISTRY

This registry lists explicit assumptions underlying Act II mechanics.
All assumptions must be visible, testable, and adjustable.

---

## A1 — Incentives Drive Aggregate Outcomes
**Used By:** M1, M8, M9  
**Assumption:** Individuals respond rationally to incentives over time.  
**Confidence:** High  
**Sensitivity:** Medium  
**Notes:** Does not require perfect rationality—directional response is sufficient.
