
# Act II · Chapter 2
## Rooms 31–35 — Mapping Power Without Panic

### Chapter Purpose
Help the reader begin identifying **where power actually sits** without triggering fear, blame, or conspiracy thinking.

This chapter introduces **directional clarity** while preserving psychological safety.

---

## Emotional Arc
**Start:** Stable confidence from Chapter 1  
**End:** Grounded alertness without alarm  

The reader should feel *more aware*, not more afraid.

---

## Psychological Contract
By Room 35, the reader agrees to:
- Look at power as structural, not personal
- Separate visibility from control
- Resist urgency and scapegoating
- Stay oriented rather than reactive

---

## Room Breakdown

### Room 031 — Power Is Directional
**Function:** Introduce power as flow, not possession  
**Psychological Shift:** Diffuse anxiety → Conceptual clarity  
**Must Avoid:** Naming actors, moralizing

---

### Room 032 — Visibility ≠ Control
**Function:** Break the illusion that visible figures run systems  
**Psychological Shift:** Suspicion → Precision  
**Must Avoid:** Cynicism, nihilism

---

### Room 033 — Incentives Shape Behavior
**Function:** Show how incentives quietly govern outcomes  
**Psychological Shift:** Confusion → Mechanistic understanding  
**Must Avoid:** Reductionism, oversimplification

---

### Room 034 — Where Decisions Actually Happen
**Function:** Gently relocate the reader’s attention  
**Psychological Shift:** Person-focused → Process-focused  
**Must Avoid:** Naming villains, triggering outrage

---

### Room 035 — Stabilization + Orientation Lock
**Function:** Regulate nervous system after power exposure  
**Psychological Shift:** Alertness → Composure  
**Must Avoid:** Call to action, alarmism

---

## Anti-Drift Rules
- No villains
- No conspiracies
- No named enemies
- No urgency framing
- No moral panic language

---

## Chapter Thesis (Pin This)
> This chapter teaches the reader how to see power clearly without becoming afraid of it.
