
# Act II · Chapter 3
## Rooms 36–40 — Incentives, Drift, and Legitimacy

### Chapter Purpose
Help the reader understand how **institutions drift away from their stated purpose** without anyone needing to betray their values.

This chapter explains *how legitimacy erodes* quietly—through incentives, shortcuts, and accumulated compromises.

---

## Emotional Arc
**Start:** Grounded alertness from Chapter 2  
**End:** Sober clarity without despair  

The reader should feel *clear-eyed*, not cynical.

---

## Psychological Contract
By Room 40, the reader agrees to:
- Distinguish intent from outcome
- See drift as structural, not moral
- Hold disappointment without collapsing into blame
- Stay oriented inside discomfort

---

## Room Breakdown

### Room 036 — Incentives Over Intent
**Function:** Show why good intentions fail inside bad incentive structures  
**Psychological Shift:** Moral confusion → Structural clarity

---

### Room 037 — Drift Is Gradual
**Function:** Explain how institutions move inch by inch  
**Psychological Shift:** Shock → Recognition

---

### Room 038 — Legitimacy Decays Quietly
**Function:** Introduce legitimacy as trust over time

---

### Room 039 — Maintenance vs Performance

---

### Room 040 — Stabilization + Moral Grounding
**Function:** Re-anchor reader after legitimacy exposure
