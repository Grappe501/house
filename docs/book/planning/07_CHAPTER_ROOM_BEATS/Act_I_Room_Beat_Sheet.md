# Act I Room Beat Sheet (Rooms 001–025)

**How to use:** Each room below includes:
- **Beat:** 1–2 sentences (what happens / what changes)
- **Mode:** Compression / Flow / Beam / Silence
- **Symbol:** the primary symbol used
- **Subplot:** which braid is touched
- **Door Line:** the last line that links deeper (Level 2/3)

> Note: These are intentionally “tight.” When drafting the room HTML, expand sensory detail first, then sharpen the final Beam/Silence line.

---

## Rooms 001–005 — Chapter 1: The Weight

### Room 001 — The Threshold
- **Beat:** The reader crosses into the house and immediately senses the load: a creak, a sag, a breath held too long. The narrator names the feeling without naming the cause.
- **Mode:** Flow
- **Symbol:** Threshold / Door
- **Subplot:** Neighbor Thread (absence)
- **Door Line:** “If you want to see what this ‘weight’ looks like over time, there’s a map on the next layer.”

### Room 002 — The Creak
- **Beat:** An ordinary movement triggers an outsized sound; the house answers like it’s been waiting. The reader recognizes their own life in that mismatch.
- **Mode:** Compression
- **Symbol:** Crack / Creak
- **Subplot:** Time Theft
- **Door Line:** “Listen long enough and you can hear where the stress travels.”

### Room 003 — The Calendar
- **Beat:** The week fills itself before the reader touches it; tasks replicate. The room ends with an image of time being eaten.
- **Mode:** Compression
- **Symbol:** Clock / Calendar
- **Subplot:** Time Theft
- **Door Line:** “The clock isn’t broken; it’s being used.”

### Room 004 — Load (First Beam)
- **Beat:** The narrator offers the first structural truth: this is not personal failure; it’s load without support. The line is clean, memorable, and short.
- **Mode:** Beam
- **Symbol:** Beam / Load Path
- **Subplot:** Red Thread
- **Door Line:** “A house doesn’t blame the floor for sagging when the beam is cracked.”

### Room 005 — Quiet
- **Beat:** A silence room that lets relief land: the reader exhales. No argument, just recognition.
- **Mode:** Silence
- **Symbol:** Breath / Light
- **Subplot:** Shame → Relief
- **Door Line:** “You’re not crazy.”
