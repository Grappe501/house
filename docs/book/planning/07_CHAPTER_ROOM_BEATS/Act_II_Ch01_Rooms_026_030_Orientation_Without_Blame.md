
# Act II · Chapter 1
## Rooms 26–30 — Orientation Without Blame

### Chapter Purpose
Teach the reader how to *look at systems* without triggering defensiveness, despair, or urgency.

This chapter trains **cognitive posture**, not ideology or action.

---

## Emotional Arc
**Start:** Calm curiosity after Act I  
**End:** Steady confidence in process and pace  

The reader should feel *safer* at the end than at the beginning.

---

## Psychological Contract
By Room 30, the reader implicitly agrees to:
- Stay curious longer
- Delay judgment
- Trust layered explanation
- Move without panic

---
