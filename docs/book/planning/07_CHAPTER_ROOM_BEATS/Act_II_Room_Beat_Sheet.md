# Act II Room Beat Sheet (v1)

**Purpose:** This document is the *single source of truth* for Act II execution (Rooms 26–50).  
It standardizes room meta to prevent drift: **mode**, **function**, **emotion**, **house element**, **symbol**, **door line**, **proof hooks**, and **word targets**.

**Act II structure:** 5 chapters × 5 rooms = 25 rooms (≈ 1 hour total).  
**Recommended read-time target:** 7,000–9,000 words total, with whitespace-aware pacing.

## Global standards

### Room Modes
- **Flow:** calm narrative movement; human, observational; carries the reader forward.
- **Compression:** tighter language; short lines; pressure; fewer clauses; urgency or constraint.
- **Silence:** sparse; whitespace is meaning; nervous system reset.

### Required fields (every room)
- **Mode**
- **Primary Function**
- **Primary Emotion / Secondary Emotion**
- **Reader Identity**
- **Stress/Growth Mode**
- **House Element**
- **Symbol Object**
- **Door Line (carry-forward question)**
- **Proof Hook (placeholder only; proof layer built later)**
- **Word Target**

### Motif palette (Act II)
Keep motifs **recurring** rather than inventing new ones every room.
- **Blueprint / Floorplan / Redlines**
- **Clock / Timer / Deadline slips**
- **Ledger / Receipt / Fine print**
- **Mirror / Reflection / Profile**
- **Default settings / Switches / Toggles**
- **Locks / Keys / Doors that “choose for you”**
- **Load-bearing wall / Stress cracks**
