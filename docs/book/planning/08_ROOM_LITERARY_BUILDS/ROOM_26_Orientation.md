# Room 26 — Orientation (Literary Build)

## Act / Module
Act II — Compliance  
Module 1: Normalization (Rooms 26–30)

---

## A. Room Function
Reset the reader’s threat detection.

Room 26 convinces the reader—briefly—that the worst is over.  
The system becomes legible, procedural, and calm.

---

## B. Psychological Target
**Cognitive relief**

The reader shifts from vigilance to comprehension.  
Suspicion softens into provisional trust.

---

## C. Emotional Curve
- Entry: Guarded tension  
- Exit: Cautious relief

No comfort. Just less pressure than expected.

---

## D. Core Symbols
- Orientation guide
- Clear signage
- Map / floor plan
- “You are here” marker
- Standardized language

Symbols feel neutral and useful, not symbolic.

---

## E. House Behavior
- Calm
- Polite
- Explicit

Tone: “Here’s how things work.”  
No manipulation yet—just clarity.

---

## F. Reader Behavior Shift
After this room, the reader:
- Stops scanning for traps
- Begins following paths
- Complies experimentally

---

## G. Timing & Geometry
- Medium-length room
- Open space
- Clear sightlines
- Efficient movement

Time feels organized.

---

## H. What Is Quietly Lost
Suspicion.

Not removed—just relaxed.

---

## I. Handoff to Room 27
Unresolved thought:

*“If they’re being this straightforward, maybe the danger was overstated.”*
