# Room 082 — The Offer
_Last updated: 2026-02-03_

**Load-bearing sentence (thesis):** A dignified offer leaves room for refusal without rupture.

## What the reader should feel
respected

## What the reader should understand
consent is maintained by allowing ‘no’ to be safe

## What the reader should do (without being told)
make space for choice; don’t convert people into instruments

## Word choices (preferred)
offer, choose, room, dignity, respect

## Avoid
shaming refusal
