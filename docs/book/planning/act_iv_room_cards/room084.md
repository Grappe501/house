# Room 084 — The New Default (BEAM)
_Last updated: 2026-02-03_

**Load-bearing sentence (thesis):** What used to be extraordinary becomes normal.

## What the reader should feel
stable; less vigilant

## What the reader should understand
durability is culture embedding, not constant alertness

## What the reader should do (without being told)
notice when practice becomes routine and protect it

## Word choices (preferred)
default, routine, normal, schedule, habit

## Avoid
victory talk
