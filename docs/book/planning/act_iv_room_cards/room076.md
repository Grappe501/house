# Room 076 — Knock
_Last updated: 2026-02-03_

**Load-bearing sentence (thesis):** Belonging begins with consent and presence, not force.

## What the reader should feel
welcomed, not recruited

## What the reader should understand
the ethical start is contact that respects the other person’s autonomy

## What the reader should do (without being told)
consider entry as invitation; action begins quietly

## Word choices (preferred)
knock, threshold, invited, offered, consent, return

## Avoid
conquest language; ‘take’ or ‘seize’ metaphors

## Handoff
to 077: legitimacy at eye level
