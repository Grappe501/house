# Room 099 — Walk Into the Lab
_Last updated: 2026-02-03_

**Load-bearing sentence (thesis):** Proof is care: show your work so trust can be rational.

## What the reader should feel
curious; steady

## What the reader should understand
claims can be tested without aggression

## What the reader should do (without being told)
seek evidence; use tools; keep humility

## Word choices (preferred)
lab, test, measure, verify, show work

## Avoid
gotcha tone

## Handoff
to 100: lights on, calm closure
