# Room 078 — One Small Ask
_Last updated: 2026-02-03_

**Load-bearing sentence (thesis):** Small asks sustain capacity; big asks consume it.

## What the reader should feel
capable; unpressured

## What the reader should understand
capacity is a constraint; respecting it increases durability

## What the reader should do (without being told)
choose one step that can be repeated

## Word choices (preferred)
one step, repeatable, light load, doable, return

## Avoid
loyalty tests; ‘prove’ language
