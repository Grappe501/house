# Global Narration Rules — Act I

Voice:
- Female
- Calm, steady, educated
- Neutral accent (broad American with subtle warmth)

Speed:
- Baseline: ~150 words per minute
- Compression rooms: 135–140 wpm
- Expansion rooms: 155–160 wpm
- Silence rooms: intentional pauses, no filler

Tone:
- Observational, not persuasive
- Warm but not sentimental
- No urgency spikes

Breathing:
- Natural inhalation at paragraph breaks
- Extended pauses marked explicitly
