### Room 004 — The Pause
**Function:** Internal Pressure (turn inward)  
**Verdict:** 🟡 Approved with a small wording risk

**Strengths**
- Pressure is primarily internal: self-awareness of feet/wall/floor
- House neutrality is maintained (“doesn’t respond”)

**Primary risk**
- The phrase **“as if the space requires it”** slightly externalizes pressure (suggests the space is demanding behavior).
  - This is small, but Room 4 is where Act II leak risks begin.
  - The pressure must remain *self-generated*.

**Suggested corrective direction (no full rewrite yet)**
- Reframe “requires” into *self-perception* language:
  - “as if you’ve decided it does”  
  - “as though you’d rather not disturb it”  
  - “as if you’re trying to be considerate without knowing why”

Goal: preserve inward pressure without implying the house is imposing rules.
