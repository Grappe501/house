# LEVEL 3 — ACT IV MECHANICS REGISTRY (Maintenance)

---

## MIV1 — Drift Detection & Alarm Thresholds
**Source Rooms:** 079, 089  
**Description:** Metrics act as early alarms before failure becomes crisis.  
**Enforcement:** Public thresholds, automatic review triggers.  
**Measurement:** Drift visibility score; time-to-correction.

---

## MIV2 — Scheduled Review Cycles (Calendar Governance)
**Source Rooms:** 076, 077  
**Description:** Governance runs on predictable cadence, not scandal-driven urgency.  
**Enforcement:** Required review windows; published agendas.  
**Measurement:** Review completion rate; backlog size.

---

## MIV3 — Audit Trails & Patch Notes
**Source Rooms:** 078, 093, 088  
**Description:** Decisions leave a readable trail; changes are documented like software releases.  
**Enforcement:** Mandatory changelogs; public diff summaries.  
**Measurement:** Audit completeness; reversal costs reduced.

---

## MIV4 — Stewardship Rotation (Anti-Capture by Turnover)
**Source Rooms:** 081, 082  
**Description:** Roles rotate to reduce gatekeeping and personal empires.  
**Enforcement:** Term limits for stewardship roles; random audits.  
**Measurement:** Concentration index of authority.

---

## MIV5 — Training Pipelines (People as Infrastructure)
**Source Rooms:** 083, 096, 097, 098  
**Description:** Skills and norms are taught deliberately; new maintainers are produced continuously.  
**Enforcement:** Certification paths; mentorship requirements.  
**Measurement:** Pipeline throughput; coverage rate.

---

## MIV6 — Incident Response Playbooks (When Metrics Flash Red)
**Source Rooms:** 089, 090  
**Description:** Pre-agreed response steps prevent panic and capture during crises.  
**Enforcement:** Published playbooks; drills.  
**Measurement:** Response time; outcome stabilization rate.

---

## MIV7 — Trust Dividend Accumulation
**Source Rooms:** 094, 095  
**Description:** Maintenance reduces fear and increases participation over time.  
**Enforcement:** Transparency + consistency.  
**Measurement:** Participation elasticity; volatility reduction.
