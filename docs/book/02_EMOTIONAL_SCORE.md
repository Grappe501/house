# Emotional Scoring & Pacing (v1)

The book follows a musical score, not a linear argument.

## Four Acts
- Act I (Pages 1–25): Recognition / Safety
- Act II (Pages 26–50): Understanding / Clarity
- Act III (Pages 51–75): Reconstruction / Grounded Hope
- Act IV (Pages 76–100): Integration / Agency

## 5-Page Breathing Cycle
Within each Act, pages repeat this pattern:
1. Compression
2. Flow
3. Flow
4. Expansion (Beam)
5. Compression or Silence

## Emotional Rules
- No sustained urgency
- No emotional spikes without recovery
- Hope must feel earned

## Reader State Goal
Readers should feel:
- Seen before taught
- Safe before challenged
- Capable before asked to act
