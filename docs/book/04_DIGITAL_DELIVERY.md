# Digital Delivery Philosophy (v1)

The digital experience is not an ebook.
It is a guided environment.

## Primary Device
- iPad-first
- Touch-native
- Landscape and portrait supported

## Modes
Each Room supports:
- Read
- Listen
- Watch (guided reading)

## Guided Reading
- Text appears at narration pace
- Key phrases may subtly animate
- Silence is preserved

## Navigation
- Swipe / page turn
- House Map (floor plan)
- Room bookmarking

The reader is inside the House.
