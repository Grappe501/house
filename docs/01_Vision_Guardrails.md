# Vision & Guardrails (Non‑Negotiables)

## The mission
Build a **worker-first American future** that strengthens democracy, preserves markets, decentralizes domestic power, and raises the quality of life for the broad working majority.

## Who this is for
The **90%** — people who work, run small/medium businesses, raise families, and build communities.

## The core promise
A system that:
- reduces stress and instability
- makes opportunity real (education + health)
- returns power to community and geography
- prevents wealth from becoming political control
- stays pragmatic: transition **without** a constitutional convention

## Golden circle rule
Every concept must be explainable as:
- **WHY** this matters to working life
- **HOW** the system changes incentives and structures
- **WHAT** the mechanism is (institution/policy/tool)

## The book vs the website rule (critical)
### The book
- ideology-neutral language (no “left/right” labels)
- lived-experience first
- simple, short, persuasive (100 pages / 3–4 hours)
- focuses on mental model, not dense policy

### The website
- deep dive library + interactive lab
- teaches political ideologies historically (education, not identity)
- includes “How the world actually works” economic mechanics section (opt-in depth)
- hosts scenario/pillar/QoL models with transparent assumptions

## Ethics & integrity rules
- No ads
- No donor capture
- No paywalling core truth (paywall depth/tools/training)
- Radical transparency in modeling assumptions
- Systems over personalities
- Accountability above ideology

## Audience rules (cross-spectrum)
- Avoid culture-war framing
- Use neutral, truth-based language
- Frame conflicts as **structures + incentives**, not tribes
- Respect faith without conceding state power (separation is enforced by structure)

## The success thresholds (anchor)
This system is designed to *improve life* for:
- Individuals earning **≤ $250,000/year**
- Businesses **≤ $20M/year** revenue

The goal is not to punish success — it is to prevent success from becoming control.

## “Global power” rule
In the book: only a **glimpse** (structural truth, no conspiracy framing).  
On the website: a full evidence-first deep dive (optional, advanced).

## What must never happen
- Drift into partisan branding
- Drift into demonization language
- Drift into “utopian” promises without transition plans
- Drift into models that hide assumptions or imply certainty

## The north star measure
If this doesn’t raise *everyday stability*, it fails.
