Act II · Room 030

Margin

Paperwork is what the system looks like when it wants to feel harmless.

It doesn’t threaten you. It doesn’t argue with you. It simply asks you to comply—again, and again, and again—until compliance feels like the only language left.

So before we move on, take a small piece of margin back.

Notice your hands.
Notice your breath.
Notice how quickly the mind tries to turn everything into tasks.

Margin isn’t laziness. It’s capacity. It’s the difference between reacting and choosing. Between surviving a process and understanding it.

This act is going to show you the machinery without turning you into a machine.

When you’re ready, we keep going—one room at a time, with enough space to stay human.
