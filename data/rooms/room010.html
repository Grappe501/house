Act I · Room 010

Silence Reset

Nothing is being asked of you here.

No decision. No conclusion. No response.

This room exists to let the nervous system catch up to the story. To slow the pace enough that the body remembers it’s allowed to be present without performing.

If you feel the urge to move on quickly, notice that too. That reflex is part of what the house is designed to interrupt.

When you’re ready, the next room will still be there.
