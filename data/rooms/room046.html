Contradictions

Contradictions.

You begin to notice the inconsistencies.

What you are told does not always match what you experience. The explanation sounds reasonable, but the outcome feels familiar in a different way.

You are encouraged to trust the process. You are also reminded to take responsibility for your results. Both statements exist comfortably side by side.

The system emphasizes fairness. It also rewards those who already know how to navigate it. These facts are never presented as conflicting.

You hold them both.

When something works, it is evidence that the structure is sound. When it does not, the reason is described as individual variance.

You replay moments, looking for clarity. Did you misunderstand? Did you expect too much? Or is the explanation incomplete?

The questions do not lead anywhere obvious. Each answer points back to another part of the system.

You are not asked to resolve the contradictions. Only to accommodate them.

Over time, this becomes a skill. You learn how to accept opposing truths without naming the tension between them.

The discomfort dulls. The logic holds, even when it does not fully align.

And because the contradictions are never acknowledged directly, they settle quietly into the background, shaping how much certainty you allow yourself to expect.
