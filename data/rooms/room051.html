Act III — Reconstruction (REBUILD) • Room 051

The Tool Belt

Buildability before belief.

The Builder doesn’t arrive with a speech. They arrive with a belt—worn leather, honest weight—tools that can’t pretend.

A square for corners. A level for truth. A pencil for plans you can change. The kind of kit that assumes mistakes will be made, and corrected.

‘If you want this to last,’ the Builder says, ‘we start with what the house can hold.’ Not what we wish it could hold. Not what sounds good. What it can hold.

That is the first mercy of reconstruction: constraints do not kill hope. They keep hope from collapsing.
