The Ledger

Open books, open trust.

The ledger is not a weapon. It’s an invitation. The Builder opens it in the middle of the room where anyone can see.

Costs. Savings. Time. Risk. What will be gained quickly, what will take years, what might fail if we don’t protect it.

The old way hid the bill until you couldn’t refuse it. The new way writes the bill first—so you can judge it.

People don’t need to be persuaded when the accounting is honest. They need to be respected.
