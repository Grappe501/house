Act I · Room 005

Breath

Nothing is asked of you here.

No explanation.
No lesson.
No conclusion to reach.

Just a pause.

The body notices before the mind does.

The shoulders drop a fraction.
The jaw loosens.
The breath comes in a little deeper than it has all day.

This is not wasted space.

It’s the space that lets everything else settle.

Most systems don’t leave room for this.
They move on quickly.
They stack one demand on top of the next and call it efficiency.

But without moments like this, weight doesn’t disappear.
It accumulates.

So we stop here.

Long enough to feel the floor under your feet.
Long enough to remember that nothing is chasing you right now.
Long enough to let the quiet do its work.

When you’re ready, we’ll keep walking.

There’s no rush.
