Room 5 — The Carry

You leave with something small.

The hallway opens back into a space that feels less defined.

There’s no clear marker that you’ve reached the end of anything. The light shifts slightly, not brighter or darker, just different enough to notice.

You take in one small detail—a scuff along the baseboard, the place where the paint has thinned from being brushed past so many times.

It doesn’t stand out. You would have missed it before.

You don’t stop to look at it for long.

Still, as you move on, the image stays with you longer than you expect.

Nothing about it asks to be remembered.

And yet, you remember it.
