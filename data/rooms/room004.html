Quiet Cost

It’s not one big disaster.

It’s smaller than that.
Quieter.

A fee you didn’t expect.
A bill that arrives wrong—and stays wrong.
A repair you delay because the timing never makes sense.

It’s the extra shift you pick up.
The weekend you lose.
The phone call you don’t make because you can’t afford what it might lead to.

It’s the way your shoulders rise without asking.
The way you check the account one more time.
The way you do the math again, like the numbers might change if you stare hard enough.

And then someone tells you it’s just life.

Sometimes that’s the sentence that makes the crack visible.
