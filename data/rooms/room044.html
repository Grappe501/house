The Performance

The Performance.

At some point, you stop responding naturally.

You learn what works. Which phrases are received well. Which concerns sound reasonable when framed the right way.

Before speaking, you imagine how it will land. You adjust your tone. You smooth the edges before they are visible.

This is not deception. It feels closer to translation— finding the version of yourself that fits the room.

You notice the shift when effort moves inward. Less energy goes into solving the problem. More goes into presenting yourself as someone worth listening to.

Your posture changes. Your timing improves. You anticipate reactions before they occur.

There is relief in this. Predictability lowers risk. The better the performance, the fewer interruptions.

Still, something narrows.

Questions become statements. Curiosity becomes caution. You speak less about what you feel and more about what can be received.

The system responds positively. Feedback improves. Doors open more smoothly.

And because the performance is rewarded, it becomes harder to remember which parts were added and which were quietly set aside.
