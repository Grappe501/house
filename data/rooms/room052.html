Constraints Are Not Enemies

If you can’t name the trade, you’re not choosing.

The house taught you this already: ignore limits long enough and the bill arrives as a crack.

The Builder lays out constraints like a map. Not to scare you. To stop the future from being built on hidden costs.

Every ‘yes’ has a ‘not yet’ inside it. Every ‘free’ has a price somewhere. Reconstruction means we write those prices down—out loud—before they write themselves into our lives.

Once constraints are visible, arguments change. People stop fighting over slogans and start choosing between real options.
