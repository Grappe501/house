Design goals

Democracy that matches people, not maps

  • President chosen by national popular vote with a majority winner (RCV or two-round).
  • House elected via multi-member districts + ranked-choice voting to reduce gerrymandering and “wasted votes.”
  • Voting is a right with national baseline standards: registration, access, and anti-suppression enforcement.
  • Party power reduced by election mechanics (open primary + RCV general) rather than party bans.
Promise: political equality cannot depend on money or geography.
