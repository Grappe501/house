WHY

Work pathways must be real, local, and paid

Not everyone wants a four-year degree. The new system treats trades, apprenticeships, and technical credentials as first-class pathways.

Rule: if society needs the work, society helps people train for it.
HOW

Apprenticeship and credential pipeline

  • Earn-while-you-learn apprenticeships partnered with employers.
  • Public project pipelines (infrastructure, energy transition, housing build).
  • Portable credentials and standardized training quality control.
  • Career transitions funded as a national resilience strategy (AI disruption buffer).
WHAT

Local economic alignment

  • Regional skills councils identify needs (healthcare, construction, manufacturing, IT).
  • Training supply matches local job demand.
  • Small business incentives to hire apprentices.
