Why this matters

Distributions beat averages

Medians are helpful, but they hide the pain. The working majority feels policy through distributions: how many households are rent-burdened, how many workers commute 60+ minutes, etc.

Rule: every distribution we use must be visible, explainable, and tied to an ACS table.
Housing insecurity proxy

Rent burden (ACS table B25070)

We pull the ACS distribution of gross rent as % of household income.

  • % rent-burdened (30%+) — common threshold used in housing research
  • % severely rent-burdened (50%+)

Schema: observed_baseline.distribution_tables.rent_burden_b25070

Mobility & time proxy

Commute-time distribution (ACS table B08303)

We pull the ACS distribution of travel time to work.

  • % commuting over 30 minutes
  • % commuting over 60 minutes

How this plugs into QoL

Turning distributions into scores

In V54, two QoL dimensions can now be driven by observed data when available:

  • Housing Security — rent burden proxies
  • Mobility & Time — commute time proxies

Next, we’ll add more distributions (debt, healthcare burden, childcare, food insecurity proxies).
