Universal Broadband

Home / The Model / Universal Broadband
Philosophy

Universal high-speed is economic citizenship infrastructure

Universal broadband is education, jobs, healthcare access, entrepreneurship, and civic participation. “Available” doesn’t count if it’s priced out of reach.

Model

Universal service standard + affordability floor

  • Every home has access to a modern high-speed standard (benchmark updated over time).
  • Affordability is part of access; price transparency and baseline plans are required.
  • Where markets fail, the public utility layer builds last-mile infrastructure or partners with co-ops/municipals.
  • Open access and competition where feasible; anti-monopoly enforcement where not.
Outcome

Leveling the playing field

  • Rural and low-income communities gain equal access to education and remote work markets.
  • Small businesses can compete on equal footing.
  • Telehealth and public service access improve while costs fall.
