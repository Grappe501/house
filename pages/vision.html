North Star

What the future feels like

People can work, raise families, start businesses, and participate in democracy without being trapped by medical debt, student debt, predatory rent, or political systems dominated by money.

Two futures

Trajectory A

Status quo

  • Rising concentration of wealth and power
  • Higher household precarity (medical, housing, education costs)
  • More political capture and distrust
  • Pressure toward punitive systems instead of preventive systems
Trajectory B

Working Majority Republic

  • Markets remain—extraction paths are blocked
  • Education and healthcare are rights
  • Democracy is insulated from money and party capture
  • Local communities regain power over safety and development
