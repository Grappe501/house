Impact Lab

Overview

Housing stability is protected by universal tenant rights and anti-extraction rules, while homeownership rises through first-time buyer pathways, fair rent-to-own conversions, and tenant right of first refusal.

Template: current system → new system change → mechanism → risks/safeguards → metrics → projections (10/25 years).
Tenant baseline

Universal landlord/tenant rules

  • Habitability and repair timelines; fee and junk-charge limits; lease transparency.
  • Anti-retaliation enforcement and eviction fairness.
Ownership

Build homeownership into the system

  • First-time buyer support; standardized fair mortgages; credit alternatives.
  • Rent-to-own standards; community land trust options; tenant right of first refusal.
  • De-incentivize property hoarding: progressive unit taxes, vacancy penalties, institutional caps.
