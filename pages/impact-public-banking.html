Impact Lab

Overview

The Public Banking Option reduces household precarity, increases local investment, and weakens the political power of financial concentration by shrinking the extraction stream (fees + predatory interest) and expanding fair capital access.

Key dynamic: stability raises productivity. Productivity grows GDP. Reduced extraction lets gains stay local.
Current system

What we see today

  • Millions are unbanked/underbanked; fee structures punish instability.
  • Small-dollar credit is dominated by high-cost lenders and overdraft reliance.
  • Small business capital access varies wildly by region and race/class.
  • Financial concentration increases systemic risk and political capture.
New system

What changes

  • Universal access to safe accounts and fair payments.
  • Emergency credit replaces payday and overdraft dependency.
  • Guarantees and wholesale funding expand local business lending.
  • Extraction revenue shrinks; productive investment share rises.
Metrics & projections

What we measure

  • Unbanked/underbanked rate; average household fee burden.
  • Payday/overdraft usage decline; emergency credit uptake and repayment health.
  • Small business lending volume by region; new business formation rates.
  • Collections and credit-score recovery; eviction and bankruptcy correlations.
  • Market concentration indices and systemic risk indicators.
