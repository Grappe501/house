AI Wealth & Anti-Monopoly Controls

Home / The Model / AI & Automation / Wealth Controls
WHY

AI creates super-wealth unless we stop it

AI concentrates value in whoever owns compute, data, distribution platforms, and IP. Without rules, the next billionaire class will be “AI landlords.”

Rule: productivity gains must benefit the whole — not a handful of owners.
HOW

Anti-capture design

  • Compute & platform anti-trust: break monopolies and prevent vertical lock-in.
  • Data rights: individuals own their data; collective negotiation sets baseline prices for data use.
  • AI productivity levy: a transparent share of AI-driven gains funds the People’s Wealth Fund.
  • Local reinvestment: wealth fund payouts + local procurement keep value circulating locally.
WHAT

What happens to the billionaire class

  • Extreme wealth concentration is capped by design (limits on income/ownership capture).
  • Existing fortunes transition toward long-term stable returns rather than capture-based growth.
  • Markets remain — but extraction is reduced and competition restored.
