Interpretation

How to read low/base/high

  • Low = slower capability and lower automation pressure
  • High = faster capability and higher automation pressure
  • Under WMR, stability increases across bands because gains are shared and transitions are funded.
  • Under status quo, volatility increases as pressure rises.
