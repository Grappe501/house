Maintenance Governance Model

Last updated: 2026-02-01. Act IV Beam anchor: Room 084 (The New Default).

What this is

A governance operating system designed to survive success: it replaces crisis-driven politics with scheduled inspection, public logs, distributed stewardship, and predictable correction.

Maintenance is not “admin.” Maintenance is the power to keep the system working.

Core parts

  • Calendar governance: reviews happen on schedule, not only after scandal.
  • Drift alarms: thresholds trigger review automatically.
  • Patch workflow: every change is logged and explained.
  • Stewardship rotation: keys circulate; gatekeeping is limited by design.
  • Training pipeline: maintainers are produced continuously.

What changes for people

  • Fewer “surprise” failures (drift caught early).
  • Lower enforcement costs (trust and compliance increase).
  • More legibility (people can see the rules and the updates).
  • Less fear (stability grows; volatility falls).

Minimum cadence (v1)

  • Weekly: drift scan (metrics check) + publish brief status note.
  • Monthly: maintenance review (top 3 drifts + actions) + patch notes.
  • Quarterly: deep audit walkthrough + stewardship rotation check.
  • Annually: “shared manual” refresh + training certification cycle.
