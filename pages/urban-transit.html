WHY

Urban mobility is a cost-of-living lever

Congestion is a time tax and a stress tax. Efficient transit reduces household costs, improves health, and increases productivity.

HOW

Transit that wins (scaffold)

  • Frequency: if you wait, you lose — prioritize frequent service.
  • Reliability: dedicated lanes where needed; signal priority; safe stops.
  • Integration: last-mile micromobility; safe walking routes; unified payment.
  • Land use alignment: encourage housing near nodes without displacement.
WHAT

Projected outcomes (scaffold)

  • Lower commute times and more family time
  • Lower household transportation spending
  • Less air pollution and fewer traffic deaths
  • More access to work and education
