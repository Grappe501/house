Pragmatic Roadmap

This roadmap assumes a democratic mandate and uses statutes first, then targeted Article V amendments to lock in the new rules.

Phase 1

Deliver results fast (statutes + executive agenda)

  • Voting access baseline; election transparency; anti-gerrymander incentives
  • Public financing pilots; disclosure and anti-corruption enforcement
  • Begin universal healthcare pathway; tuition-free public education pathway
  • Labor power reforms (organize/bargain protections)
  • Antitrust enforcement surge; start People’s Wealth Fund formation
  • Impact Lab dashboards: public accounting of outcomes
Phase 2

Lock the system in (targeted amendments)

  • Popular vote presidency
  • Right to vote baseline
  • Public elections authority + anti-dark money
  • Court term limits + enforceable ethics
  • Majority-rule legislative process (no supermajority traps)
  • Economic citizenship rights (education, healthcare, organize)
  • Digital civil rights (data ownership, anti-coercion)
Phase 3

Consolidation and scaling

  • Scale People’s Wealth Fund dividend and retirement reserve
  • Standardize training and local implementation systems
  • Expand local prosperity engine (capital access, small business growth)
  • Institutionalize community safety model and reentry pipelines
  • Continuous improvement through metrics and public oversight

Execution cadence

First 100 days / first year / years 2–3

First 100 days

  • Pass a Democracy Protection package: access baseline, transparency, anti-gerrymander rules.
  • Stand up anti-capture enforcement: independent inspectors general, procurement integrity rules.
  • Launch Universal Broadband build standard and affordability floor (treat as infrastructure).
  • Create the People’s Wealth Fund framework (legal charter + governance + anti-capture guardrails).
  • Begin student-debt abolition process (federal cancellation) and design mortgage conversion authority.
  • Establish AI governance: risk tiers, frontier testing requirements, and algorithmic due process for high-stakes domains.

First year

  • Universal school meals + food baseline simplification and auto-enrollment.
  • Universal tenant baseline law + junk-fee restrictions + eviction fairness.
  • Labor power reforms (organize/bargain protections; portable benefits scaffolding).
  • Antitrust surge: merger moratorium in highly concentrated sectors; begin orderly breakups where necessary.
  • Energy reliability plan: grid hardening, rate transparency, investment mandates.

Years 2–3

  • Public banking option (fair accounts + small loans) to end predatory finance.
  • Scale People’s Wealth Fund dividend and begin retirement reserve growth.
  • Mortgage conversion rollout with swap instruments; targeted principal reduction where justified.
  • Article V amendment campaign bundle to lock core rules (popular vote, voting right, court ethics/terms, digital rights, money limits).
How we avoid “collapse” while transforming systems

Swap instruments and public options

Major transitions (student debt, mortgages, finance) follow one rule: convert and swap rather than explode. We remove extraction by offering stable public options and exchanging legacy assets for predictable instruments, preventing runs and panic.

  • Student debt: cancel federal balances; convert private loans through fair-value pathways.
  • Mortgages: public refinance option + asset swaps + targeted principal reduction.
  • Finance transition: create a safe stability asset and make productive investment more profitable than extraction.
