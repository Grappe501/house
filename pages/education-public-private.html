Education: Public First, Private Allowed

Home / The Model / Education
Principle

Public education is the common floor

Education is always free in this system. Public education is not a “backup”—it is the civic foundation. Private education can exist, but it cannot hollow out the public system or create a hereditary class society.

Reconcile private education

Allowed, but with public obligations

  • Non-displacement rule: private schools cannot receive public subsidies that starve public schools.
  • Transparency: if any public funds touch a private institution, finances and outcomes must be open.
  • Access rules: anti-discrimination and disability accommodations enforced.
  • Common civic curriculum: basic civic education standards apply for democracy stability.
Core trade: choice is allowed, extraction is not.
Incentivize public systems

Make public schools the best option

  • Quality guarantee: universal funding floor and teacher support.
  • Wraparound services: counseling, meals, health supports, after-school programs.
  • Modern facilities: broadband, labs, trades training, arts.
  • Pathways: apprenticeships and credential routes tied to local economies.

People choose public systems when they are excellent, trustworthy, and welcoming.

Governance

Local control with rights floors

  • Local school governance aligned with Decentralized Governance pillar.
  • National standards limited to rights floors, transparency, and baseline competency metrics.
