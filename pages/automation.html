Philosophy

Automation should liberate people, not discard them

Technology should increase freedom. But when productivity gains are captured by owners alone, automation becomes a threat. This system ensures automation increases national prosperity and that prosperity is shared.

We do not fight technology. We fight extraction of productivity gains.

Mechanics

The Productivity Sharing Stack

  • People’s Wealth Fund dividend rises with national productivity.
  • Sector transition compacts require employers benefiting from automation to fund worker transitions.
  • Paid retraining and credential pathways through free education.
  • Portable benefits and wage insurance in defined transitions.
Rule: If automation increases output, households should see higher stability—not higher precarity.
Governance

AI deployment in the workplace

  • Workers have rights to know when algorithmic systems evaluate performance.
  • High-stakes employment decisions require explanation and human review.
  • Ban coercive surveillance in private spaces and off-duty behavioral tracking.
Entrepreneurship

How automation expands small business capacity

  • Public broadband + training makes AI tools available to small firms.
  • Public banking expands fair credit for modernization and productivity upgrades.
  • Procurement preferences for SMEs using productivity tools to raise wages and output.
Transition plan

Early actions

  • National workforce transition dashboards by sector and region.
  • Launch paid training pathways in high-demand sectors (health, energy, infrastructure, skilled trades).
  • Establish wage insurance and portable benefit rails through the public option systems.
