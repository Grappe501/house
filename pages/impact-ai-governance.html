Impact Lab

Overview

We regulate AI by risk tiers and mandatory testing while expanding compute, talent, and standards leadership so the U.S. remains globally competitive.

Current system

What we see today

  • AI deployed in high-stakes domains without due process
  • Opaque data extraction and surveillance incentives
  • Race-to-market pressures overwhelm safety
New system

What changes

  • Risk-tier framework; prohibited uses; high-risk requirements
  • Frontier model safety testing and auditing
  • Algorithmic due process in high-stakes decisions
  • Competitiveness investments: talent, compute, diffusion to SMEs
Metrics & projections

What we will measure

  • Incidents in high-risk AI, bias/appeal outcomes, transparency compliance
  • AI adoption by small businesses, productivity measures, workforce transitions
  • International standards adoption and allied interoperability
