Golden Circle

WHY: health is freedom

In the Working Majority Republic, health is treated as a public freedom — like roads or clean water. When health is hostage to employment, debt, or geography, people become easier to exploit.

Promise: healthcare is always free at point of use, and mental health is treated as core infrastructure.
Golden Circle

HOW: universal access + prevention + community capacity

  • Universal coverage that removes premiums, deductibles, and medical debt.
  • Prevention-first public health: nutrition, exercise access, screening, maternal health.
  • Mental health everywhere: integrated care, crisis response that isn’t jail, and trauma-informed services.
  • Stress reduction policy: time security, housing stability, work predictability, and debt relief.
  • Rural parity: clinics, telehealth, mobile care units, and transport access.
