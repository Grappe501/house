What this is

Surplus Allocation Projection (line-item driven)

This page projects what happens after WMR reaches primary surplus:

  • How big the surplus is each year (from line items),
  • How it is allocated by rule (debt paydown / People’s Wealth Fund / reinvestment),
  • And how that accelerates debt reduction.
Unbiased rule: allocations are published and the math is visible. Change requires public amendment to rules.

How it ties together

End-to-end chain

  1. Budget Line Items phase in and improve the primary balance.
  2. When primary balance turns surplus, Surplus Allocation Rules activate.
  3. Debt paydown becomes mechanical; People’s Wealth Fund grows; reinvestment continues.
