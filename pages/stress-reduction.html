WHY

Stress is the silent tax

Chronic stress drives disease, family breakdown, addiction, violence, and burnout. A pro-worker republic must reduce stress structurally — not just tell people to “cope better.”

HOW

Stress reduction levers (policy, not slogans)

  • Time security: predictable schedules, paid leave, and stable childcare.
  • Debt relief: retire medical debt, abolish student debt, reduce predatory lending.
  • Housing stability: anti-hoarding rules + ownership pathways.
  • Transport access: reduce commute burdens and isolation.
  • Community safety: legitimacy and prevention reduce fear burden.
WHAT

What life feels like

  • Fewer crisis months (medical bills, eviction risk, surprise fees).
  • More predictable planning for families and small businesses.
  • Lower burnout and higher civic capacity.
