Impact Lab

Overview

Retirement remains a guaranteed right. Social Security stays as the floor and is strengthened; the People’s Wealth Fund includes a protected retirement reserve that supplements the floor over time.

Template: current system → new system change → mechanism → risks/safeguards → metrics → projections (10/25 years).
Design

What changes

  • Social Security is preserved and strengthened for long-term solvency.
  • Credit caregiving and socially necessary labor.
  • Earlier full-benefit retirement options for physically demanding jobs (occupational equity).
  • Retirement becomes a flexible phase (gradual reduction, mentoring, part-time options) without punishment.
