Impact Lab

Overview

Decentralized governance increases legitimacy, responsiveness, and resilience by aligning authority with lived experience.

Key outcome: fewer culture wars, more problem solving.
Current system

What we see today

  • Distant decision‑making disconnected from local reality.
  • National culture war battles over local issues.
  • Low trust and disengagement from civic life.
New system

What changes

  • Local charters govern daily life within constitutional limits.
  • National government focuses on coordination, defense, and standards.
  • Policy emerges from lived needs rather than ideological abstraction.
Metrics & projections

What we measure

  • Civic participation rates at local levels.
  • Public trust and satisfaction surveys.
  • Policy compliance and enforcement legitimacy.
  • Economic retention: % of wealth circulating locally.
