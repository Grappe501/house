Non-negotiable

Medical debt must be abolished

Medical debt is punishment for getting sick in a rationed system. When healthcare is universal, medical debt becomes illegitimate legacy damage.

Rule: no medical debt in a working-majority republic—past or future.
What happens to existing medical debt

A one-time medical debt reset

  • All medical debt is retired: hospital bills, collections, judgments tied to medical bills.
  • Credit repair: credit reports are automatically corrected with no lasting penalty.
  • No tax liability: debt retirement is not taxable income.
  • No means testing: universal reset avoids stigma and bureaucratic gatekeeping.
How it’s implemented

Clean rules, fast execution

  • Immediate: stop collections, lawsuits, wage garnishments, and interest accumulation.
  • Public settlement rails: where needed, debts are purchased/settled at market discount and extinguished.
  • Provider stabilization: hospitals receive transition support so safety-net systems do not collapse.
  • Billing reform: universal payment rails eliminate surprise billing and patient billing for covered care.
Design: remove the debt, then remove the machinery that creates it.
Why it improves the economy

Debt abolition is growth policy

  • Households regain credit capacity for housing, vehicles, and entrepreneurship.
  • Stress and mental health burdens drop.
  • Earlier care reduces emergency utilization and catastrophic costs.
  • Local economies stabilize as families are not pushed into bankruptcy cycles.
Preventing recurrence

Universal healthcare ends the pipeline

  • No premiums/deductibles for covered care.
  • Clear coverage rules and appeal rights.
  • Fraud enforcement targets providers and corporations—not patients.
