Impact Lab

Overview

Defense is redesigned to protect people first: readiness and deterrence per dollar, reduced procurement waste, and service incentives that build stable lives rather than exploiting desperation.

Template: current system → new system change → mechanism → risks/safeguards → metrics → projections (10/25 years).
Current system

What we see today

  • High spending with uneven outcomes and procurement inefficiency.
  • Recruitment and retention strain when service is treated as a poverty-escape path.
  • Permanent war incentives distort budgets and accountability.
New system

What changes

  • Outcomes-per-dollar dashboard: readiness, audits, maintenance, housing quality, retention.
  • Service incentives based on honor + skills + lifetime stability (service dividend, skill pipelines).
  • Procurement reform and anti-consolidation protections.
  • Invest in domestic resilience (cyber, disaster readiness) as national security.
