Life systems

  • Healthcare system design (universal, free at point of service)
  • Education system design (pre-K to career)
  • Childcare and family stability
  • Mental health and addiction as public health
  • Disability rights and dignity
  • Environmental resilience and disaster readiness

Democracy & governance

  • Election design (RCV, multi-member districts, popular vote)

Safety & justice

  • Restorative justice pathways (placeholder)

Rights in the digital age

  • Personal data ownership
  • Freedom from data coercion and retaliation
  • Collective data negotiation
  • Surveillance limits: private spaces inviolable

  • Children’s data protection and digital legacy rights

Housing & food

  • Universal landlord/tenant baseline
  • Anti-hoarding property rules and institutional ownership caps
  • Homeownership ladder (rent-to-own, first-time buyer pathway)
  • Mortgage conversion and principal reduction (phased)
  • Food baseline + availability standard + food hubs
  • Local producer stability and anti-monopoly in food chains
