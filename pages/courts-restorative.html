Courts, Diversion & Restorative Justice

Home / The Model / Justice & Safety / Courts & Restorative
WHY

Accountability without permanent ruin

The status quo turns many nonviolent mistakes into lifetime instability. That instability creates more harm.

Rule: punish what must be punished; repair what can be repaired; prevent what can be prevented.
HOW

Diversion pipeline

  • Problem-solving courts (mental health, addiction, veterans, youth).
  • Pre-trial diversion for eligible nonviolent cases.
  • Restitution + service plans that restore victims and communities.
  • Graduated sanctions instead of all-or-nothing collapse.
WHAT

Restorative justice (bounded)

  • Victim consent required.
  • Structured processes with trained facilitators.
  • Measured completion and follow-up.
  • Not a substitute for serious violent offenses.
Metrics

What we track

  • Recidivism rates for diversion vs incarceration paths
  • Victim satisfaction (when restorative is used)
  • Time-to-resolution and cost per case
  • Long-term stability outcomes (employment, housing)
