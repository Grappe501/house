Impact Lab

Overview

Resilience reduces long-run costs by preventing cascading failures: outages, displacement, health crises, business collapse, and food disruption. It also increases national security and stability.

Current system

What we see today

  • Slow recovery, opaque contracting, and unequal rebuilding outcomes.
  • Infrastructure fragility and repeated failures.
  • Displacement after disasters, especially for renters and low-income homeowners.
New system

What changes

  • Resilience hubs and hardened infrastructure reduce outage and mortality risk.
  • Transparent contracting and anti-profiteering enforcement.
  • Right-to-return and housing stability mechanisms prevent displacement spirals.
Metrics & projections

What we measure

  • Outage frequency/duration; recovery time to baseline services.
  • Disaster mortality and health outcomes; heat/cold illness metrics.
  • Displacement and housing stability post-event; business reopening rates.
  • Cost avoided through prevention vs recovery spending.
