Medical Debt Elimination

Home / The Model / Medical Debt Elimination
Philosophy

Medical debt is policy violence

Medical debt is not a “bad luck” problem—it is a structural failure. When a nation allows illness to become bankruptcy, the economy becomes less productive and democracy becomes less legitimate.

Because this model makes healthcare free at the point of service going forward, legacy medical debt becomes a stranded transition cost that must be removed.

Immediate actions

End medical debt harm first

  • Ban medical debt credit reporting and medical collections that threaten housing/employment.
  • Stop interest and fees on existing medical debt immediately.
  • Universal dispute rights with fast timelines and automatic relief while disputes are open.
Bright line: No one loses a home or job because they got sick.
Transition design

How to clear existing medical debt without destabilizing providers

  • Discharge pathway: erase qualifying household medical debt by statute through a national program.
  • Provider stabilization: replace chaos billing with predictable payment streams under universal care.
  • Claims cleanup: set a defined lookback window and standardized verification to prevent fraud.

Debt relief is paired with universal payment reform so the same debt cannot reappear.

Future system

Healthcare free at point of service

  • Preventive care and chronic care access reduces total cost over time.
  • Pricing is standardized and transparent; surprise billing structures are removed.
  • Pharmaceutical pricing and procurement are reformed to reduce structural overpayment.
Economic impact

Why this grows GDP

  • More workforce participation and productivity.
  • Lower stress, fewer untreated conditions, fewer ER-only pathways.
  • Higher business formation as households aren’t chained to “job lock.”
