Time & Stress

This domain measures whether life is livable: predictable schedules, less crisis churn, less commute burden, and more room for family and community.

  • Cost burden proxy (current scaffold)
  • Transport burden proxy (current scaffold)
  • Future: volatility metrics, sleep/health proxies, overtime instability
In v32, this domain is anchored to Stress Reduction by Design.
