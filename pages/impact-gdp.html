How we will project GDP impacts

This page will quantify how the system changes GDP by reducing extraction and increasing productive capacity: health, education, stability, entrepreneurship, and infrastructure.

  • Stability effects: lower bankruptcies/evictions, lower churn, lower crime and health crises.
  • Investment effects: local investment via public banking and anti-monopoly reforms.
