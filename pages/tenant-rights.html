Universal Tenant & Landlord Rules

Home / The Model / Housing & Ownership / Tenant Rules
WHY

A home is not a loophole for exploitation

A working-class republic cannot allow basic shelter to be governed by predatory contracts. Universal rules create predictable rights and reduce fear-based living.

HOW

Universal tenant protections (baseline)

  • Standardized, plain-language leases.
  • Habitability standards with fast enforcement.
  • Fair eviction process with notice requirements and court speed limits.
  • Repair escrow mechanisms and dispute resolution.
  • Ban retaliation for reporting violations.
WHAT

Landlord responsibilities and transparency

  • Mandatory disclosure of ownership structures (no shell games).
  • Open books for audits when public funds or programs are involved.
  • Limits on junk fees and hidden charges.
  • Clear maintenance timelines and penalties for noncompliance.
