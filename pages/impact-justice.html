Impact Lab

Overview

Crime is reduced over time by focusing on clearance for violent crime, ending poverty penalties, and building rehabilitation + reentry systems that prevent repeat harm.

Template: current system → new system change → mechanism → risks/safeguards → metrics → projections (10/25 years).
Core stack

What changes

  • End poverty-as-crime traps (fees, cash bail spirals, technical violation churn).
  • Sentencing separates by risk and harm; treatment-first for nonviolent cases where appropriate.
  • Inside-out rehabilitation: literacy, credentials, paid work, and savings.
  • Reentry package: housing bridge, job placement, treatment, and stability supports.
