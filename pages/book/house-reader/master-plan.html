PRINCIPLES

Ethical tension design

The House is built to move emotion without coercion. No villains. No threats. No conversion. Only environments that mirror lived systems: how stability is offered, how friction is distributed, and how “normal” is manufactured.

  • Delayed recognition — discomfort arrives after understanding, not before.
  • Friction is information — ease is a design choice, not a moral one.
  • Agency is real — the reader can stop, skim, or stay.
  • No shame — complicity is treated as human, not sinful.
