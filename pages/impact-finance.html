Impact Lab

Overview

The finance transition reduces boom-bust volatility and increases productive investment. It strengthens small business, local ownership, and household stability—raising long-run GDP while reducing inequality and extraction.

Key outcome: more productive investment, fewer crises, broader prosperity.
Metrics & projections

What we measure

  • Credit access by region and income; small business lending rates and survival.
  • Housing credit quality: refinancing frequency, foreclosures, junk fee reduction.
  • Market concentration indices across key sectors; merger activity.
  • Volatility: crisis frequency, household bankruptcy rates, systemic risk indicators.
  • Wealth distribution and People’s Wealth Fund dividends over time.
  • GDP growth composition: productive investment vs financialization.
