WHY

Costs are real — so benefits must be real

Transportation investments only work politically if families feel benefits quickly. WMR uses phased delivery: quick wins first, then scaling, then long-term corridor builds.

Promise: show costs and assumptions openly, and track outcomes publicly.

WHAT

How we keep it pragmatic

  • Use existing rights-of-way and assets before building new.
  • Bus-first where it’s faster to deploy; rail where it wins long term.
  • Pair with broadband and telework policies to reduce demand pressures.
  • Publish a public dashboard: cost, timelines, ridership, reliability, safety.
