Impact Lab

Overview

Community-governed safety improves outcomes by increasing legitimacy, reducing unnecessary contacts and escalation, and shifting resources toward prevention and precision enforcement.

Key outcome: lower violence + fewer harmful encounters + higher trust.
Current system

What we see today

  • Low trust, uneven accountability, and inconsistent standards.
  • Over-policing of poverty and under-policing of exploitation and corruption.
  • Escalation cycles and high-cost incarceration pathways.
  • Federal-local tensions and perceived occupation in some contexts.
New system

What changes

  • Local governance boards and public safety charters with real authority.
  • Open data and independent investigations for serious incidents.
  • Specialized response teams for mental health, traffic, and domestic violence.
  • Prevention stack reduces demand for policing; precision enforcement targets violence and trafficking.
Metrics & projections

What we measure

  • Violent crime rates and clearance rates; shooting incidents and retaliation cycles.
  • Stops/searches and use-of-force rates; complaint volume and resolution timelines.
  • Trust metrics: community surveys, cooperation rates, witness participation.
  • Response times for emergencies vs non-emergencies; diversion to services.
  • Officer wellness, fatigue, and misconduct indicators.
