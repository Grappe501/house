Golden Circle

WHY: education is national infrastructure

In a working-class republic, education is not a luxury. It is the main engine of productivity, innovation, civic capacity, and social mobility.

Promise: education is always free — from early childhood through career transitions.
Golden Circle

HOW: universal access + lifelong retraining + civic literacy

  • Universal public option that is excellent enough to make the private market unnecessary for most families.
  • Debt reset: abolish student debt under the principle that society benefits collectively.
  • Lifelong learning: rapid reskilling tracks aligned to regional economies and emerging tech.
  • Civic education that produces competent voters and local leaders.
  • AI transition readiness: education becomes the shock absorber for automation.
