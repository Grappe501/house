Food Guarantee & Nutrition Floor

Home / The Model / Food & Agriculture / Food Guarantee
WHY

Hunger is a policy failure

In a high-output economy, hunger is not inevitable. It’s a design choice.

Rule: no child, no elder, and no working family goes hungry in the richest nation on earth.
HOW

Nutrition floor with dignity (scaffold)

  • Universal baseline access to nutritious staples.
  • Local distribution partners (schools, clinics, community hubs).
  • Smart anti-waste logistics (surplus routing, cold storage, rapid delivery).
  • Fraud controls via transparent auditing, not humiliation.
WHAT

What it changes

  • Lower chronic disease burden
  • Higher school performance and attendance
  • Lower healthcare and justice costs long-term
