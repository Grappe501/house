WHY

Utilities must be boring — not captured

When utilities become political machines, families pay forever. WMR makes utility governance transparent and rule-based.

HOW

Accountability mechanisms (scaffold)

  • Open books and public dashboards
  • Independent audits on schedule and on demand
  • Anti-gouging rules for emergencies
  • Anti-corruption enforcement and conflict-of-interest bans
  • Local escalation: more power at local levels with clear federal minimum standards
WHAT

What this prevents

  • Hidden rate increases
  • Insider contracting
  • Service discrimination (rural/poor neighborhoods)
  • Infrastructure neglect until crisis
