What changed

Trade must stop hollowing out communities

Trade policy becomes a working-class policy when it enforces fair labor standards and prevents race-to-the-bottom offshoring.

Deal requirements

Non-negotiables in trade agreements

  • Enforceable labor standards and inspection regimes.
  • Environmental baselines with penalties for violations.
  • Anti-tax-haven rules and beneficial ownership disclosure for cross-border flows.
  • Procurement and supply-chain reciprocity.
Local outcomes

What Americans feel

  • More domestic manufacturing and skilled trades demand.
  • Less wage suppression from offshoring threats.
  • More stable regional economies and small business ecosystems.
