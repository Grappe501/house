Impact Lab

Overview

Digital rights prevent modern exploitation: data extraction, surveillance, and algorithmic discrimination. Participation in society cannot require surrendering personal sovereignty over information.

Template: current system → new system change → mechanism → risks/safeguards → metrics → projections (10/25 years).
Non-negotiable

Freedom from data coercion

  • It is illegal to discriminate against anyone for withholding or negotiating data use.
  • No privacy penalty tiers; no retaliation for opting out of optional data collection.
  • Data use above strict necessity must be negotiated transparently—no hidden language.
Enforcement

Make violations existentially expensive

  • Strict liability for misuse.
  • Per-person statutory damages and revenue-scaled penalties.
  • Private right of action (no forced arbitration for data rights disputes).
