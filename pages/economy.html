Keep capitalism. Block extraction.

Capitalism with anti-capture guardrails

  • Bust monopolies and reduce finance governance concentration.
  • Limit extreme wealth capture so wealth cannot become private government.
  • Create a People’s Wealth Fund that pays a citizen dividend.
  • Expand worker ownership pathways and small business capital access.
Simple framing: Billionaires aren’t the enemy. Billionaire control is.
Philosophy

Capitalism without extraction

Markets can generate abundance. The failure is not “capitalism vs. socialism.” The failure is capture—when concentrated wealth uses monopoly power, political spending, and surveillance to turn markets into a one-way extraction machine.

This model keeps entrepreneurship and profit, but blocks the pathways that let profit become private government.

  • Profit stays when it comes from serving people.
  • Extraction ends when it comes from monopoly choke points, wage suppression, predatory finance, or political capture.
Mechanics

How shared growth works

  • Competition restoration: enforce antitrust, restrict mergers in concentrated sectors, and prevent “killer acquisitions.”
  • People’s Wealth Fund: a national endowment that captures a small slice of returns from public goods and national prosperity, paying a citizen dividend.
  • Local prosperity engine: small business credit access, local procurement standards, and infrastructure investment that raises productivity.
  • Worker power: right to organize and bargain, portable benefits, and pathways for worker ownership—so wages rise with productivity.
Key idea: we grow GDP by raising national productivity, then share prosperity through a dividend and stronger wage power—not by nationalizing industries.
Safeguards

Avoiding common failure modes

  • Inflation control: phase major relief (debt reset) and pair it with supply expansion (housing, energy, logistics).
  • Capital flight control: stable rule-of-law plus new safe investment instruments makes the system investable.
  • Capture defense: anti-corruption enforcement and data rights prevent the next oligarchy from forming.
