Anti-Hoarding & Anti-Monopoly Property Rules

Home / The Model / Housing & Ownership / Anti-Hoarding
WHY

When housing becomes a stock portfolio, communities collapse

Large-scale property accumulation turns local housing into a rent-extraction machine. We de-incentivize hoarding so ownership stays local and competition stays real.

Principle: you can invest, but you cannot capture whole communities.
HOW

Anti-hoarding rules (scaffold)

  • Progressive tax/fees on large-scale residential property holdings.
  • Limits on bulk acquisitions in distressed markets.
  • Right-of-first-refusal for tenants and local buyers in bulk sales.
  • Transparency of corporate ownership chains.
WHAT

Local ownership incentives

  • First-home priority programs.
  • Local small landlord standards with fair return but strong protections.
  • Community land trusts to keep land price stable across generations.
