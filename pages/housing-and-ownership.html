Golden Circle

WHY: housing stability creates freedom

Housing is the biggest monthly pressure on working families. When housing becomes a speculative asset, families lose the ability to plan, save, and build local wealth.

Goal: raise the floor, expand home ownership, and end rent-extraction as the dominant path to wealth.
Golden Circle

HOW: anti-hoarding + universal rules + ownership pathways

  • Universal landlord/tenant rules to standardize rights and prevent exploitation.
  • Anti-hoarding/anti-monopoly limits on large-scale property accumulation.
  • Local ownership pathways that move renters to owners (down payment support, shared equity, community land trusts).
  • Mortgage transition reform to reduce bank capture without collapsing finance.
  • Rural-urban balance through transport + land access + local development incentives.
