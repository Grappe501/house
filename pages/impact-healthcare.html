Impact Lab

Overview

Universal healthcare reduces household stress, improves labor mobility, and lowers long-run costs by shifting care upstream. Governance quality is measured by access, outcomes, and transparency—not denials.

Key outcome: healthier people + less financial collapse + higher productivity.
Metrics & projections

What we measure

  • Wait times by specialty; rural access coverage.
  • Preventive care utilization and avoidable ER visits.
  • Chronic disease outcomes over time (diabetes control, hypertension).
  • Provider burnout and staffing stability metrics.
  • Drug/device price indices vs international benchmarks.
  • Household out-of-pocket spending and medical bankruptcy rates.
