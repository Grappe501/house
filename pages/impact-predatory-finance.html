Impact Lab

Overview

Ending predatory finance raises the economic floor without inflation by stopping extraction, not by injecting uncontrolled cash.

Current system

What we see today

  • Billions extracted annually via fees and interest
  • Households cycling through debt traps
  • Credit damage that blocks mobility
New system

What changes

  • APR and fee caps eliminate extraction
  • Public and community alternatives expand
  • Credit becomes a bridge, not a trap
Metrics

What we track

  • Fee revenue reduction
  • Household liquidity stability
  • Use of emergency credit alternatives
  • Bankruptcy and eviction correlations
10- and 25-year projections added later.
