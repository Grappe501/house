Overview

Public safety is community-governed and outcome-driven. The model separates armed policing from non-criminal crisis response and keeps federal ground presence constrained and locally accountable.

Template: current system → new system change → mechanism → risks/safeguards → metrics → projections (10/25 years).
Community control

Taking policing back to community

  • Community Safety Boards with real authority over policy, transparency, and accountability triggers.
  • Public safety as a system: behavioral health response, mediation/violence interruption, targeted policing for violent crime.
Federal limits

Federal presence constraints

  • Narrow federal mission scope; no domestic occupation model.
  • Local civilian leadership retains primary control during emergencies; federal support is time-limited and transparent.
