Overview

Immigration policy is designed to protect citizen wages, end underground exploitation, and create a clear, enforceable path into legal work and eventual citizenship—without dehumanization.

Template: current system → new system change → mechanism → risks/safeguards → metrics → projections (10/25 years).
Rights and limits

Undocumented status in this model

  • Healthcare at point of service remains available (public health and human dignity).
  • No citizen dividend/profit sharing without citizenship (ownership stake is civic membership).
  • No voting without citizenship.
Employer accountability

End the shadow labor market

  • Universal work authorization verification with anti-discrimination safeguards.
  • Auditable payroll standards; randomized wage audits in high-risk industries.
  • Living wage enforcement: underpayment triggers back pay + penalty into the Workers’ Fund.
  • Worker-protection firewall so reporting wage theft is safe and effective.
