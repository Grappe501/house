What this is

National debt paydown model (line-item driven)

This page answers: how do we fund the system, reach surplus, and pay down debt?

Debt falls when the primary balance improves and/or GDP grows faster than interest. In V46, the primary balance improvement comes from explicit Budget Line Items.

How to read this

What’s “positive” and what happens next

  • Primary surplus means revenues exceed non-interest spending.
  • When a primary surplus appears, WMR allocates it by rule: Surplus Allocation Rules.
  • Debt paydown becomes credible because the rule is public and mechanical.

V47 will add a “surplus allocation projection” table using the same line items.

Honesty

What would make this fail?

  • Recession shock during transition without stabilizers
  • Political sabotage (underfunding, partial implementation)
  • Badly designed taxes that push burden back onto working families
  • Failure to enforce anti-monopoly and anti-extraction rules
