Baseline, Participation & the Social Floor

WHY

A baseline prevents collapse

When automation accelerates, a society needs a baseline that prevents mass instability. WMR uses a social floor that is not “socialism” — it is stability infrastructure.

Baseline

What everyone gets by default

  • Healthcare (free at point of use)
  • Education and retraining access (free)
  • Emergency housing protections and food access (floor)
  • Basic legal protections, data rights, and due process
Participation

What changes with participation

  • Wealth fund dividend tiers can be tied to contribution (work, caregiving, service, training).
  • People can opt out of certain contribution paths — but cannot exploit the system.
  • Non-participation triggers structured engagement offers (training, service, community work).
Design goal: dignity without incentivizing permanent disengagement.
What about people who don’t participate?

Rules (scaffold)

  • No one is denied healthcare or basic food access.
  • Cash-like dividend components can be reduced if a person refuses all participation paths without disability/valid exemption.
  • Participation options include work, caregiving, education, service, and verified community contribution.

We will build this into the People’s Wealth Fund logic later.
