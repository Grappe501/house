Impact Lab

Overview

Food security improves health, learning, and productivity while stabilizing local economies. By rebuilding local logistics and enforcing fair markets, we reduce price volatility and corporate extraction.

Key outcome: lower hunger, lower healthcare costs, higher stability.
Current system

What we see today

  • Food deserts and rural access gaps.
  • Price volatility and corporate consolidation pressures.
  • Diet-driven chronic disease and stress.
  • Farmers squeezed by buyer power and input monopolies.
New system

What changes

  • Food Security Guarantee with access standards and automatic stabilizers.
  • Local food hubs and institutional procurement create stable demand for producers.
  • Mobile markets and hub distribution close rural gaps.
  • Enforcement against gouging/collusion and abusive contracting.
Metrics & projections

What we measure

  • Food insecurity and child hunger rates.
  • Price indices for staple foods vs wages and inflation.
  • Health outcomes tied to diet (diabetes, hypertension, obesity trends).
  • School attendance and performance linked to meal stability.
  • Local farm viability: number of farms, margins, processing access.
