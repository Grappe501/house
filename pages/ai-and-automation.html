Golden Circle

WHY: AI is either liberation or capture

AI will compress costs, automate labor, and concentrate power unless we design the rules. In the Working Majority Republic, AI is treated as a public productivity engine that must benefit the whole.

Core goal: AI makes working-family life easier — not disposable.
Golden Circle

HOW: share productivity, control monopolies, keep humans sovereign

  • Productivity dividend: a portion of AI-driven gains flows into the People’s Wealth Fund and pays citizens.
  • Anti-monopoly AI policy: break compute/data monopolies; prevent “five corporations own everything.”
  • Data rights: people own their personal data; consent required; discrimination for withholding is illegal.
  • Work transition infrastructure: free retraining, paid transitions, apprenticeships, and local job pipelines.
  • Safety & alignment governance: mandatory auditing, incident reporting, and “red lines” for surveillance.

Connection to QoLI

How this changes the Quality of Life Index

AI affects nearly every domain. In the model, AI is treated as a multiplier that can either raise QoLI fast (shared gains) or crash it (job loss + capture).

  • Income & Work Security (job disruption vs dividend)
  • Time & Stress (shorter workweeks vs instability)
  • Democratic Agency (capture vs transparency)
  • Affordability (cost compression vs monopolistic pricing)
