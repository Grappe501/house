Diplomacy first

Conflict prevention is cheaper than war

Diplomacy is treated as critical infrastructure: well-staffed, well-funded, and accountable. The goal is to prevent crises and stabilize regions before conflicts erupt.

Tools

How we do it

  • Regional diplomacy teams with cultural and language expertise.
  • Early warning systems and conflict mediation capacity.
  • Targeted sanctions focused on elites (anti-kleptocracy), not ordinary people.
  • Partnership investments that build mutual capacity, not dependency.
