Watershed governance

Manage water by how it flows

Watersheds are the true geography of water. Governance and enforcement should follow the water—not jurisdictional convenience.

Core rules

What must be true everywhere

  • Open reporting of discharges and test results.
  • Independent sampling authority (citizen + scientific oversight).
  • Clear liability rules and long-term monitoring mandates.
  • Public right to sue when agencies fail to act (structured, anti-abuse).
Protecting rural communities

No sacrifice zones

  • High-risk operations must post bonds and carry cleanup insurance.
  • Local communities have veto triggers for projects that threaten drinking water sources.
  • Economic development must not depend on contamination.
