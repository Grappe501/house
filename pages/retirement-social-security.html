Retirement, Social Security & the People’s Wealth Fund

Home / The Model / Retirement
Principle

Retirement is a right earned through a lifetime of contribution

In the current system, retirement has been privatized into anxiety: market swings, predatory fees, and workers forced to “time” their lives around volatility. A working-majority republic guarantees dignified retirement by combining a public baseline with a national wealth dividend that grows with the country.

Goal: stable retirement for everyone without turning old age into poverty risk.
What happens to Social Security

Protect it, strengthen it, modernize it

  • No benefit cuts: current and near retirees are protected as a first principle.
  • Stability upgrade: strengthen solvency through anti-extraction revenue streams (wealth dividend rails, anti-monopoly enforcement, and reduced crisis costs).
  • Simplified benefits: clearer tiers, less bureaucracy, faster disability adjudication.
  • Care integration: healthcare is free at point of service, reducing Medicare cost pressure and retiree risk.

Social Security remains the guaranteed floor. We add a second pillar that grows with national prosperity.

People’s Wealth Fund — retirement tier

A portion held for the later years

The People’s Wealth Fund distributes an annual dividend to citizens. A portion of each citizen’s dividend is automatically allocated to a protected retirement account—similar to a pension reserve.

  • Automatic allocation: a defined % is reserved each year into a retirement tier.
  • Portability: follows the person across jobs and states.
  • Low-fee index management: governed publicly with strict anti-corruption rules.
  • Early access limits: protected from emergency leakage except for defined catastrophes.
Design: everyone becomes a partial beneficiary of national productivity growth—not just investors.
Does retirement go away?

No—retirement becomes predictable

  • Retirement remains a stage of life supported by a stable floor.
  • Flexible work is optional: many people will still choose meaningful part-time service or mentoring roles.
  • Purpose pathways: local communities can create voluntary “elder contribution” roles (schools, civic boards, community hubs).

We remove the fear that forces elders into work out of desperation.

How we raise the floor for the poor

End poverty traps

  • Automatic supports: benefits scale smoothly—no cliffs that punish small income gains.
  • Housing + healthcare + food floors reduce crisis costs and stabilize families.
  • Work incentives without coercion: training, childcare, and transportation access expand real opportunity.
  • Disability dignity: faster, humane disability pathways without endless denials.
Bright line: we do not punish people for being poor—we remove the conditions that create poverty.
Protecting against market volatility

Retirement without casino risk

  • Social Security provides baseline certainty.
  • Wealth Fund retirement tier uses long-run index strategies with anti-speculation guardrails.
  • Public banking and stability policies reduce boom-bust pressure on households.
Implementation

Transition without collapse

  1. Lock Social Security: no cuts; strengthen administration and disability processes.
  2. Stand up Wealth Fund tiers: annual dividend + retirement reserve allocation.
  3. Protect existing pensions/401(k)s: reduce predatory fees; provide optional migration paths to low-fee public rails.
  4. Publish dashboards: poverty rates among elders, retirement readiness, disability timelines.

This is a “both/and” transition that stabilizes today while building the future pillar.
