Impact Lab

Overview

Automation can increase GDP while reducing working-class stress if productivity gains are shared and transitions are supported. The key is preventing a “winner-take-all” capture of automation benefits.

Current system

What we see today

  • Productivity gains often flow to owners; wages stagnate.
  • Displacement creates regional collapse and long-term job scarring.
  • Worker surveillance and algorithmic management expand without due process.
New system

What changes

  • Dividend rises with productivity through the People’s Wealth Fund.
  • Transition compacts: firms benefiting from automation fund retraining and wage insurance.
  • AI workplace due process and surveillance limits protect dignity.
Metrics & projections

What we measure

  • Productivity vs wage growth; dividend levels over time.
  • Transition success rates: time-to-new-job, wage recovery, credential completion.
  • Regional stability: business formation, unemployment duration, household debt service.
  • Algorithmic workplace complaints and resolution outcomes.
