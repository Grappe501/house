Intergenerational contract

Retirement stays — the guarantee strengthens

  • Retirement does not go away. Social Security remains the guaranteed floor.
  • Funding is strengthened (e.g., address payroll cap / high-end contribution mechanisms).
  • Caregiving and socially necessary labor receive credit.
  • The People’s Wealth Fund includes a protected retirement reserve that supplements—not replaces—Social Security.
