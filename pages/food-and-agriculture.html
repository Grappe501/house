Golden Circle

WHY: food is a right and a local economic engine

Food insecurity is not just hunger — it is health collapse, school failure, stress, and community instability. A working-class republic guarantees food availability and builds resilient local supply.

Goal: guarantee nutritious food access for everyone while strengthening local farmers and small producers.
Golden Circle

HOW: local production + fair markets + universal access

  • Local supply resilience: regional production targets, processing capacity, and distribution.
  • Anti-monopoly agriculture: break concentrated corporate control over inputs, processing, and pricing.
  • Universal nutrition floor: no one goes hungry; healthy food is the baseline.
  • Rural prosperity: farmers and processors get fair margins; communities keep value locally.
  • Transparency: price chains are visible from farm gate to shelf.
