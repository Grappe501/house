Principle

Automation should shorten the workweek, not widen inequality

When machines do more, people should gain time, security, and opportunity. Automation becomes a shared dividend instead of a mass displacement event.

Tools

How we share productivity gains

  • Automation dividend into the People’s Wealth Fund.
  • Wage insurance and rapid retraining stipends.
  • Local job placement pipelines tied to infrastructure and care sectors.
  • Worker impact assessments for major automation deployments.
Small business protection

Don’t let AI become a monopoly moat

  • Public AI tools and training for small businesses.
  • Open standards and portability to prevent lock-in.
  • Anti-monopoly enforcement for platform AI bundling.
