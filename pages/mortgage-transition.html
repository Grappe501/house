Mortgage Reform & Transition Plan

Home / The Model / Housing & Ownership / Mortgage Reform
WHY

We reduce bank capture without crashing the system

Mortgages tie families to the finance class. Reform must be pragmatic: we reduce household risk and predatory leverage while preserving stability.

Design goal: lower payments + less volatility + more principal going to households, not fees.
HOW

Transition strategy (scaffold)

  • Create a Public Refinance Window for qualified owner-occupants at transparent rates.
  • Allow conversion of eligible loans into stable, plain-vanilla terms (no traps).
  • Limit servicing abuse and enforce standard disclosure.
  • Gradually shift mortgage finance toward community banks/credit unions and public options.

We will add a detailed plan for handling MBS markets and investor transition without systemic shock.

WHAT

Debt relief without collapse (principles)

  • Target owner-occupants first (not speculators).
  • Use discount purchase mechanisms where possible.
  • Phase changes over time with transparent rules.
  • Pair with supply-side housing build to prevent price spikes.
