Maintenance Playbooks (v1)

When metrics flash red, we don’t improvise. We follow a known sequence.

Playbook 01 — Drift Alarm

  1. Confirm signal (data quality check)
  2. Declare threshold breach publicly
  3. Trigger review window
  4. Propose correction options
  5. Implement correction + log patch notes

Playbook 02 — Capture Attempt

  1. Identify vector (money, appointment, loophole)
  2. Pause affected process (if allowed)
  3. Escalate to independent audit
  4. Publish findings
  5. Close loophole via patch
