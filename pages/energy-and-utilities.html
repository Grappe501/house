Golden Circle

WHY: utilities are the hidden tax on working families

Energy, water, and broadband determine what life costs and what opportunities exist. When these systems are captured, people pay forever. Under WMR, utilities are treated as public infrastructure: markets can exist, but extraction cannot.

Goal: universal access + stable prices + resilient systems + local prosperity.
Golden Circle

HOW: universal service + transparent pricing + anti-monopoly + resilience

  • Universal broadband: equal high-speed access everywhere (rural parity as a national productivity strategy).
  • Water security: safe drinking water and modern infrastructure as a public health guarantee.
  • Energy transition: reliability first, then decarbonize with cost transparency and local benefit sharing.
  • Utility accountability: open books, rate transparency, and public dashboards.
  • Local ownership options: co-ops/municipals where they outperform.

Connection to QoLI

Where utilities map into QoLI

  • Affordability: energy + water + internet are unavoidable fixed costs.
  • Health: water quality and indoor energy stability reduce illness and stress.
  • Education: broadband is now a prerequisite for opportunity.
  • Income & Work: connectivity and reliable power enable higher productivity and new jobs.
  • Time & Stress: outages and utility debt create constant crisis churn.
