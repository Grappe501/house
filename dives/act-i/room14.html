Module context

Rooms 11–15 — Reframe → Scale Shift → Validation → Non‑Blame → Breath

This module is the first real *repair arc* in Act I. After the system is revealed (06–10), the narrator is given language that reduces shame. Stress is reframed as signal, then shown as structural; anger is validated without creating villains; blame is withheld; the reader is given breath and perspective before the next compression cycle.

Psychology target

Prevent scapegoating. Keep the reader from turning clarity into hatred; preserve safety and complexity.

Emotional move

Safety → trust → grounded focus.

Symbols introduced / evolved
  • Wide room / open geometry: space to think without panic
  • Two chairs facing same direction: alignment without coercion
  • A blank plaque: refusal to assign a single name
  • A ledger with many hands: systems are collective, not singular
  • A window with no bars: freedom begins as perception
Prose beats
  • Narrator is tempted to pick a villain; the room refuses easy targets.
  • Multiple causes appear: habits, incentives, fear, tradition, convenience.
  • The House’s politeness is shown as a social technology, not a person.
  • Narrator feels safer: complexity reduces paranoia.
  • Exit line introduces the need for breath: clarity requires oxygen.
Handoff

The next room is a breathing space—an earned pause before the next squeeze.

Author constraints
  • Keep the voice calm: “older narrator with restraint.”
  • Do not sermonize. Offer naming, not instruction.
  • Let the House appear briefly as language: plaques, captions, labels.
  • End each room on a line that feels like a handoff, not a wrap.
