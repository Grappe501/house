Module context

Rooms 11–15 — Reframe → Scale Shift → Validation → Non‑Blame → Breath

This module is the first real *repair arc* in Act I. After the system is revealed (06–10), the narrator is given language that reduces shame. Stress is reframed as signal, then shown as structural; anger is validated without creating villains; blame is withheld; the reader is given breath and perspective before the next compression cycle.

Psychology target

Normalize anger as a sign of violated boundaries, not a character flaw. Permit moral energy.

Emotional move

Validation → release → steadiness.

Symbols introduced / evolved
  • Crack as honesty: damage that tells the truth
  • A small hammer left untouched: violence is not required for truth
  • A label: 'This is not you': separating identity from condition
  • Red thread visible for first time: the through-line is real
  • A sealed jar of 'good manners': politeness as containment
Prose beats
  • Narrator admits anger in plain language—no apology.
  • The House tries to rename it as 'unreasonable'; narrator refuses the label.
  • Anger is framed as integrity: a boundary alarm.
  • A small, clean release—breath, unclenched jaw, steadier stance.
  • Exit into a more open space where blame is withheld to keep clarity.
Handoff

With anger permitted, the narrator can stop hunting villains and start reading patterns.

Author constraints
  • Keep the voice calm: “older narrator with restraint.”
  • Do not sermonize. Offer naming, not instruction.
  • Let the House appear briefly as language: plaques, captions, labels.
  • End each room on a line that feels like a handoff, not a wrap.
