A people-first constitutional model: democracy without money-rule, capitalism without extraction.
Purpose

Working Majority Republic is a draft constitutional and policy model designed to protect the working majority, keep markets and entrepreneurship alive, and remove the structural pathways that let money dominate democracy.

Working premise: democracy cannot survive when most people live in precarity. We rebuild institutions so ordinary people cannot be economically exploited or politically outspent.
